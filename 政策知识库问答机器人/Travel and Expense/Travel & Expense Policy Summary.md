PERSONAL CAR / MILEAGE ALLOWANCE
The mileage rate employees can claim for using personal vehicles is published locally. Car mileage is claimed for
travelling on business and can be claimed from your place of work or home complying to local tax requirements.
Car mileage is calculated by deducting your regular commute from the mileage claim.
CORPORATE CARD
• Company corporate cards are not for personal use. Corporate cards are only permitted for approved business
travel expenses. For Concur users, any such transactions will feed into the Concur system and employees will be
required to mark them as personal and submit on an expense report to clear. Reminders will be sent and escalated
to line management for any transactions that go unsubmitted.
Send any expense related questions to your local finance department.

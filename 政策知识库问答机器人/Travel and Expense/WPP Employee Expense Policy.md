Our **Employee Expense company policy** outlines how we will reimburse employees for work-related expenses which are only those expenses which are **wholly and necessarily** incurred in the performance of an employee’s duties for their employing WPP Group company. 

# Scope

This policy applies to all WPP Group employees that need to spend money for workrelated activities.   

# Travel Expenses

The Global Travel & Expense policy covers business travel as well as Meetings, Conferences and Events. This policy can be found and WPP Travel pages can be accessed  * *

**What are work-related expenses? **

These are expenses that employees may occur in a business setting such as dinners with clients or travel by taxi or tube to and from client meetings or events. Taxis to and from the office from your home, other than for late working after 8pm will not be reimbursed. 

Work-related expense are capped in many cases, and your line manager should approve them. If you spend more than the approved amount or the expenses are excessive or not wholly and necessarily incurred in the performance of your duties, you must cover the extra cost yourself. Some clients cannot accept any form of entertainment and are subject to their own polices and you should make sure you are aware of those restrictions or limits. 

In many countries there are tax thresholds on the amount of any work-related expenses that may be claimed and a restrictive approach to the term wholly and necessarily incurred in the performance of an employee’s duties. Expenses that tax authorities do not consider to be work-related may be a taxable benefit if reimbursed by the employer. 

# Employee Entertaining and Team Events

Wherever there are onsite restaurant or bar facilities, these events should be held at a WPP office / campus, and employees must get approval from the relevant Department Head and budget holder before incurring any costs.  

If the entertainment includes a meal, then the allowable meal spend per employee must not exceed the maximum permissible Daily Meal Cap. The total cost of the meal and/or event must be paid for and claimed by the most senior WPP employee in attendance. 

# Non-allowable expenses

WPP Group companies will not reimburse and employees should not claim for the following expenses: 

joining fees or annual membership fees for any social or sporting clubs or private members clubs for example Soho House; 

professional association membership fees – unless specifically agreed in your employment contract or pre-approved by the Department Head; 

gifts for other employees or third parties unless in special circumstances and subject to Department Head approval; 

travel booked outside of the Global Travel & Expense policy; 

subscriptions to newspapers, magazines, linkedin or other on-line memberships; 

training outside of those organised by the WPP Group unless pre-approved by the Department Head; 

donations or sponsorships to charitable and/or not for profit organisations; 

personal trips such as taxis to and from an employees office or home or schools unless related to late working after 8pm and approved by your Manager; 

personal lunches and/or other subsistence such as coffee or other beverage – unless during travelling on company business as per the caps in the Global Travel & Expense policy; 

IT or mobile phones, airpods, cases, chargers, adaptors, travel screens or any other mobile phone equipment purchased in a personal capacity unless specifically agreed in your employment contract or pre-approved by the Department Head; 

Personal mobile phone charges or membership fees for personal credit cards – employees should only use corporate issued phones or credit cards; 

Private health prescriptions; 

Stationery, printing materials, office furniture – unless specifically agreed in your employment contract or where it is a statutory allowance. 

This list is not exhaustive. Employees should use their judgement in assessing whether an expense is claimable and wholly necessary in the performance of their duties. Please ask your Department Head about reimbursable expenses before you submit a claim. 

If you have been issued with a corporate credit card, you should only use it for business expenses which should be reclaimed as below; it must not be used for personal expenses.  

Managers should not incur expenses on other employee’s credit cards such as their PA or members of their team that report to them. Expenses should also not be split across multiple credit cards or claim submissions to reduce visibility of the amount of the expenses being incurred and reimbursed. 

# Reimbursement Procedure

When an employee has incurred work-related expenses, they should: 

Submit a reimbursement claim in relation to their own expenses and not on behalf of someone else. Receipts and bills for business dinners and transportation should be submitted within 45 days; 

The expense must be fully justified and detailed in the claim, including the purpose of any meal and/or entertainment for example and the names of all WPP employees or clients who attended. 

Please note that submitting a reimbursement claim for gifts, entertainment or hospitality does not amount to a disclosure under WPP’s Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality policy. Any such disclosure required should be made separately following the process set out in WPP’s Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality policy which all WPP agencies and employees are required to follow.  

We want to avoid overspending or unnecessary costs. In cases of consistent falsified or exaggerated claims, the employing company may take disciplinary action.

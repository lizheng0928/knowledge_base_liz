**WPP Media**** China Offline Stamping SOP**

**群邑中国线下文档盖章流程**

**1. ****Purpose**** ****目的：**

This document is prepared to control offline stamping of WPP Media China, in order to facilitate offline stamping according to the policy of WPP Media China.

本文档用于规范公司线下盖章流程，以确保其符合公司政策并具有实际可操作性。


**2. ****Scope**** ****范围：**

The system and procedure apply to all departments and employees within the management scope of WPP Media China.

本制度及程序适用于所有群邑中国管理范围内的部门及其员工。


The system and procedure are applicable to the following seals:

本制度及程序对应以下印章的使用：

Company seals: 6 in Shanghai, 3 in Beijing and 2 in Guangzhou, which are kept by the FOC and used as needed.

公司公章：上海6枚，北京3枚，广州2枚，存放于FOC，用于各种需要加盖公章的情形

Contract seals: 7 in Shanghai, 3 in Beijing and 3 in Guangzhou, which are kept by the FOC and used for contracts, agreements, orders, spot plans, etc. Another 8 contract seals are kept by the Commercial Team in Beijing and used for procurement of traditional media.

合约专用章：上海7枚，北京3枚，广州3枚，存放于FOC，用于合同、协议、订单、排期等。另有8枚存放于北京商务，适用于传统媒介采购合同用章。

Financial seals: 8 (kept by SSC or FOC) in Shanghai, 3 in Beijing and 4 in Guangzhou, which are used for financial work-related circumstances.

财务专用章：上海8枚，存放于SSC或FOC保管，北京3枚，广州4枚，用于财务工作相关的情形

Invoice seals: 10 in total, which are kept by SSC and used for paper invoices.

发票专用章：共10枚，存放于SSC，用于加盖纸质发票


All seals are kept by the Finance Director or those at higher levels, and locked in file cabinets when not in use. They shall be kept temporarily by a designated person if the seal keeper is out or on vacation.

印章均由财务总监或以上级别人员保管，不用时锁于档案柜中。保管人外出或者休假时，指定专人临时保管。


**3. ****Principle**** ****原则：**

Company seals must be used with an approval when necessary.

公司印章仅限于必要且获得适当审批后使用。

Special seals instead of general company seals shall be used where appropriate.

能使用专用章的情形，需使用专用章，而非通用公章。

**4. ****Process narrative**** ****流程描述：**

**Financial stamping process:**

**财务盖章流程：**

An applicant shall prepare the final document and fill out a general stamping request form (or a special stamping request form for contracts signed with customers and media/non-media suppliers), which shall be stamped with appropriate seals. Approvals that are emailed or signed in accordance with the approval matrix in Appendix 2 shall be printed and submitted together with the request form and document (with a signature in the left or right corner or an endorsement) to the FOC, followed by stamping request registration. Relevant information such as the applicant’s contact information and the purpose and recipient of the document shall be filled legibly and correctly in the registration form. For documents involving confidential personal information, the applicant may submit a request form only, in which the stamping statement shall be checked. After registration, the document shall be stamped by the FOC team.

盖章申请人准备好需盖章的文档，确保文档是最终版，填写附件一通用盖章申请表(如果客户合同申请表和媒体、非媒体供应商合同申请表使用对应专用申请表)，选择适用印章，按照附件二审批权限表获得邮件或者签字的审批，将审批全部打印，连同申请表和脚签或背签后的盖章文档，一起递送到FOC，按指引进行盖章申请登记，登记时清晰填写本人联系方式、文档用途和盖章文档的接收方等信息。填写登记表务必字迹清晰端正。如涉及人事机密文档，可仅递交盖章申请表，递交人在申请表上勾选盖章申明一行。登记完毕交由FOC团队安排签章。


The approval matrix in Appendix 2 lists the approvals required for documents to be stamped. The Commercial Team shall review whether stamping requests are rational, approved by team leader and the appropriateness of using the seal. In addition to the provisions stipulated in relevant management procedures, FOC shall also check stamping requests for signatures by competent personnel of the Commercial Team and compliance with the requirements in Appendix 2 and 3.

附件二审批权限表列明各项盖章文档需要的审批，商务需审阅盖章申请是否合理、是否已获得正确的团队领导的审批，以及选择的印章是否合适。除了有相关管理规程规定的需要遵守规程的规定之外，FOC的责任在于检查对应商务的签字是否正确，是否对应附件二和三的列示。


Upon receiving a stamping request, the FOC team shall check the names of the document and related party (e.g. supplier, customer or employee) in the stamping request form and registration form. In case of no discrepancy, the FOC team shall accept the request form and document, which shall be signed by FOC and returned by FOC as stipulated in the table below. In the event of any discrepancy or incomplete approval, stamping requests shall be rejected.

FOC团队接到需盖章申请时，核对盖章申请表与登记表内容中文档名称、对方名称(如供应商名称、客户名称、员工名称)，核对无异后收下申请表和盖章文档，安排FOC签字，并在下表对应时间来FOC领取。核对有异或者审批不全的盖章申请不会被接受。

**Seal borrowing process**

**印章外借流程**

Only the Business Director and those at higher levels are allowed to request for seal borrowing. In this case, an applicant shall submit the stamping request form and registration form according to the aforesaid process, together with a receipt. The receipt shall be filled out as stipulated in Appendix 5, signed in handwriting form by the applicant and team leader, and submitted two working days in advance to the Head of FOC for approval. FOC shall lend the appropriate seal after verifying the stamping request form and receipt. Once the seal is returned to FOC, FOC shall fill in the return date (with signature) in the receipt.

仅Business Director及以上职级可以申请印章外借，申请人需按上述流程提交盖章申请表和登记，并提交借条。借条需要按附件五填写完整，并获得申请人和团队领导手书签字，并提前2个工作日递交FOC负责人审批。FOC核对盖章申请表和借条后交付印章。印章用完归还时，FOC收回印章，在借条上注明回收日期并签名。

This SOP has been through discussion among commercial, HR, Finance Operation and Controlling and legal departments.

本流程已经过商务部门、人事部门、群邑财务运营和控制部门以及法务部门的广泛讨论。

公司在群邑中国内部网页上对本制度及流程进行更新。每个员工有责任经常访问网站从而了解并遵守最新的制度及程序。

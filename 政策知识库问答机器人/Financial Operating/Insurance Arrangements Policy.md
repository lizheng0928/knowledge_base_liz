The following global insurance policies must be complied with: 

professional indemnity (also known as advertisers’ errors and omissions) for all agencies. A per claim retention applies in this programme. 

directors’ and officers’ liability insurance for any director or officer of any direct or indirect (more than 50%) owned agency, 

fidelity/crime insurance for all agencies of WPP of which the first US$2 million is borne by the company, 

umbrella liability insurance provides additional limits against certain exposures faced by WPP. This programme is in addition to existing automobile and general (public) liability programmes, 

fiduciary liability, 

international terrorism; for full list of global insurance policies please refer to the Global Insurance Manual or contact the WPP Risk Manager. 


If further information is required on the extent of insurance cover that these worldwide policies provide, this should be obtained from the WPP Risk Manager or 



the local Marsh broker in your area. You can access the Marsh contact list  under ‘Marsh Local Contacts’ link. 

WPP is under an obligation to report all losses and potential claims arising under any of these insurances. All losses and potential claims are to be reported as soon as they are known to the WPP Risk Manager. Additionally, UK companies are to inform Marsh, the local insurance brokers. 

WPP and its professional insurance advisers will decide whether the loss can be pursued under the terms of the insurance policies.

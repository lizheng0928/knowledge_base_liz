# Introduction


In this policy, “M&A Activity” includes: 


the purchase or sale of any shares in, or business assets of, any company or other business unit merging with any other business or company; 

“acqui-hire” arrangements (i.e. the hiring of employees with related client portfolios) and similar arrangements;  

investments in, and subscriptions for, shares in any other business (and entry into any option arrangements), including the issue of shares in relation to services performed; and 

any agreements, conditional or otherwise, to do any of the above. 


All M&A Activity, regardless of size of the target or the proposed consideration, is managed and co-ordinated by the WPP Corporate Development team.  


Agencies should not submit any offer or proposal, binding or otherwise, or enter into non-disclosure or confidentiality agreements, in relation to M&A Activity, without the approval of the WPP Director of Corporate Development.  


# Process


Subject to the above, any correspondence with third parties relating to potential M&A Activity should be marked as ‘subject to contract’ and should refer to the fact that any proposal is subject to WPP approval. 


No brokers, advisers or other third parties may be appointed in relation to M&A Activity, without the approval of the WPP Director of Corporate Development. No external legal firms or lawyers may be instructed other than by the GC Corporate or Group Chief Counsel. 




Any agency proposing M&A Activity should submit a written proposal to the WPP Director of Corporate Development. Proposals should, as a minimum, contain the following details: strategic rationale; target background and financial history where known; background information on management; client analysis; and financial structure of the proposed transaction. 


WPP Corporate Development will conduct or commission due diligence (including financial, legal, tax, IT), led by WPP Transaction Services. 


All documentation in relation to M&A Activity including offer letters and heads of terms, and all contractual documentation, will be prepared by WPP Corporate Development and WPP Legal. 


WPP Corporate Development will lead negotiation of any M&A Activity. 


In relation to acquisitions, the finance director of the relevant agency will ensure that a plan (an Integration Plan) is put in place to integrate the acquired company within WPP’s financial reporting, tax and treasury structures. This Integration Plan must be agreed by the WPP Transaction Services Director and, following completion of the acquisition, WPP Transaction Services will review the achievement of the Integration Plan. A template Integration Plan can be provided by WPP Corporate Development. 


No Communications in relation to M&A Activity, including internal or external announcements, press releases or other public statements may be made without the approval of the WPP Communications Director. 


# Disposals


No employee of the Group is authorised to discuss the disposal of any business or company or part of a business or any assets belonging to the Group including trading names or domain names without prior written approval from the WPP Director of Corporate Development.  


# Affiliations


Agency brand names should not be licensed to third parties or included in corporate names of any entity which is not controlled by WPP. “Affiliation” agreements, where a third-party trades as “in association with” an agency are rarely now appropriate because WPP does not control “affiliates”, and Affiliation Agreements carry a high degree of risk and may only be entered into with the approval of WPP Legal and after appropriate due diligence, including reputational due diligence. All Affiliation Agreements will be based on WPP’s template and will be subject to periodic review.

Engaging Affiliates
1. Introduction
In this policy, an Affiliate is any company which is not wholly owned or controlled by WPP or GroupM
and which an OpCo wishes to engage to provide media planning and buying services to our clients,
typically in markets where we do not have a presence.
An Affiliate is any company meeting the above definition, regardless of the terminology used to describe
it, such as “commercial partner”, “preferred partner” or “local representative” (this is not an exhaustive
list).
Our relationships with Affiliates carry a number of risks, including reputational, financial and bribery
and corruption. We have therefore defined strict requirements for assessment and approval of all new
Affiliates as well as ongoing monitoring of existing Affiliates.
This policy does not apply to:
▪ Companies which are wholly owned by WPP or GroupM; or
▪ Companies in which WPP or GroupM has a majority shareholding.
Note that even if an Affiliate is requested by a client, OpCos must still follow the process below.
2. Process
2.1 Role of Affiliates
Only companies which are wholly owned or controlled by WPP or GroupM are allowed to operate under
global client MSAs. In markets where there is no such company, we can direct clients towards contracted
Affiliates. Such Affiliates must have an overarching Affiliation Agreement with WPP or GroupM but
must contract directly with clients for the provision of services.
The process outlined in this policy must be followed prior to entering into any Affiliation Agreement.
The Affiliation Agreement will specify the limited extent to which an Affiliate is permitted to represent
publicly that it is working “in cooperation with” or “in affiliation with” WPP, GroupM or an OpCo brand.
Under no circumstances will an Affiliate be permitted to publicly present itself in such a way that
suggests it is part of the WPP or GroupM group of companies or any of the Opco networks, and we place
prohibitions on the use of co-branding, websites or email addresses which incorporate the name or logo
of WPP, GroupM or an OpCo.
2.2 Evaluation and approval process for new Affiliates
The negotiation and execution of all Affiliation Agreements will be overseen by WPP Legal (with input
from local and/or regional Legal as needed). Before commencing the contract negotiations, an internal
review and approval process, facilitated by the DM360 Portal, must be followed. Detailed procedures
are outlined below and for guidance on using the tool please refer to the DM360 Affiliate reference card
(available via this link).
2.2.1 Assessment (including due diligence)
Whenever a new affiliate relationship is being considered, an online Request Form must be submitted
in the DM360 Portal. This triggers the following mandatory forms:
▪ Affiliate Information Form – to be completed by the prospective Affiliate. As part of the
Form, each prospective Affiliate is required to sign the WPP Code of Business Conduct.
Alternatively, the Affiliate can share its own Code of Business Conduct, which will be accepted
provided it covers the same principles.

▪ Affiliate Assessment Form – to be completed locally capturing information from both the
Affiliate Information Form and the outcomes of the due diligence procedures performed (see
below). The OpCo must include a business case / rationale for requesting an Affiliate (e.g. no
presence in the market to service a global client; existing Affiliate is conflicted out).
Due diligence is mandatory for all new Affiliates and includes the following steps:
1. The OpCo must obtain external due diligence from a WPP-approved provider1 which must cover
(at a minimum) the following areas:
a) business registry information;
b) ownership and management (including directors, shareholders owning 5% and more and
ultimate beneficial owners);
c) creditworthiness / financial stability checks;
NOTE: If any of the above information (a – c) is not available via external sources, the
Affiliate must provide it.
d) government affiliations and political connections;
e) litigation;
f) regulatory and criminal records checks;
g) sanctions checks. N.B. checks must be conducted on all individuals identified in (b) as well
as the business entity and its parent company;
h) reputational checks; and
i) enquiries of local sources.
2. The OpCo must obtain references from other clients of the Affiliate.
3. The OpCo must conduct a site visit or a face-to-face meeting with the Affiliate’s management
team to discuss the processes and policies they have in place to ensure good governance (see
Checklist for Affiliate Management Meeting in Appendix 2).
The OpCo must document the results of these steps and attach them to the online Affiliate Assessment
Form. The submission must be reviewed by the GroupM Market CFO owning the relationship with the
Affiliate. Where any areas of concern are identified (including in relation to the ownership,
creditworthiness / financial stability of the Affiliate, or reputational red flags), local management must
be satisfied that associated risks can be appropriately mitigated.
2.2.2 Approval process
The approval process is bottom-up and sequential, i.e. all Affiliate arrangements must be approved by:
(1) GroupM Market CFO; (2) GroupM Regional CFO; (3) GroupM Global Legal; (4) GroupM Global CFO;
and (5) WPP Legal.
Legal teams must be consulted at each stage as necessary.
WPP Legal are the final approver and will oversee the negotiation and execution of an Affiliation
Agreement (with input from local and/or regional Legal as needed).
Please note that the appointment of an Affiliate is not approved until you receive a system notification
to this effect.
2.3 Ongoing monitoring
OpCos have an ongoing responsibility to ensure that Affiliates continue to be used and engaged only for
the reasons permitted by this policy and that any material changes to an Affiliate’s business are identified
promptly as they occur. This ongoing responsibility includes, for each Affiliate: obtaining an Annual

Letter of Representation (see Appendix 1); and, every 2 years from the date of final approval by WPP
Legal, re-assessing the Affiliate and re-obtaining external due diligence.
If information is identified, at any time, which might have given rise to concerns if known during the
initial approval process for the engagement, OpCo management must review the relationship with the
Affiliate (including the relevant Affiliation Agreement) and consult with GroupM Global Legal and/or
Risk & Controls. OpCos may be asked to re-obtain external due diligence (if they have not already done
so). OpCos must use the Affiliate Assessment Form to document re-assessment and re-obtain approvals
in line with Section 2.2.2 above.
Examples of such concerns include:
▪ a change of legal entity through which the Affiliate operates;
▪ a material change in ownership or management;
▪ related party information not previously disclosed;
▪ a serious ethical issue with the Affiliate; or
▪ a change in the Affiliate’s creditworthiness or financial stability.
If the local OpCo or any party involved in the Affiliate approval process becomes aware that the Affiliate
publicly represents that it is part of our network, including through the use of co-branding, websites or
email addresses which incorporate the name or logo of WPP, GroupM or an OpCo, the person with such
awareness must immediately consult GroupM Global Legal.
Regional management must monitor Affiliate arrangements across their region, including by
encouraging reviews of the above issues and reports of Affiliate performance.
If you wish to terminate an Affiliate, please contact GroupM Global Legal before taking any steps to do
so.

Appendix 1 - Annual Letter of Representation
Affiliate: Date:
The following letter must be signed by the company CEO and CFO annually and sent to [insert name of Operating
Company] (herein after referred to as the “Network”), at the address contained in the Affiliation Agreement.
# Item Comments
1 We are familiar with the Affiliation Agreement between the
Network and ourselves and have reviewed the Agreement
recently to ensure we are not in breach of any of its terms, since
signing the Agreement or since signing the last Letter of
Representation (whichever occurred most recently).
2 We confirm that since signing the Affiliation Agreement or since
signing the last Letter of Representation (whichever occurred
most recently) there have been no: changes of legal entity
through which the Affiliate operates; material changes in the
Affiliate’s ownership or management; previously undisclosed
related parties; serious ethical or reputational issues regarding
the Affiliate’s business; or material changes in the Affiliate’s
creditworthiness or financial stability.
3 We are aware of the content of all client contracts applicable to
our client portfolio and confirm we are compliant with all the
terms contained within. We have provided to the Network’s
account director for each client a copy of the most up-to-date
contract we have in place with the relevant client.
4 We confirm that we are acting as an Affiliate exclusively for the
Network in the territory, being [insert territory].
5 We confirm that we adopt a zero-tolerance attitude towards
fraud and bribery, including that committed by staff and also
corporately. This is communicated to all staff, and if instances
are discovered, there are appropriate disciplinary measures up
to and including termination of employment.
6 We confirm that neither we nor any of our Affiliate Shareholders
are the subject of any Sanctions. We confirm that the Affiliate
Shareholders are the sole legal and beneficial owners of our
entire share capital. We confirm that neither the Affiliate’s
management nor the Affiliate Shareholders have given or
received bribes.
7 We confirm that there are sufficient rules, procedures and
physical security in place to ensure appropriate confidentiality
of all client information, and that our key staff have signed
confidentiality commitments.
8 We are aware of WPP’s strict legal and ethical standards, as
contained in WPP’s Code of Business Conduct (CBC), and
confirm that we conduct our business in accordance with the
CBC.
9 We confirm that any Network trademarks, information bases,
proprietary tools and other Network services are only being used
as contractually agreed.

Appendix 2 – Checklist for Affiliate Management Meeting
Affiliate: Date of Meeting:
Before engaging an Affiliate, or when re-evaluating an existing Affiliate, the OpCo must conduct a site visit or a
face-to-face meeting (using Teams / Zoom if necessary) with the Affiliate’s management team to discuss the
processes and policies they have in place to ensure good governance. At a minimum, the meeting should cover the
points set out below, which are aligned with the confirmation that the Affiliate will be required to give going
forward in its annual Letter of Representation. Unless otherwise stated, the points below apply to both existing
and new Affiliates.
# Item Comments
1 For existing Affiliates: The Affiliate has confirmed that they have
a copy of the Affiliation Agreement, its management have
reviewed it and the Affiliate is not in breach of any of its terms.
For new Affiliates: The Affiliate has been provided with a copy
of the proposed Affiliation Agreement, its management have
familiarised themselves with it and they have confirmed the
Affiliate will be able to comply with its terms.
For both existing and new Affiliates: The Affiliate is aware of the
requirement to provide an annual Letter of Representation.
2 For existing Affiliates: The Affiliate has informed the OpCo of
any: changes of legal entity through which the Affiliate operates;
material changes in the Affiliate’s ownership or management;
related parties; serious ethical or reputational issues regarding
the Affiliate’s business; or material changes in the Affiliate’s
creditworthiness or financial stability.
For new Affiliates: The Affiliate agrees to inform the OpCo
promptly of any: changes of legal entity through which the
Affiliate operates; material changes in the Affiliate’s ownership
or management; related parties; serious ethical or reputational
issues regarding the Affiliate’s business; or material changes in
the Affiliate’s creditworthiness or financial stability.
For both existing and new Affiliates: Any concerns relating to
these matters have been discussed and appropriate
explanations/assurances obtained.
3 For existing Affiliates only: The Affiliate is aware of the content
of all client contracts applicable to its client portfolio and
confirms that it is compliant with all terms contained therein.

each client a copy of the most up-to-date contract it has in place

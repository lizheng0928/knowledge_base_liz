# Paying Non-WPP Agency Workflow

# Proposed workflow | March 2023

# 1. Workflow

This workflow applies when paying a non-WPP agency as a third-party vendor to provide services to clients, and WPP Media only acts as a paying agency.

- Applicant should complete the required preliminary steps, including 1) client appointment confirmation, 2) other routine vendor setup and contract procedures, and 3) Advance DD – see section 2.1.

- Once the preliminary steps are completed, applicant should confirm whether the appointed vendor standard clause is included in the relevant arrangement, as this determines whether WPP Media’s rights and responsibilities are discharged.

- If the appointed vendor standard clause is included, WPP Media’s rights and responsibilities are considered discharged, and the team may proceed with contracting.

- If the appointed vendor standard clause is not included, WPP Media’s rights and responsibilities are not discharged, and the case must proceed to the global approval (see section 2.2) before contracting can continue.

- Where global approval is required, R&C should coordinate the submission process. If global approval is obtained, the team may proceed with contracting. If global approval is not obtained, the business team should follow up with Global on the approval status or reassess and re-arrange the proposed business arrangement.

# 2. Process Details

## 2.1 Advance DD

- Advance DD is performed by Commercial through the WPP Media company background check platform https://www.xin.xin/ .

## 2.2 Global Approval

- Agency Commercial prepares the “Paying non-WPP agency approval form”.

- R&C reviews the approval form and submits it for WPP Media CFO and CEO approval.

- R&C submits the case for APAC and Global approval.

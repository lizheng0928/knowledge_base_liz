# General Policy Statement

WPP plc and its subsidiaries (**the WPP Group**) will not conduct business with a sanctioned country, business or individual. We will never do business in high risk sanctioned countries such as Iran, North Korea or Russia or any other sanctioned countries, the list of which may change from time to time. 

We commit to performing proactive and robust due diligence and checks on any third parties we wish to engage with, escalating any areas of concern through established channels and policies. 

The WPP Group will comply with all sanctions, embargoes, or restrictive measures (**Sanctions**) to which we are subject. We will maintain effective controls and use independent and comprehensive processes to ensure compliance with and awareness of all Sanctions which may impact us. 

The WPP Group is completely transparent about its operations in jurisdictions which are subject to Sanctions regimes to regulators, investors and its clients and partners. 


# Sanctions Compliance at the WPP Group


The WPP Group, and its people, must comply with all applicable Sanctions and must not engage in any business which violates, or has the potential to violate, Sanctions. 

Non-compliance with Sanctions is a serious offence. It can lead to criminal penalties, substantial fines and reputational damage for WPP. 

Due diligence checks, prior to any new engagement with new third parties, including partners, clients, suppliers and vendors, should be undertaken (by employees whose role it is) to assess whether those new third parties are under any Sanctions.  

Please use the WPP Sanctions search platform **Nexis Diligence+**, which can be accessed via your to conduct your assessments. Use of this platform enables you to search with the benefit of a cohesive, comprehensive and up-to-date Sanctions lists encompassing all applicable jurisdictions.  


Consistent use of this platform protects us from inadvertent dealings with sanctioned countries and entities and must be used for any proposed new engagements. WPP will use this platform to proactively maintain a watching brief, and receive new alerts, on entities and individuals within WPP’s community. 

Any breach of this Policy will be investigated, and where employees have committed a breach, disciplinary action will follow and may include dismissal.

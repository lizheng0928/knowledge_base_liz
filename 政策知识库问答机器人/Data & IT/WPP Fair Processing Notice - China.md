WPP FAIR PROCESSING NOTICE - China

1.    ABOUT THIS FAIR PROCESSING NOTICE

1.1.  This is the Fair Processing Notice (Notice) for GroupM (as defined below). This Notice applies to employees, officers, partners and other personnel of GroupM (e.g., secondees, temporary staff, and interns, etc.).

1.2.  During the course of  our  activities,  we,  GroupM, will  process  personal  information  (PI) (which  may  be  held  on  paper,  electronically,  or  otherwise)  about  our  people  and  we recognise the need to treat it in an appropriate and lawful manner, in accordance with the Personal  Information  Protection  Law  of the  People’s  Republic of China  (PIPL) and other applicable data protection laws. The purpose of this Notice is to make you aware of how we, as a PI handler, will process your PI.

1.3.  ’We  may amend this Notice from time to time to keep up with any changes in applicable data protection laws or how we process your PI, for which we do not require employees’ approval, but we will keep employees informed of material changes.

1.4.  This Notice forms part of the WPP Policy Handbook and is referred to in the WPP Data Code of Conduct, as amended from time to time. GroupM employees acknowledge that their PI is processed by GroupM in accordance with this Notice.

2.    DEFINITIONS

2.1.  "Personal information" or “PI” means information we hold about you from which you can be identified, or which is related to you. It may include but is not limited to contact details, photographs, expressions of opinion about you or indications as to our intentions about you and other personal information.

2.2.  "Processing" means the carrying out of any operation or set of operations in relation to PI, and  includes any of the following: accessing,  collecting,  storing, transferring,  disclosing, destroying or using PI in any way; the terms “process” or “processes” shall be construed accordingly.

2.3.  “Sensitive  PI” means PI that, once leaked or illegally used, is likely to infringe the human dignity  of  individuals  or  endanger  personal  and  property  safety,  including  biometric information  (e.g.  fingerprint  or  facial  recognition),  religious  beliefs,  specially-designated status,   health   status,  financial   accounts   (   accounts,   deposit   and   payment information), individual location tracking (e.g. whereabouts, lodging information), etc., as well as the PI of minors under the age of 14.

2.4.  “GroupM”,  "we",  "us",  “our”   means   GroupM   (Shanghai)  Advertising   Co.   Ltd.  and  its branches and affiliates, incorporated in China where the PIPL applies.

3.    THE TYPES OF PI WE COLLECT

3.1.  In connection with your employment with GroupM, we may collect PI about you:

(a)   directly from you;

(b)   from a third party acting on your behalf, for example recruiters;

(c)    from affiliates of GroupM;


(d)   from publicly available sources;

(e)   from governmental and other organizations; and

(f)    when we generate it by ourselves.

3.2.  Such PI may include:

(a)   basic information, including but not limited to full name, residence address, telephone number, government-issued ID information (e.g., ID card and passport), user account of third-party platforms (e.g., WeChat account, Huawei ID, etc.), personal email address, birth date, nationality, racial and ethnic origin, household registration details, religious beliefs, marital status, gender, social insurance and housing fund details, bank account details, emergency contact information, family member information (e.g., full name, date of  birth,  ID  card  number  and  gender  of  your  spouse  and  children),  educational background, degree, qualifications, skills and work experience, references, personal file, and work permit details;

(b)   information collected during the course of carrying out background and related checks and company secretarial functions on behalf of GroupM, including but not limited to details of prior employment and positions held, and details of payments made to or by former employers on termination of employment;

(c)    details  of  your  suitability  to  work  with  us,   including  but  not  limited  to  your  past performance at work, your character, details of your fitness to work, verification of the information that you provide to us as part of your application, and if legally permitted, any history concerning criminal convictions and/or health checks;

(d)   information  pertaining to your work  relationship, including but not limited to dates of employment,  job  title   and  function,  work   location,  workplace  telephone   number, working email address, employee number, department, business unit, business expense reimbursement   information,    training   records,   employment    contract   terms    and conditions,  supervisors,  salary,   number  of  hours   logged  or  worked,  grade   level, employment evaluations, reviews and appraisals details and records, disciplinary details and records, absence reasons and dates, business travel records, work-related accidents or injuries and other contractual benefits. We will ask you for information required for other  matters  such  as  processing  payroll,  conducting  training,  and  administrating benefits;

(e)   all the working paper you created during your work, including but not  limited to emails, data files, blueprints, memos, spreadsheets, presentations, etc;

(f)    information about your use of corporate systems and software, including but not limited to  PI  related to  account setting,  routine  inspections,  internal  investigations or dispute resolution cases;

(g)   voice,  fingerprint  data, facial  recognition data, image or likeness that we take through videotape, audiotape,  moving footage,  photograph or collect from other devices  (e.g., CCTVs) for attendance management, security, compliance or other purposes when you are in GroupM’s workspace areas or engaged in GroupM or its affiliates’ activities;

(h)   necessary and relevant information such as health information and whereabouts to be collected for  other  special  reasons  (e.g.,  epidemic  prevention  and  control,  or  work- related accidents investigation);

(i)    information provided through the WPP group whistleblowing advice service;

(j)    details of holdings in,  payments made by, and other transactions in connection with, WPP group share options plans; and

(k)    information required as a result of legal, tax and regulatory  requirements or internal policies.

3.3.  The above information may involve your sensitive  PI, and we will process sensitive PI when necessary to support our relationship with our employees, or when required by law legislation


or statutes. We will use our best effort to implement commercially reasonable controls and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your sensitive PI.

4.    HOW WE ARE LIKELY TO USE YOUR PI

4.1.  We will  process PI about our people for legal, personnel, administrative and management purposes and to enable us to meet our legal obligations as an employer. Specifically, your PI may be processed for the following purposes:

(a)   to  carry  out  pre-employment/pre-contractual  background  and  related  checks  and  to conduct due diligence (such as to verify the details that you provide and to ensure that you are suitable for the role for which you have applied) as determined by applicable laws. This may involve the use of a background check agency or other third parties acting as our agents to conduct these verifications and searches;

(b)   to respond to your enquiries and requests;

(c)    to meet GroupM’s legal obligations as an employer (where applicable) and perform its obligations and exercise its rights under your contract of employment or other contracts with GroupM. For example, GroupM will use your PI to identify you, pay you, and to evaluate your individual performance,;

(d)   for   the   management   and   administration   of   our   employees,   including   attendance management, employee transportation and accommodation arrangement, business card production, workforce analysis, position adjustment, administration of share options plans, implementation of performance evaluation, where applicable, to administer your pension;

(e)   to  provide  benefits  in connection with your employment or contract, including but not limited to purchasing commercial insurance for you and your family, providing medical services, providing learning resources;

(f)    for  the  management  and  administration  of  our  business,  including  business  planning, project management, the fulfilment of your job description and position responsibility (e.g., management of third-party platform accounts);

(g)   to  report  on and  monitor globally our progress in promoting equal opportunities and compliance with equal opportunities legislation;

(h)   to facilitate communication with you at your workplace through use of your workplace address, telephone number, and working email address;

(i)    for supplying and monitoring your use of work equipment or corporate systems including monitoring the professional use of computer equipment and telecommunication networks, as well as any other devices and machines used within the context of work;

(j)    to  comply  with  and  to assess compliance with applicable  laws,  rules and  regulations (including  tax  reporting  purposes  pursuant  to  tax   legislation),   national   standards, industrial standards, voluntary standards we decide to adopt, anywhere in the world and internal policies and procedures;

(k)   to execute terminations of employment and enforce post-termination obligations;

(l)    to  detect,  investigate  and  prevent  fraud  and  other  crimes  or  malpractice  or  carry  out internal compliance investigations;

(m)  for the purpose of, or in connection with, any legal proceedings (including prospective legal proceedings) and any regulatory requests for information, including to obtain legal advice or to establish, exercise or defend legal rights;

(n)   the administration and maintenance of our databases storing PI;

(o)   to contact you or your family in the event of an emergency and for business continuity purposes;

(p)   for health and safety-related investigations (e.g., epidemic prevention and control, work- related accidents investigation and preventive measures, etc.);

(q)   to operate the WPP group whistleblowing advice service;


(r)    for administration of WPP group share options plans;

(s)    for due diligence and audit in relation to corporate, business and asset acquisitions;

(t)    to  provide  references to third parties upon request, such as governmental authority or specific social organizations; or

(u)   for purposes otherwise set out in this Notice or as notified to you at the specific time of collection.

4.2.  GroupM is entitled to use your PI for the purposes listed above because one or more of the legal bases set forth in “Lawfulness” applies.

5.    DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

We will comply with the data protection principles in the PIPL and such additional conditions for processing as may be relevant under applicable law, including but not limited to the following principles.

5.1.  Lawfulness

We will process your PI provided that at least one legal basis applies. These legal bases include but are not limited to:

(a)    Processing your PI where we have obtained your specific and informed consent;

(b)   Where there is or will be a contract of employment between you and GroupM, we may process your PI for the purposes of concluding or performing that contract;

(c)    We may process your PI for the purpose of human resources management according to our labour rules and regulations or concluded contracts (where applicable);

(d)   We   may   process   your   PI   for   purposes   of   compliance   with   legal   or   regulatory requirements. Your PI may also be required to be processed in terms of specific local legislation or statutes; or

(e)   We may process your PI in order to establish, exercise or defend our legal rights or for the purpose of legal proceedings.

In  most  instances,  you  are  required  to  make  your  PI  available  and  the  supply  of  the information is accordingly mandatory. In instances where it is voluntary, we will inform you accordingly.

5.2.    Purpose limitation

We will only process your PI for the specific purpose or purposes notified to you.

5.3.    Necessity and minimisation

Your PI will only be processed to the extent that it is necessary for the specific purpose or purposes notified to you.

We will not keep your PI for longer than is necessary for the purpose or otherwise required by law. This means that your PI will be destroyed or erased from our systems when we are no longer required or authorised to retain it.

5.4.   Transparency

We will provide you with information about how we process your PI in this Notice or other means,  including  but  not  limited  to  categories  of  processed  PI,  purpose  of  processing,


processing methods, retention periods, and methods and procedures for you to exercise your rights under the PIPL.

5.5.    Data accuracy and integrity

We will keep the PI we store about you accurate and up to date. Please notify us if your personal details change or if you become aware of any inaccuracies in the PI we hold about you.

5.6.    Data security

We will  ensure  that  appropriate  measures  are  taken  against  unlawful  or  unauthorised processing of PI, and against the accidental loss of, or damage to, PI.

We have in place procedures and technologies to maintain the security of all PI from the point of collection to the point of destruction. We will only transfer PI to a third party if they agree  to  comply  with  those  procedures  and  policies,  or  if  they  put  in  place  adequate measures.

Maintaining data security means guaranteeing the confidentiality, integrity and availability (for authorised purposes) of the PI.

6.    DISCLOSING INFORMATION TO THIRD PARTIES

We may contract with other group companies, affiliates and other third parties to perform certain duties on our behalf concerning processing your PI for the purposes set out in this Notice. In so doing, we will disclose your PI to such third party. Such third parties are required to maintain the confidentiality of your PI and are permitted only to use your PI for legitimate business purposes as defined by GroupM. Examples of such legitimate business purposes include but are not limited to  managing  personal  file,  sending  postal  mail,  auditing  or  analysing  data,  providing  IT and software service, providing travel reservation services, managing third-party platform accounts, processing payroll, completing benefits administration, and recruiting employees.

In addition, we are a global company and therefore we may transfer your PI to countries for certain purposes around the world including but not limited to the US and Europe, to the extent permitted by the PIPL or applicable data protection laws. We will take appropriate measures to provide adequate protection for PI that is provided to third parties, and we will require third parties to put in place appropriate safeguards to ensure your information is adequately protected. Please refer to Appendix for the details of current cross-border data transfers.

7.    YOUR RIGHTS

To the extent provided by the PIPL, you may have legal rights in relation to your PI that GroupM holds. Besides the rights listed below, you also have the right to request us to interpret this Notice for you. These rights may include:

(a)    request access to and request for a copy of the PI we hold about you, and details about how we are processing your PI;

(b)   request rectification, correction, or updates to PI that we hold about you if it is inaccurate or incomplete;

(c)    withdraw your consent where we process your PI on the basis of your consent. Please note, however, that this will not affect the lawfulness of any processing undertaken before your withdrawal.


(d)   request PI to be transferred in a structured, commonly-used and machine-readable format to the extent this right is relevant in the employment context;

(e)   in some circumstances, request GroupM to erase your PI. Please note that there may be circumstances where GroupM is  legally entitled to  retain your  PI  regardless of any such request;

(f)    request restriction processing of your PI; and

(g)   object to the processing of your PI in certain circumstances, such as you have the right not to be subject to a decision with a major influence on your rights and interests based solely on automated processing.


Please contact our HR team to exercise any of these rights.

You also have the right to make a complaint to the supervisory authority if you’re not happy with how we’ve processed your PI.

8.    BREACHES OF DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

If you consider that the data protection principles have not been followed in respect of PI about yourself or others you should raise the matter with your line manager. Any breach of the PIPL or other applicable data protection laws will be taken seriously and may result in disciplinary action.

9.    CONTACT AND QUERIES

Should you have any queries regarding this Notice or processing of PI by GroupM, please contact our HR team.

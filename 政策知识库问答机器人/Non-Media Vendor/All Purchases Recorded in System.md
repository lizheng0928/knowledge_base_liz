**All Purchases Recorded in System**

Issued in August 2023

# Purpose & Scope

This SOP brief sets out how vendor purchases must be recorded in the purchasing systems, and how to decide whether a purchase is recorded in Minder or in Chorus. 

This SOP brief applies to all vendor purchases, including media purchases, media & non-media bulk purchases, and non-media purchases, made by all co-workers who handle vendor purchases in WPP Media China.

# Principal

- All vendor purchases must be recorded in a system, either in Minder or in Chorus.

- As a general principle, all media purchases are recorded in Minder.

- Bulk purchases should not be artificially separated into smaller parts, as this creates extra process or work.

# Decision Criteria: Minder or Chorus

Use the questions below to decide where a vendor purchase should be recorded. In principle, answer “Yes” means recording in Minder, and answer “No” means recording in Chorus. For the last two rows, the decision is made collectively with the other indicators.

**Principles of Purchase Recorded in Minder vs ****Chorus**

| Decision criterion | Control objective | Assessment question | Evidence to consider | System to record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service nature | Ensure the transaction is recorded in the appropriate system based on the nature of the purchase. | Is it advertising or a bulk purchase involving advertising? | Connection to media resource buying and any inseparable non-media costs included in the same purchase. | Y – Minder N – Chorus |
| Vendor qualification | Ensure the applicable vendor due diligence and approval requirements are followed. | Is the supplier subject to media-specific qualification checks or approval requirements? | Applicable qualification requirements, due diligence records and approval matrix. | Y – Minder N – Chorus |
| Intermediary involvement | Ensure the contracting, purchasing and payment arrangements follow an appropriate intermediary route | Is a broker involved in contracting, purchasing or payment instead of dealing directly with the ultimate service provider? | Contracting party, purchase order recipient, invoice issuer and payment recipient. | Y collectively with other indicators – Minder N collectively with other indicators – Chorus |

* Advertiser introduces or promotes its product or service directly or indirectly via certain media format, here excluding Nexus service.

# Escalation and Approval

Where a Minder booking for a media purchase or a bulk media and non-media purchase is not aligned, the case must be escalated sequentially to the Nego Head, the MM Head of Commercial & Agency CCO, and the Head of Risk & Control. If alignment cannot be reached, final approval from the CFO is required. Once aligned or approved, the purchase must be recorded in Minder or Chorus, as applicable.

Payment Terms Guidance

**Document Version:** 2.0

**Date Issued:** 10 September 2025

**Effective Date:** 1 September 2025*
































**1. Purpose:**

This Payment Terms Guidance document establishes the standard payment terms for third party spend incurred with the cost borne by WPP (**where ****reference in this Guidance to “****WPP****”**** includes all**** Agencies (excluding minority owned)**) and outlines the conditions and process under which deviations to those terms are permitted. 


The governance applied ensures consistency, supports working capital management, and protects WPP’s financial interests by aligning third party payment terms with industry norms and corporate priorities.


**2. Scope:**

The initial scope of the Guidance will exclude (i) SMEs (see Annex 1), (ii) individual freelancers and/or contractors and (iii) Client and client pass through spend including Media and Production. 


This Guidance applies to anyone in WPP involved in the procurement, negotiation, and payment of goods and services where the cost is borne by WPP. 


Where there is governing local legislation on payment terms, that will take precedence over the payment terms as set out in this Guidance (see Annex 2 for examples).


All procurement activity must follow the  and you must engage the Global WPP Procurement team according to the defined thresholds and processes set out in that Policy.


Where procurement activity is carried out locally (and not through WPP Procurement as per the scope of the Global Procurement Policy), before initially engaging a new third party or discussing payment terms, check if this third party is already in use elsewhere within WPP. This crucial step helps us leverage existing relationships and optimise consistency of our payment terms across the Group. Please utilise Conga, GEP and/or your local contract storage locations to perform this check (and if you need access to Conga, contact the team via the following link: ).


**3. ****Standard Payment Terms****:**

WPP’s standard payment terms are Net 60 days** from receipt of invoice, with payment being released to the third party on the next available WPP payment run following the payment due date. Of note we typically run weekly payment run cycles.


**WPP recognises established local market payment term conventions. If the standard payment terms in a specific local market are typically longer than 60 days, these more favourable terms should be accepted and maintained and not reduced to Net 60 days.


The standard payment terms (including reference to our payment run cycle provision) must be included in all third-party contracts, purchase agreements and/or purchase orders relating to costs to be borne by WPP (for the scope of spend as defined in section 2).


The obligation to implement our standard payment terms for any third-party purchases is **effective immediately** for all new and renewed contracts and purchases, but to allow you to update your internal purchasing processes and to allow the deviations approval resources to manage requests in a pragmatic manner the deviation process will be introduced in the following phased approach:


| Phase | Country(ies) | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | UK, USA, Canada, Germany | 1 September 2025 through to 31 October 2025 |
| 2 | Brazil, Australia | 1 November 2025 through to 31 December 2025 |
| 3 | India, Switzerland, Singapore, France | 1 January 2026 through to 28 February 2026 |
| 4 | Rest of World | From 1 March 2026 |


What this means is immediately, and no later than from 1 September 2025 **all** third-party purchases must, wherever possible and in line with local legislation, have our standard payment terms applied. It is the accountability of any employee purchasing goods or services on the behalf of WPP to ensure that this Guidance is followed. This Guidance does not apply to contracts or purchases made before 1 September 2025.


The Guidance deviations process set out in section 4 below will only come into effect as your country is captured in the phased approach set out in the table above. For countries captured in later phases, we encourage you to introduce a local finance sign off process to capture any deviations until such time as the Guidance deviations process is active in your country.


Where a third party is also a client, we will strive to achieve reciprocal payment terms. Our aim is to align our payment terms to them with the terms they extend to us.


**4. ****Deviations ****from Standard Terms****:**


We recognise that there are circumstances where we will be unable to achieve Net 60-day payment terms and therefore we will maintain a matrix of allowable deviations as part of this guidance (see Annex 3). 


To ensure compliance with all applicable laws, if local legislative requirements dictate payment terms shorter than Net 60 days, please bring this to the attention of . This will then be reviewed and where appropriate added as a recognised exception to our standard terms.


Aside from the allowable deviations set out in Annex 3, any deviation from the standard Net 60-day terms must be first approved in line with the table below before any commitment to purchase is made.

**5****. ****Use of Payment Terms in ****Negotiations****:**

Payment terms may be used as a negotiation lever only in exceptional circumstances and where commercially justified. In such cases, a reciprocal commercial benefit, such as a discount, rebate, or other value, must be sought from the third party to compensate for any concession made. If any support is needed in this matter, contact .


**6****. ****Responsibilities****:**

- Procurement/Purchasing Teams: Ensure that contracts reflect the standard payment terms before committing to a purchase and manage the approval process for any deviations.

- Finance Teams: Validate compliance with this Guidance during the contracting process (Finance Approvers) and upon invoice processing (Accounts Payable).

- Treasury: Review and approve deviations on high-value contracts and assess cash and working capital impacts.


**7. ****Limitations****, Review and Enforcement****:**

This Guidance is subject to revision at any time at the discretion of WPP senior management. It does not override any existing legal obligations or contractual terms unless explicitly stated.


This Guidance will be reviewed at least annually, or more frequently as required to reflect changes in business priorities or external market conditions.


Failure to comply with this Guidance may result in contractual, financial, or audit risks for WPP and could lead to disciplinary action.

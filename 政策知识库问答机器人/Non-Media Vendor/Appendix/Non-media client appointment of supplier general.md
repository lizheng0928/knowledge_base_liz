## Slide 1

### 推荐客户指定供应商函件格式

尊敬的客户代表：

根据贵司的指定要求， [供应商公司全称] 被指定为 [服务名称] (,或从 [ ] 至 [ ]这段时间内)的供应商。

请回复此封邮件来确认您的指定。

Yours,

[申请人姓名]

**备注: 此邮件要求发给客户负责人或客户授权人（或客户合约中约定人）

## Slide 2

### Recommended format of client appointment of supplier

Dear client representative:

According to your appointment, [supplier name] is appointed as a supplier of [service name] (,or for the period from [ ] to [ ]).

Please confirm your supplier appointment by returning this email.

Yours,

[Applicant’s name]

**Comments: this mail is required to send to the head in-charge or his delegates of the client (or as client contract stated)

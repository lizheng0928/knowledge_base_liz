PROCUREMENT POLICY 


# POLICY STATEMENT


WPP is committed to having the right policies and processes in place to manage the key stages of commercial delivery from third parties: from the initial identification of a business need to selection of the right suppliers to contracting with them to paying for the goods or services received – and to ensure that these stages are conducted in a legal and ethically responsible manner and deliver sustainable commercial value throughout. 


This policy governs and supports the selection of and contracting or renewing with suppliers or adding work orders pursuant to a master services agreement and must be followed by all WPP employees and agencies. 


Please note that this policy does not cover post-contract stages of commercial delivery such as ordering and payment activities – for governance and support on these please see the relevant Purchase Order-to-Pay Policy (note: all WPP agencies should have a policy covering post-contract stages including contracting, financial onboarding, purchase orders and payment activities). 


All WPP agencies and employees must comply with and be mindful of the following WPP policies whilst following this policy: 

WPP Code of Business Conduct 

ABC policy

**STEP 3*****:****** *****What is the total ****estimated ****spend**** of the work? **

This is the total amount that needs to be spent with a supplier over a 12 month period. If there are multiple stages to a project in the course of a year, all stages should be calculated together. Spend should not be broken up into multiple smaller work orders or contracts. Click for guidance on estimating total spend. Note: at contract stage this will need to be approved by the relevant authorised person as set out in the relevant Delegation of Authority matrix (note: all WPP agencies should have an up-to-date Delegation of Authority matrix in place and ensure that employees know about it and when to refer to it). 


For spend equal to or over US$ 100,000 across a 12 month period, please contact WPP Procurement who will partner with you to: 

identify and source potential suppliers; 

assess the risk associated with engaging suppliers to mitigate any negative impact on WPP; 

negotiate and establish commercial terms with suppliers at the best possible terms and price; 

ensure compliance with best practice, such as responsible procurement, sustainability, and diversity; and 

conduct benchmarking to identify trends and opportunities for cost reductions and process improvements. 


For spend less than US$ 100,000 across a 12 month period, continue to Step 4. 


**STEP 4:**** Can the required service be provided by a supplier on WPP’s Approved Supplier list? **

Clickor a live list of WPP’s Approved Suppliers. All suppliers on this list have passed through due diligence and risk checks with WPP Procurement. 


If the supplier you are considering is on this list, please (a) ensure completion and signature of this by the WPP employee requesting use of the supplier and appropriate management of any exceptions flagged on the form in line with WPP’s Related Parties & Conflicts of Interest Policy; and (b) liaise with your Finance team to verify identity and legal status (including business registration and licence checks, existence / registration numbers / DUNS, proof of banking and tax details) and see your network’s Purchase Order–to–Pay Policy to onboard the supplier. 


If not, continue to Step 5. 


**STEP 5: Does the supplier pass due diligence?**** **


**Basic due diligence **


For all new suppliers engaged for a period of less than six months or in total 840 hours across a 12 month period and with spend equal to or under US$ 15,000, the following must be in place to pass due diligence: 

A contract, purchase order or invoice template that requires compliance with WPP’s Supplier Code of Conduct and is signed by the supplier 


For all other new suppliers, the following must be in place to pass due diligence: 

Completion and signature of thisby the WPP employee requesting use of the supplier and appropriate management of any exceptions flagged on the form in line with WPP’s Related Parties & Conflicts of Interest Policy 

Completion and signature of this by the supplier and appropriate management of any exceptions flagged on the form in line with WPP’s Related Parties & Conflicts of Interest Policy 

If (a) either related party form confirms the existence of a related party or (b) the workstream that the supplier is to be engaged for relates to work for a government or state-owned body (as defined in WPP’s ABC policy): confirmation that at least two other quotes have been obtained from alternative suppliers or the chosen supplier quote has been satisfactorily benchmarked 

Signature of WPP’s by the supplier or confirmation of adherence to its own 

Code of Business Conduct that comprises the same principles as WPP’s Supplier Code of Conduct 



**Advanced due diligence **



In addition to the above basic due diligence, for all suppliers or for renewals where last checks were carried out six or more years ago and, in either case, where spend across 12 months is equal to or over US$ 100,000: advanced due diligence is required in addition to the above basic due diligence in the form of satisfactory completion by a reputable external and independent provider of reputation checks (which should include adverse media checks and confirmation that the supplier has not committed tax fraud or evasion nor bribery or corruption) and creditworthiness checks on the supplier. 


Following completion of the above five steps, please see the relevant Purchase Order-to-Pay Policy to contract with and onboard the supplier.

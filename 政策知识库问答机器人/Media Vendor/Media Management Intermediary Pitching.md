# WPP MEDIA CHINA - MEDIA MANAGEMENT INTERMEDIARY PITHCING GUIDELINE FOR SIGNIFICANT PITCHING OVER 50 MM CNY


## Introduction


The aim of this guideline is to further ensure the fairness and transparency of Intermediary pitching process and remind the key principles and standards for the best execution of the process. 

This guideline also describes the recommended steps to be rolled out from Jan 2025 in the pilot program for Intermediary pitching over 50MM CNY. 

For pre-existing requirements on Intermediary pitching, please refer to WPP MEDIA intermediary policy and WPP MEDIA China [SOP-Media Vendor management].  

The technology intermediary selection or pitching less than 50MM CNY is not required to follow this guideline, but can use it as reference. 


General Principles for Intermediary pitching

Intermediary Pitching shall follow below principles:  

- Serve the best interests of clients and WPP MEDIA and ensure fairness to Intermediary candidates

- Follow the necessary segregation of duty principle and implement independent review, to avoid conflict of interests and to ensure control effectiveness.

- Follow the pre-agreed selection criterions and process and provide necessary transparency to all stakeholders.

- Standardize the audit trail record keeping method.


Pilot program for significant Intermediary pitching (over 50 MM CNY) 

Below section describes the framework for executing Intermediary pitching over 50 MM CNY from Jan 2025, as pilot program. 

Note that the Intermediary pitching below 50 MM CNY can simply follow pre-existing process as described in WPP MEDIA China [SOP-Media Vendor management]. 

For pitching between [10 MM ~ 50 MM CNY], it is also required to perform the preliminary assessment when determining the final Intermediary candidates, by using the template in step 3 (tab2-assessment). 


Roles and Responsibilities

- MEDIA MANAGEMENT is responsible to maintain a Pitching Committee Member Pool to register those employees qualified to become pitch committee members.

- Negotiation team is responsible to define the Intermediary selection criterions and the RFI/RFP (request for information / request for proposal) template with support of domain experts from negotiation team, commercial, market intelligence, R&C, etc., which constitutes an expert panel. The criterions & template design shall reflect the best interest of clients and WPP MEDIA.

- MEDIA MANAGEMENT Commercial is responsible to execute the random selection of pitching committee members and plays the role of pitching committee secretary, setup necessary information barrier in convening committee and centralize the record keeping of various pitching records.

- A team independent from negotiation (quote as “independent team” in below) is responsible to source the Intermediary candidate as per the pre-set criterions. This independent team shall not have any reporting line to negotiation.

- Committee members are responsible to give faire scoring of Intermediary candidates and provide the rationale. The committee members shall be composed of representatives from 4 teams i.e. Nego, Commercial, Data Center, TP & Accountability, and shall not have any direct conflict of interest including any direct business involvement or any interests with Intermediary candidates.

- Expert panel is responsible to approve the selection criterions and RFI/RFP template and any variation from previously approved template, which are proposed by negotiation team. For the pilot run, the expert panel shall be composed of at least one negotiation team head, MI head, Commercial Head,  the pitching committee secretary, one TP head, the designated project manager (Lily Zhang), one control function (e.g. R&C) or their delegates.

- Senior management (CIO, CFO, CIGO) is responsible to approve the selection result. Especially in the pilot phase, Senior management can oversight the shortlist process, the pitching selection criterions and RPI/RPF templates, their material changes.

- Risk & Control is responsible to perform post control of pitching process on risk based approach.


Above setup aims to achieve segregation of duties and independent review at least in below domains:  

- The selection of committee members is independent from Negotiation.

- In candidates sourcing process, the sourcing criterions design and execution of sourcing are segregated between the Negotiation and the independent team.

- The pitching scoring is executed by committee members, which are independent from concerned negotiation.

- RFP and Scoring Template is designed by Negotiation, then independently reviewed by expert panel and senior management.

- Independent testing by R&C over the pitching process


General Steps:  

- Maintain the pitching committee pool and select the committee members on random basis

- Sourcing the Intermediary candidates longlist based on pre defined criterions

- Shortlist Intermediary candidates and perform preliminary assessment

- Define RFP and Scoring Template

- Collect RFP

- Run Pitching Committee

- Approval

- Post control


The details of each steps are described in the following sections. And indicated pitching roadmap is illustrated in appendix 4 for reference.  


Maintain the pitching committee pool and select the committee members on random basis :

- The heads of Negotiations, Pricing, TP, Commercial, booking shall nominate the qualified staff to constitute a talent pool.

- Nomination criterions are Director and above, at least 3 years’ working experience in WPP MEDIA and 5 years in industry, subject to team heads’ judgment on qualification.

- Designated Commercial staff will run random pick the pitch committee members via online tool. The selected committee members’ list is kept confidential until pitching day. The committee members shall be composed of representative from 4 teams i.e. Nego, Commercial, Data Center, TP&Accountability, and shall not have any direct conflict of interest.  For example, the member shall not directly be involved in the target activities of the pitching or have any personal interest in the Intermediary candidates.

- A declaration of conflict of interest and confidentiality shall be signed by the selected members (email is sufficient) and the secretary shall keep the records in the centralized location.


Souring Intermediary candidate long list by independent team under criterions preset by Nego

- The beneficiary Negotiation team is responsible to customize the selection inclusion criterions to source Intermediary long list (e.g. licensing status, scale, revenue, media owner award list etc.) and to define the longlist ranking criterions with respective weighting%. The beneficiary negotiation team can also setup exclusion criterions when necessary.

- The independent team will then source the Intermediary long list based on above criterions, by exploring market data and via preliminary RFI (Request for Information) etc. and calculate the ranking score.

Shortlist Intermediary candidates and perform preliminary assessment 

- An independent team recommends a shortlist of candidates (at least 3 candidates) based on the Intermediary longlist ranking. If the negotiating team disagrees with the longlist search process, the data used or the ranking, they can submit written revision request with comments and justifications. The independent team can agree to revise, or further escalate to the expert group or senior management for resolution.

- The negotiation team will conduct a preliminary assessment of the shortlisted Intermediaries, supported by the independent team (responsible for providing justification for the longlist ranking) and commercial (providing independent analysis of due diligence). The negotiation team should conduct an initial due diligence on the candidate, including an assessment of any other risks or roadblocks, and raise them at the preliminary assessment stage, such as conflicts of interest, commercial secret confidentiality issues, disagreements over standard contractual clauses, or other risks not disclosed in the external due diligence report. At the same time, the commercial team conducts independent due diligence analysis.

- The negotiation will send invitation to the top rank Intermediaries that have passed the preliminary assessment to join the pitching and loop the independent team in the communication. The Intermediaries accepted the invitation are the final candidates. A minimum of 3 Intermediary candidates should be selected for a valid pitching.

- The negotiation team, with the support of commercial and independent team, recorded the initial assessment of the vendor candidates, along with the longlist ranking, in the template （see appendix 2-Template of preliminary assessment）.


- In the pilot run, expert panels and senior management can provide input & review the criterions, sorting process, when needed. When the proposal is mostly in line with previous approved criterions, method, the negotiation only needs to seek approval on the deviated part.


Define RFP and Scoring Template 

- Negotiation team is responsible to customize the RFP (Request for Proposal) and pitching scoring Template.

- In the scoring template, the negotiation team can define the weighting for the 3 assessment categories (pricing, qualification and competency), for example 50%, 20% and 30% respectively. The negotiation team can also customize the precise assessment elements and their sub weighing according to the deal nature and specificities. Upon reasonable justification, nego can consult external parties to build a comprehensive sourcing/scoring criterions. For example, for pitching on client specific campaign, client can contribute to define the criterions with rationale or even approve the criterions similar to expert panel, as WPP MEDIA is supposed to help client to find the intermediary most adapted to client needs.  The RFP and scoring mechanism are illustrated in below templates in [appendix 3-Illustration of RFP and scoring structure and an example for reference].


- Negotiation team shall apply formular in scoring template wherever possible. E.g. for quantifiable criterions such as pricing, company size, the scoring shall be formular based.

- RFP shall be designed to ensure input consistency and enable comparison on the same standard. Collected RFP with non-standard input might be considered as invalid and discarded in pitching.

- Nego can set bid bottom line (an absolute value or variance threshold) in advance. For bidding below the bottom line, Nego can take measure e.g. request security deposit or memo etc., to mitigate malicious bidding risk.

- In the pilot run, expert panels and senior management shall approve the RFP and Scoring Template in advance. The negotiation team sends the template proposal to the expert panel and senior management with a review period of at least 48 hours. The review shall be completed by the expert panel and senior management within the review period, or may propose an extension of the review. The negotiation team can organize meeting to expedite the review. If the panel and senior management do not raise objections within the review period, they will be deemed as having agreed to the proposal.


Collection of RFP 

- After expert panel approval, the negotiation team shall communicate the criterions and RFP template to the Intermediary candidates and offer them clarification when necessary.

- The negotiation team and the committee secretary are jointly responsible to collect the RFP from Intermediary candidates. The recommended approach is that the secretary creates a designated pitching mailbox for receiving RFP, of which the access is restricted to secretary. Then negotiation team send RFP and ask Intermediary candidates to revert to this designated mailbox. As the mailbox owner, the secretary can then further forward the RFP to the necessary recipients (negotiation and committee members). Technical bids (relatively fixed fact-based information such as qualifications and capabilities) and the external due diligence report, can be sent to the negotiators and commission members in advance to assess the validity of the inputs , while commercial bids (variable information such as pricing, pre-payment capacity, etc.) must be opened on the spot (on the committee day).

- Before the pitching meeting, the negotiating team and the committee members should evaluate the validity of the collected RFPs. In the case of unclear, inconsistent or obvious misrepresentation of the calculations, the Committee shall request written clarifications and proofs from the Intermediary, and the clarified and corrected content shall not alter the actual content of the RFP. With the unanimous consent of the committee members, RPFs that do not meet the filling requirements, or Intermediary refuse to clarify, or bid below bottom line without corresponding explanation or proofs of bidding can be regarded as invalid bidding, and the reasons for the rejection of the bids shall be recorded in detail. In principle, Intermediary cannot amend the substance of a RFP after it has been submitted, unless there is a second round of pitching.


Run Pitching Committee 

- The committee secretary (a designated commercial staff) is responsible to setup committee meeting while keeping the committee member names confidential until the pitching day.

- The secretary shall circulate the materials (including the external due diligence report) at 48 hours in advance to committee members and negotiation team and inform them the planned timeslot, including sufficient time for Q&A and members group discussion. Committee members can propose to prolong the session when needed.

- The common standard for pitching is:

- 15-30 mins deal intro by negotiation, further explain the scoring criterions to the committee members and remind them of the attention points in scoring criterions, and the items to further clarify during Intermediary presentation. The member from Commercial shall explain to the members the considerations in the due diligence report and the due diligence matters to be further clarified by the Intermediary.  Any members can raise questions on templates and attention points in the discussion.

- 30-60 mins per Intermediary for their respective pitching presentation, including Q&A session

- 5-20 mins group discussion (committee members & Nego) for each Intermediary and writing down scoring and rationale.

- Before and during the committee, the negotiation team and committee members are responsible to further assess if still any unclear inputs in final RFP, any items to pay further attention or requiring further clarification.

- Intermediary candidates present their pitching slides during the committee in their allocated timeslot.  Committee members and Negotiations can raise questions to Intermediaries for clarifications. The negotiation team should not suggest any tendentious hints in the discussion.

- Committee members shall assess and score as per the pre-set criterions and template, write down the score rationale and provide their final score and rationale to the before the end of committee. If necessary, committee members may engage in a clarifying discussion, which is limited to clarification of facts and scoring criteria, and the negotiation team shall not make any tendentious hints in the discussion.

- The secretary calculates the quantitative scoring as per template and consolidate the pitching result. A senior negotiation (Director or above) shall perform a checker control on the calculation & consolidation and sign off. The negotiation team will then seek further approval from senior management.

- Multiple rounds of pitching are allowed. However Negotiation team shall inform the Intermediary candidates in advance if there will be more than 1 round of pitching. An example of 2nd round of pitching is to request Intermediary candidates to refresh the pricing section on the pitching day within a designated timeslot during pitching committee. The Intermediary candidates shall send the final refreshed pricing section in a password protected excel and only give the password to the secretary within the designated timeslot. The information of the previous round of the pitching should be strictly confidential in the subsequent round of the pitching, and relevant personnel should not communicate verbally or informally with the supplier before the final results are confirmed.


Approval 

The negotiation team shall seek senior management’s approval on the pitching result, with supporting material attached. The supporting materials includes but not limited to: candidates shortlisting records, RFP approval, scoring result and Commercial’s dual control records etc.     


The pitching result is subject to all internal approval including regional/global approval and in-depth due diligence. The communication related to the pitching result shall clearly state those conditions. 


Archives & Post control  

- Commercial team shall upload the approval with supporting materials in a centralized method (secured database or folder).

- R&C is responsible to perform sample testing on risk based approach and report the result to senior management. Senior management can advise the key risk areas which shall be tested in priority.


Publication and Review 

The guideline shall be published within MEDIA MANAGEMENT and reviewed at least on annual basis.

# WPP MEDIA中国 - MEDIA MANAGEMENT -5000万人民币以上重要供应商比稿指南


介绍  

本指南的目的是为了进一步确保供应商比稿过程的公平性和透明度，并强调最佳执行流程的关键原则和标准。 

本指南描述了自 2025年1月起对超过5000万人民币的供应商比稿推行的试点计划，及其需要遵行的步骤。

有关供应商比稿的现有要求，请参阅 WPP MEDIA Intermediary政策和 WPP MEDIA China [SOP-Media Vendor management]。 

技术类供应商的比稿和5000万人民币以下比稿不要求执行本指南，但可以作为参考。 


供应商比稿的一般原则

供应商比稿应遵循以下原则：  

- 为客户和 WPP MEDIA 的最大利益服务，并确保对供应商候选人的公平性

- 遵循必要的职责分离原则和独立审阅，避免利益冲突并确保控制有效性。

- 遵循事先商定的筛选标准和流程，并向所有利益相关者提供必要的透明度。

- 标准化记录留痕及其保存方法。


重大供应商比稿（超过5000万人民币）的试点计划

以下部分描述了从 2025 年 1 月起对超过 5000万人民币的比稿推行的试点计划的操作步骤。  

请注意，比稿金额低于5000万人民币的可以简单地遵循 WPP MEDIA China [SOP-Media Vendor management] 中描述的旧有流程。 

对于在 [1000万人民币~ 5000万人民币] 之间的比稿，还需要在敲定入围候选人时使用步骤 3 中的初步评估模板（tab2-assessment）进行初步评估并记录。


角色和职责

- MEDIA MANAGEMENT 负责维护一个比稿委员会成员库，以记录有资格成为比稿委员会成员的员工。

- 谈判团队负责定义供应商选择标准和RFI/RFP（信息请求/提案请求）模板，并得到来自谈判团队、商务、GMK、R&C 等领域专家组成的专家小组的支持。标准和模板设计应反映客户和 WPP MEDIA 的最大利益。

- MEDIA MANAGEMENT Commercial 负责执行比稿委员会成员的随机选择，并担任比稿委员会秘书，并在召集委员会中设置必要的信息屏障，并集中保存比稿流程中的各项记录。

- 一个独立于谈判的团队（下文中称为“独立团队”）负责根据预设标准寻找供应商候选人。这个独立团队不应向任何谈判汇报。

- 委员会成员负责对供应商候选人进行公平的评分并提供评分理由。委员会成员应由来自4个团队的代表组成，即Nego、Commercial、Data Center、TP&Accountability，并且不应有任何直接的利益冲突,包括不应与相关的候选人有直接的业务联系或利益联系。

- 专家组负责批准由谈判团队提出的选择标准和 RFI/RFP 模板，以及批准标准或模板的任何变化。在试运行阶段，专家组应由至少一名谈判组负责人、一名MI负责人、一名商务负责人、比稿委员会秘书、一名 TP 负责人、指定的项目经理（Lily Zhang）、一名控制职能人员（R&C）或其指定人组成。

- 高级管理层（CIO、CFO、CIGO）负责批准比稿结果。在试运行阶段,高级管理层可审阅候选人筛选流程，比稿评分标准和 RPI/RPF 模板及其重大变更。

- 风控部门(R&C)负责以基于风险的方法对比稿过程进行事后控制。


上述设置旨在至少在以下领域实现职责分离和独立审查： 

- 委员会成员的选择独立于相关谈判。

- 在搜寻候选人的过程中，搜寻的标准设计和搜寻是分别由谈判团队和独立团队来独立执行的。

- 比稿评分由委员会成员执行，他们独立于相关谈判。

- RFP 和评分模板由相关谈判设计，然后由专家小组和高级管理层进行独立审查。

- R&C 对比稿过程进行独立测试


一般步骤：  

- 维护比稿委员会人才库并随机选择委员会成员

- 根据预定义的标准寻找供应商候选人长名单

- 筛选供应商候选人并进行初步评估

- 定义RFP和评分模板

- 收集RFP

- 组织比稿委员会

- 批准

- 事后控制


以下各节介绍了每个步骤的详细信息。 附录4中展示了比稿时间线安排的例子以供参考。


维护比稿委员会人才库并随机选择委员会成员：

- 谈判、定价&TP、商务、MI负责人应提名合格的员工组成比稿委员会人才库。

- 提名标准为总监及以上，至少3年WPP MEDIA工作经验和5年行业工作经验，并取决于团队负责人对其资格的总体判断。

- 指定的商务人员将通过线上工具随机选择比稿委员会成员。入选委员会成员名单在比稿会之前是保密的。委员会成员应由来自 Nego、Commercial、Data Center、TP&Accountability 4个团队的代表组成，并且没有任何直接的利益冲突。例如，比稿委员会成员不得直接参与比稿相关媒体、供应商的业务流程，也不得与供应商候选人有任何利益关系。

- 利益冲突及保密声明应由选定的成员签署(邮件签署即可)，秘书应集中保存相关记录。

- 相关谈判团队负责定义用以搜索供应商长名单的选择标准（例如许可证状态、规模、收入、媒体所有者奖励名单等），谈判团队也需定义长名单排名标准及相应权重，用以计算长名单排名。相关谈判团队还可以在必要时设置排除标准。

筛选供应商最终候选人并进行初步评估 

- 独立团队根据供应商长名单排名来提出入围比稿会的候选人名单（至少 3 名候选人）。如果谈判团队对长名单的搜索过程，使用的数据和排名有异议，可以提出书面意见和理由，要求修订。独立团队可以同意修订，或者进一步上报到专家组或高级管理层进行决议。

- 谈判团队将在独立团队（负责提供排名理由）和商务（提供尽职调查独立分析）的支持下对入围供应商进行初步评估。 谈判团队应对候选人进行初步的尽职调查，包括评估其任何其他风险或障碍点，并在初步评估阶段提出，例如利益冲突、商务秘密保密问题、对标准合同条款的分歧或外部尽职调查报告中未披露的其他风险。同时商务团队进行独立的尽职调查分析。

- 谈判团队在商务和独立团队的支持下，将供应商候选人的初步评估，连同长名单排名一起记录在的附录2的模板中。

- 在试运行阶段，专家小组和高级管理人员在必要时可以提供意见并审查标准、排序过程。当提案与先前批准的标准、方法部分一致时，谈判只需要对与之前审批过的标准的有所不同的部分来寻求专家组和高级管理人员的批准。


定义RFP和评分模板 

- 谈判团队负责定制 RFP（Request for Proposal）和比稿评分模板。

- 在评分模板中，谈判团队可以自定义3个评估类别（定价、资格和能力）的权重，例如50%、20% 和 30%。谈判团队也可以根据交易性质和具体情况定制精确的评估子要素及其权重。在有合理理由的情况下，谈判可以咨询外部以建立全面的搜索/评分标准。例如，对于客户特定的Compaign，客户可以参与定义标准并说明理由，甚至类似专家小组一样批准标准，因为 WPP MEDIA 应该帮助客户找到最适合客户需求的中介。RFP和评分机制见 【附录3 ：RFP 和评分结构图示以及供参考的示例 】

- 谈判团队应尽可能在评分模板中使用公式计算评分。例如，对于定价、公司规模等可量化的标准，评分应尽量基于公式。

- RFP 的设计应确保输入的一致性，并支持在同一标准上进行比较。 收集到的含有非标准输入的RFP可能被视为无效投标而不予考虑。

- 谈判团队可以提前设置标底（绝对值或差异阈值）。对于低于标底的出价，谈判团队可以采取措施，例如要求保证金或签署备忘录等，以降低恶意出价风险。

- 在试运行中，专家小组和高级管理层应提前批准 RFP 和评分模板。谈判团队发送模板提案给与专家组和高级管理层，并设置至少48小时的审阅期。专家组和高级管理层应在审阅期内完成审阅，或可以基于理由提出延长审阅时间。谈判团队可以组织会议以加速审阅。若专家组和高级管理层在审阅期限内没有提出反对意见，可以默认为同意提案。


RFP 的收集 

- 专家组批准后，谈判团队应将评分标准和 RFP 模板传达给供应商候选人，并在必要时向他们提供澄清。

- 谈判团队和委员会秘书根据以下描述从供应商候选人那里收集RFP。委员会秘书创建一个指定的比稿邮箱来接收RFP，其中访问权限仅限于委员会秘书。然后，谈判团队发送 RFP 并要求供应商候选人回复到这个指定的邮箱。作为邮箱所有者，秘书可以在合适的时机进一步将 RFP 转发给必要的收件人（谈判团队和委员会成员）。技术标（资质能力等基于事实的相对固定的信息）包括第三方尽职调查报告，应提前发给谈判和委员以及时评估其有效性，商务标（价格，垫款能力等可变信息）必须现场（当天）开标。

- 在比稿会之前，谈判团队和全体委员应评估收集的标书的有效性。对于不清晰，不一致或明显表述计算错误的内容，比稿委员会应要求供应商提供书面澄清和证明，所澄清补正的内容不应改变标书的实际内容。 在委员们的一致同意下，不符合标书填写要求、拒绝澄清或低于标底并无法提供相应出价证明的标书，可以视为无效，并予以详细记录废标原因。 原则上，除非有第二轮比稿，否则供应商不能在提交标书后修改标书中的实质性内容。


组织比稿委员会  

- 委员会秘书（指定的商务人员）负责安排委员会会议，同时对委员会成员的姓名保密，直到比稿会。

- 秘书应提前 48 个小时将材料（包括第三方尽职调查报告）分发给委员会成员和谈判团队，并告知他们计划的时间段，包括足够的问答和讨论时间。委员会成员可以在需要时提议延长会议。

- 比稿会的参考时间标准是：

- 15-30 分钟：谈判团队介绍比稿背景信息，向委员进一步解释说明具体评分标准并提醒注意点、和有待在比稿会中进一步澄清的事项, 商务部的代表委员应向委员会成员解释说明尽职调查报告中的注意事项和有待供应商进一步澄清的尽职调查事项。任何委员可以在此时中提出评分标准方面的问题和提醒其他委员注意的事项。

- 每个供应商 30-60分钟进行各自的比稿演示，包括问答环节

- 对每个供应商进行5-20分钟的评分，并写评分和理由。

- 在委员会之前和期间，谈判团队和委员会成员负责进一步评估最终 RFP 中是否仍有任何不明确的信息，需进一步关注或澄清。

- 供应商候选人在委员会期间在分配的时间段展示他们的比稿幻灯片。委员会成员和谈判可以向供应商提出问题以进行事实的澄清，谈判不可在讨论中作含有倾向性的暗示。

- 委员会成员应根据预先设定的标准和模板进行评估和评分，写下评分理由，并在委员会结束前向委员会提供他们的最终分数和理由。如有必要，委员可进行澄清性质的讨论，该讨论仅限于对于事实和评分标准的澄清，谈判团队不可在讨论中做任何倾向性暗示。

- 秘书根据模板计算定量评分并整合比稿结果。高级谈判团队人员（Director或以上级别）应对评分计算及整合结果进行复核并签字。然后谈判团队将寻求高级管理层的进一步批准。

- 允许多轮比稿。但是，如果有超过一轮的比稿，谈判团队应提前通知供应商候选人。组织第二轮比稿的一种方式是要求供应商候选人在比稿日的指定的时段内更新定价部分。供应商候选人在受密码保护的 excel 中发送最终更新的定价部分，并且仅在指定时间段内将密码发送给委员会秘书。之前轮次的比稿的信息应在后续轮次的比稿中严格保密，相关人员在最终结果确定前不应和供应商进行口头或非正式沟通。


批准 

谈判团队应就比稿结果寻求高级管理层的批准，并附上支持材料。证明材料包括但不限于：候选人入围记录、RFP 审批、评分结果、商务复核记录等。

比稿结果须经所有内部批准，包括区域/全球批准和深入尽职调查。关于比稿结果的沟通应明确说明这些条件。

存档和事后控制  

- 商务团队应以集中方式（具备安全控制的数据库或文件夹）保存所有比稿支持材料和审批记录。

- R&C负责根据风险级别对比稿流程进行样本测试，并向高级管理层报告结果。高级管理层可以就样本测试的关键风险领域和优先级提供建议。


发布和重审 

该指南应在 MEDIA MANAGEMENT 内部发布，并至少每年进行一次审查。

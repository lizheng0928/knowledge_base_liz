重要供应商实地探访指南建议 

- Selection of significant brokers : Broker list will be selected by WPP Media China’s management in the first quarter of each year  as the objects of annual onsite visit

重要供应商选择：由WPP Media China管理层在每年一季度选定应执行实地探访的供应商名单，作为年度实地探访对象 

Timing of onsite visit: The new brokers will complete the on-site visit within 3 months after onboarding. As part of broker annual review process, to onsite visit before the second quarter of each year  

实地探访时间：新增供应商在入库后3个月内完成实地探访，延期供应商在每年第二季度完成实地探访。 

Participants in onsite visit : 1 commercial + 1 Nego to jointly perform this 

实地探访参与人员：商务部同事一名+谈判人员一名 

Remote video is allowed if broker’ office is out of WPP Media China’s office cities scope 

若供应商办公室不在WPP Media中国办公城市范围内，可采用远程视频方式探访 

Key points for onsite points:check original business certificate,#of staff,  designated WPP Media China service team setup as promised,  Chinese wall with broker team serving our competitor. 

实地探访要点：检查营业执照原件，员工数量，是否按照承诺组建指定的WPP Media服务团队，与WPP Media中国存在竞争关系的其它广告公司服务团队是否有信息隔离墙 

Visit records to be kept.   

实地探访记录需要保存

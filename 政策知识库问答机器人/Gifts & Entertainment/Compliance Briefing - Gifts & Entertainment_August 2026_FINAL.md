Compliance Briefing -- -Gifts & entertainment
1. Introduction
The WPP’s AFBAC Policy and Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality policy 1, must be strictly
adhered to by all employees. By complying with these policies , WPP Media seeks to
protect not only the Company but each employee from potential ABC violations of relevant
anti-bribery laws and regulations. 2
The purpose of this document is to provide guidance on WPP Media ’s additional
requirements with regards to gifts and entertainment (G&E), given or received. This
guidance applies to employees of all agencies and WPP Media units globally. Any
exceptions must be formally approved by the WPP Media Global Risk & Controls Director .

2. General guidance
2.1 KEY CONSIDERATIONS
The policies are not intended to preclude occasional G&E of reasonable nature and value .
As WPP Media is governed by both local and international anti -corruption regimes,
whether or not the G&E item is a local custom is irrelevant. In summary, the key
considerations for G&E are:

2.1.1 Prohibited G&E
Below is the summary of other G&E items that are not allowed . Any exceptions require
approval of the WPP Media Global Risk & Controls Director.
Absolute NO GO
• G&E to / from government officials are considered unacceptable and not permitted ;
• Cash, including spending cash provided by the inviting party;
• Gifts linked to volume of business with the provider;
• Items that are offered or received with an expectation of reciprocity (i.e. returning the favour) or with an
intention of seeking improper advantage;
• Items which are in breach of any applicable laws or regulations;
• Gift, entertainment or hospitality prohibited by the client contract ;
• Items which are inappropriate or offensive;
• Items, known to b e prohibited by the other party’s company or by any of our clients ;
• Lavish gifts , entertainment or hospitality (e.g. car, luxury watch, holiday, etc.);
• Spouses are invited and paid for by the external party 4;
• Foreign travel without the inviting party present; and
• G&E at the time / leading up to an important business decision involving the inviting party (e.g. pitch,
renegotiation of contract, etc.).

3. WPP Media additional requirements
All WPP Media use the global online G&E R egister 5 (StarCompliance) . This is a web -based
tool accessible via this link. The tool has:
• Separate forms for gifts and entertainment being given or received to account for
different risks ;
• Embedded workflows with set thresholds for local / regional / global approval ;
and
• Reporting capability to enable effective local, regional and global review and
oversight.
A user guide is available on the WPP Media Compliance site .
3.1 G&E GIVER OR RECIPIENT
All G&E above the nominal value must be approved by the employee’s Line Manager
before the G&E is offered or accepted. This approval must be retained by the employee .
Only G&E items above the local threshold (per person) are required to be submitted in the
online G&E tool. For all G&E Register submissions, evidence of Line Manager approval must
be attached in the online form. Any items above the local threshold require additional
approvals in line with the matrix embedded in the system and the rules described further
down in this Compliance Briefing.
The requestor must allow sufficient time for the approver(s) to review and action any G&E
items submitted (keeping in mind that there may need to be several levels of review
depending on the G&E value). Please note that approvers have discretion to reject any
last-minute G&E approval requests.

3.1.1 Sporting, cultural events and recreational activities
Personnel invited to attend sporting (e.g. World Cup), cultural / social (e.g. concert,
festival) and recreational activities (e.g. ski trips) must be of a suitably senior level such
that the invitation is objectively considered appropriate.
Note that all such activities / events will be subject to extra scrutiny.
For all sporting, cultural or recreational activities, Agency / WPP Media must pay for any
travel6 and accommodation . This should either be invoiced to WPP Media by the Supplier
at a commercial rate or booked by the employee via the company travel centre. Where
for whatever reason this is not possible, WPP Media Global CFO pre -approval must be
obtained .
3.1.2 Cannes
Individuals attending Cannes are approved centrally at WPP Media Global level . The lists
of approved individuals will be available to the relevant regional and global G&E approve rs.
Individuals outside the central list may not be allowed to attend .
Only entrance tickets or accommodation of reasonable value may be accepted from
vendors (subject to approvals) . OpCos must cover transportation for WPP Media and
Agenc y employees (i.e. not allowed to be paid by vendors ).

Appendix 1: Approver’s guide
This section is intended to help G&E approvers in assessing G&E requests. By having clear
rules and applying them consistently across our businesses we aim to protect our
personnel and WPP against the slightest appearance of:
• Undue influence -- -causing ‘improper performance’ by the recipient, i.e. not acting
in good faith, impartially, or in accordance with a position of trust ;
• Favouritism or making business decisions based on allegiances, personal interest,
or relationships (i.e. not in the best interests of WPP Media or our clients ); and / or
• Conflict of interest - any activity that conflicts or appears to compromise the
exercise of independent judgment. Refer to the Compliance Briefing -- -Employee
Conflict s of Interest.
Please note that any G&E being offered to government / public official s (see section 2.1.1)
is not permitted and will be auto -denied in the online Gift Register . If, however, an item
reaches you for approval and you note that question ‘Is the Recipient / Giver a government
entity / state owned company?’ was responded to incorrectly , you must reject the
submission and flag it to the Risk & Controls team.
NEW : Approvers must verify that evidence of the employee’s Line Manager approval has
been attached in each G&E Register submission. Local, regional and global reviewers must
not approve submissions where the required Line Manager approval evidence is missing.

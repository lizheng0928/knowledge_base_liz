**Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality Policy**** **


Any proposed gifting, entertainment or hospitality should be reviewed against this policy and will fall into one of four buckets: 

unacceptable and should not be given or received; 

acceptable and may be given or received; 

acceptable and may be given or received with prior approval; or 

acceptable and may be given or received with prior approval AND is valued above a set financial threshold and so must be disclosed on a gift register. 



**STEP 1: Is the gift, entertainment or hospitality acceptable or unacceptable (to give or receive)?**** **

The issue of what is acceptable can be difficult and may require reference to local custom and practice. 

WPP’s Code of Business Conduct states: 

*We will not offer any items of personal inducement to secure business. This is not intended to prohibit appropriate entertainment or the making of occasional gifts of minor value unless the client has a policy that restricts this.** *


*We will not **accept** for our personal benefit goods or services of more than nominal value from suppliers, potential suppliers or other third parties.** *


*We will not… give, offer or accept bribes, whether in cash or otherwise, to or from any third party, including but not restricted to government officials, clients and brokers or their representatives.** *

Gifts, entertainment and hospitality will be considered unacceptable and not permitted: 

where the law or client contract prohibits the gift, entertainment or hospitality (even if your contact at the client insists otherwise – note: you are responsible for knowing the rules around any client engagement you work on) 

where they are given or received around a pitch or key decision 

where they are given to or received from a government official 

where a cash gift is proposed 

For further guidance please refer to the following or contac



WPP’s Gifts, Entertainment & Hospitality Guidance Booklet WPP’s AFBAC Policy and AFBAC Guidance Booklet 

WPP’s online training How We Behave 



**STEP 2: If acceptable, is prior approval required?**** **

Line manager approval will be required: 

where the gift, entertainment or hospitality exceeds your agency’s financial threshold – please check with your local CFO or CPO 

where the gift, entertainment or hospitality is not nominal value (e.g. simple breakfast, coffee, lunch cost) 

where the gift, entertainment or hospitality is not a one off or appears to be becoming regular 

where the gift, entertainment or hospitality is in part or whole for personal use e.g. 

sporting tickets for an employee plus one 

where there is a conflict of interest for the giver or receiver (see further [WPP’s Conflicts of Interest Policy]) 



**What is the right financial threshold for my market?**** **

Each CFO is responsible for setting a local financial threshold. This must not be higher than US $200, it is usually lower. If you have questions about this threshold, please raise them with your local CFO. 



**STEP 3: If acceptable and prior approval is required and has been obtained, disclose on your agency’s gift register where the gift, entertainment or hospitality is above your agency’s financial threshold.**** **

All WPP agencies must maintain a gift register. Gifts, entertainment or hospitality above the financial threshold must be recorded in this gift register. 

If you are an employee of a WPP agency, please contact your local CFO, CPO or network Risk & Controls team for further information on how to access and record gifts, entertainment or hospitality on the agency gift register. 


If you are a WPP HQ employee, please complete and send it to wppgiftregister@wpp.com – this email account is maintained by WPP’s Business Integrity team. 



The process above (including approvals and gift register entries) should be followed prior to the giving or receiving of any gifts, entertainment or hospitality.

# Purpose目的：

This policy aims to regulate the client set up process of WPP Media China, ensuring its compliance to company policy and adapting to practice.

本文档用于规范公司新客户建立流程，以确保其符合公司政策并具有实际可操作性。

# Scope范围：

This policy and procedure is applicable to all WPP Media China managed departments and their employees.

本制度及程序适用于所有WPP Media中国管理范围内的部门及其员工。

# Definition定义：

Direct Client: Customers, with whom  agencies directly sign service contracts and provide services within the scope of the service stipulated in the business license. (For example: design, production, publishing, acting domestic and foreign advertising, brand consulting, public relations consulting, customer relationship management and other related business consulting services). 

直接客户：代理公司以公司名下各法人实体与之直接签订服务合同并提供营业执照约定服务范围内（比如：设计、制作、发布、代理国内外各类广告，提供品牌咨询、公共关系咨询、客户关系管理其他相关商业咨询服务）等各项服务的客户。

Indirect Client: Media activity is generated by a WPP Media company but the contract is signed with end customers by inter-company (any company where WPP owns 20% or more), and invoiced on the inter-company letterhead and payments by end customers.

非直接客户：集团关联公司（WPP持股20%或以上的任何公司）直接与之签订服务合同，并委托WPP Media集团提供服务，最后由集团关联公司开具发票和收取款项的客户。

Credit Insurance: WPP Media insure the insurance company against the client’s credit risk for direct client and indirect client under WPP Media.

信用保险：为防止客户信用风险发生，WPP Media集团为直接客户和非直接客户向保险公司购买的保险。

Client Credit Limit: Limit of insurance liability. The maximum accumulated amount of liability of an insurance company for credit risk in an insurance year specified in a credit insurance policy.

客户信用额度：客户保险责任限额。是信用险保单中所规定的保险公司在一个保险年度中累计承担每个客户信用风险的最高责任额。

Internal Credit Limit(ICL): For unsecured clients, ICL is an internal sign off process, through which local, regional and global stakeholders are made aware of the rationale to provide exposure to an account. 

内部信用额度：对于保险公司信用额度不足的客户，内部信用额度是一个内部的审批流程。通过这个流程，本地、区域和全球的利益相关者可以了解为一个账户提供风险敞口的理由。

Zero fee & commission contract：The contract where there is no remuneration paid by the client.

零服务费且零佣金合同：客户不支付报酬的合同。

# Principle原则：

- All new clients should be assessed for risk and credit rating by professional research & consulting company in advance, and get the client credit limit from the insurance company before we provide the service.

- 所有新客户在提供服务前，应事先由专业的研究咨询公司进行风险和信用等级评估，并获取保险公司给予的客户信用额度。

- Before providing services to clients, the approval process for client code setup should be completed first. Services can be provided, after signing a service contract with clients or getting the Service Authorization Letter from clients or the chopped spot plan/ signed spot plan by the Market Head of client or above level with key commercial term.

- 为客户提供服务之前应先完成客户入库审批流程，并与客户签订服务合同、或获得客户出具的服务授权书、或带有主要商务条款的客户盖章或客户（市场主管或以上级别）签字的排期。

- The client set up will involve a number of different levels of approval in different stages. Each approver should fulfill the review responsibility within his/her responsibility scope, manage client credit risk reasonable.

- 客户入库在不同阶段会涉及多项不同级别的审批，每个审批人在自己职责范围内应尽到审阅责任，在为客户服务过程中，合理把控客户的信用风险。

- Every client code should be consistent with the legal entity, whom we will contract with, invoice to, and accept payment from, i.e. one client legal entity one client code.

- 每个客户代码所对应的客户法人实体在签合同、开发票时应保持一致，一个客户法人实体对应一个客户代码。

- Client Credit check 客户信用调查

Agency shall conduct a credit review for each prospective client prior to the signing of any contract or letter of intent. The client company reviewed should be the correct principal to the contract.

代理机构应在签订合同或意向书前对每个潜在客户进行信用审查。被审查的客户公司应与合同主体一致。

Agency commercial team shall complete the Credit Check order [appendix 9.1] and apply to the financial team for credit assessment on the client, while it receives the pitch notice from client. The financial team shall inform Credit Check Company to issue the credit report, and then send back the report to when received. Generally, it takes 2~12 working days from request to received.

代理公司商务团队接到客户比稿通知时即应填写信用报告调查订单 [附件9.1]，向财务团队申请对客户进行信用调查。财务团队通知信用调查公司出具信用调查报告，收到客户信用报告后发送回代理公司商务团队，一般需要2~12个工作日。

- Client Credit Insurance 客户信用保险

Agency shall request a credit limit from the credit insurer for all clients on the potential highest exposure. In the event of the decision being nil or restricted from the credit insurer agency should assess the client viability for an Internal Credit Limit (ICL) or mitigate the exposure otherwise for the portion of uninsured exposure.

代理公司应要求信用保险公司对所有客户的潜在最高风险提供信用额度。如果保险公司给予客户的信用额度为零或有限制，代理公司应评估客户内部信用额度的可行性，或减少未保险风险部分的风险敞口。

Agency commercial team shall follow Credit Insurance Process to apply for the credit limit, which will protect agency against the client’s credit risk in cases of insured clients’ insolvencies or protracted default.

代理公司商务团队应按照信用保险流程申请信用额度，保护代理公司在被保险客户破产或长期违约情况下免遭信用风险。

If client couldn’t get the credit limit, agency should take following measures to mitigate the risk, including but not limited to:

- Request client prepayment;

- Request client to provide bank guarantee or the qualified parent company guarantee;

- A back to back agreement with Vendors/Brokers;

- If clients are also  vendor, hold AP to ensure exposure are fully covered.

- For Indirect client, request indirect client to buy credit insurance for the end client or provide guarantee for the full payment to WPP Media company even if the end client don’t make the payment.

如客户不能获取信用保险，代理公司应当采取包括但不限于以下措施，来减轻信用风险：

- 要求客户预付款；

- 要求客户提供银行担保函或有资质的母公司担保函；

- 与供应商签署债权转让条款；

- 如果客户同时是供应商，则控制应付款项的支付以确保应付账款能弥补风险敞口;

- 对于非直接客户，要求其为终端客户购买保险，或者由其出具承诺函承诺其会支付全部款项即使终端客户未支付款项。

Government clients are exempted.

政府客户除外。

- Zero Fee & Commission Contract 零服务费且零佣金合同

As a general rule, zero fee & commission contract must not be agreed since it creates potential tax issues resulting in additional tax penalties. 

一般来说，零服务费且零佣金合同容易引起潜在税务问题而导致税收罚款是不被允许的。

However, in exceptional circumstances they may be allowed following the pre-approvals set out. The Global Agency CFO or the Global Agency Commercial Director in respect of global client agreements, or the Regional Agency CFO in respect of regional or local client agreements must pre-approve such a contract. For multi-agency contracts, approval should be sought from the Global CFO or  Global Commercial Director.

特殊情况下，可以按照预先核准权限进行审批。全球客户零服务费零佣金合同需要全球代理公司首席财务官或全球代理公司商务总监批准，区域或本地客户零服务费且零佣金合同需要区域代理公司首席财务官批准。跨代理公司的客户零服务费且零佣金合同需要全球首席财务官或者全球商务总监批准。

- Other Risk Clause 其他风险条款

Other contractual terms and conditions that may cause risk to the Agency, such as:

- There is a special media buying KPI from client, but agency couldn’t sign a back to back agreement with vendor.

- The compensation for error and omission required by the client exceeds the stipulation of the vendor.

- The convention of abnormal traffic.

其他会导致代理公司风险的合同条款。比如：

- 客户对媒介购买关键绩效指标有特殊要求，但不能与供应商签署背靠背协议。

- 客户要求的错漏播补偿超过供应商规定的错漏播补偿条款。

- 异常流量的约定。

Without aligning with  China negotiation team, the Agency should reject the above clauses of the Client Contract which constitute a risk. In any special case, the Agent shall obtain approval from Agency Commercial Head, Agency Client President,  China CTO,  China CFO and  China CEO.

Generally, it takes 3~9 working days from request to receive Regional CFO’s approval.

- 如果是代理公司与全球客户交易，则代理公司将集中进行评估。

- Where there is a regional deal, the regional lead market should make the assessment.

- 如果是区域交易，则区域主导市场应该进行评估。

- Local markets only need to do local clients.

- 本土市场仅需要对本土客户进行评估

- 签订对WPP或WPP持多数股权的关联公司有约束的条款，需要获得相关公司, 法务和全球客户运营团队的特别批准。

- 如果代理公司指定的重要员工不再为代理公司工作，合同中不应包含因此被允许终止合同的条款。

客户需要经过资质审核，并报管理层审批通过和财务部审核无误后，方可在系统中建立客户代码以供使用。

以上人员审批签字确认该客户的使用和相关信息无误后，连同入库所需资料提交本地财务总监进行审核：

财务资料审核完毕，本地财务总监将入库申请发送至首席财务官审批。

Especially for local performance client, it will follow the following approval matrix：

对本土效果类客户，将根据以下审批权限进行审批：

- Trading team should work on the Credit Limit or ICL (unsecured exposure) approve ASAP.

- 谈判团队应及时为该效果类客户申请保险额度或内部保险额度。

# Periodical Review 定期审阅

Management shall regularly review client credit exposure, agency commission and client list, and ensure the risk of client is under control. 

公司管理层应定期对客户信用风险，代理商佣金和客户清单进行审核。

- Credit Exposure Review 信用风险审核

 China CFO regularly reviews the credit exposure status with Agency Commercial Head, and assess the risk. 

中国首席财务官定期与代理公司商务负责人共同审核风险敞口，评估风险。


- Agency Commission代理商佣金

- At least every year, SSC China team runs out a full client list from Adept with the agency commission, and the commercial team will verify the agency commission ratio and notify SSC China to update client agency commission in the system.

- 中国共享服务中心每年会从Adept中导出一份完整的客户代理佣金清单，商务团队核实代理佣金比例，并通知中国共享服务中心在Adept中更新代理佣金比例。


- Client List Review 客户清单审核

At least every year, SSC China team runs out a full client master code list from Adept with the below information:

Have not been traded in the past 15 months and there is no AR and WIP balance.

Or there is no credit insurance or ICL.

中国共享服务中心每年会从Adept中导出一份完整的客户代码清单，并提供如下信息：

15个月没有交易且没有应收账款和未开发票余额的客户清单。

或者没有信用保险或内部保险额度的客户清单。

Finance sends the list to SSC China team to deactivate the client code in Minder and Adept. If a deactivated client needs to be re-enabled, the application form should be resubmitted according to point 5 or a valid client contract and the risk mitigation certificate, including but not limit to Credit Limit Certificate/Internal Credit Limit .

财务将筛查后的客户名单通知中国共享服务中心在系统中停用相关客户代码。停用客户如需重新启用，应按照第5点或凭借仍在有效期内的客户合同和信用保险额度证明/内部信用保险额度等减轻风险证明重新提交申请。

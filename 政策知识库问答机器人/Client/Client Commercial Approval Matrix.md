Approval Matrix related to key client commercial topics. Applicable to all WPP Media Agencies.
Notes
1) WPP Pricing Council and Client billing & payment terms - Route notification Local->Regional->Global WPP Media-> WPP.
2) Client billing & payment terms - WPP Policy:
1. Fee - Payment terms for a new client or amended payment terms for an existing client relating to fees must be no longer than 30 days
from end of the month of service, or 60 days beginning of month of service. On an exceptional basis, and with approval by the Network
Global CFO and WPP Group Commercial CFO, payment terms of 60 days from end of the month of service can be agreed.
2. Third party costs and expenses - Payment terms for third party costs (including media) and expenses should be made at least on a cash
neutrality basis. The Agency should never be required to use its own funds for procurement or payment of client third party costs.
3) Fee at risk - This section does not apply to Proprietary Media / Flex. The fee at risk percentages should not to be interpreted as
acceptable or default fee at risk levels relating to media buying performance. Guidance is: i) follow the economic principle that more risk
equals more reward, for example, ratio of 2 upside to 1 downside, ii) media buying KPI should be clearly defined and documented with
input from the trading teams, iii) fee at risk for service evaluations, which are subjective and open to interpretation and manipulation,
should be kept at relatively small levels, and iv) a specified fee at risk as the sole remedy for missing media pricing commitments clarifies
that the Agency is not underwriting the pricing commitment.
4) Proprietary Media penalties - Following updates to Client Industry Body Client Contract Templates (e.g. ANA Version 3), clients and
media consultants are increasingly looking to include penalty clauses in client contracts, where the Agency fails to comply with any terms
relating to Proprietary Media e.g., “Full compensation (media costs and Fees) is required if Proprietary Media is booked without
complying with the terms of this clause [x]”. Agreeing to clauses of this kind are not proportionate to the risk the client is taking because:
i) the client will benefit from any media running; and ii) such clauses are in addition to a client’s right to make a claim against the Agency
for breach of contract for any losses they suffer. These clauses expose WPP to significant financial risk given the likely lead time for non-
compliance being discovered, for example, in a contract compliance audit.
5) Proprietary Media / Flex without an Opt-in - The following qualification process applies for approvers:
i) If it’s a “red client”, it’s an automatic "no";
ii) Any services you will provide to any international client should be cleared by the WPP Media Global commercial team & client
leadership first;
iii) The services will have been presented to the client (at an appropriately senior level in their organisation) and they have a strong
desire to use them;
iv) The client has been provided with the terms and conditions and are actively reviewing them, they understand them, they are aware
that they must be signed and we are confident they will sign them - you may gain comfort from the client having signed up to these
services in other markets; and,
v) If we proceed, then the services are clearly identified on any media plan and on any invoice and the client is reminded of the terms
and conditions.
Our objective is to ensure that we are being transparent with the client that these services are subject to different terms, that they are
clearly identified on any documentation and that they stand up to scrutiny by the client's auditors and consultants.
6) Audit and Guarantees - Approval is required from WPP Legal Team. Please note that Guarantees does not relate to media price and
quality commitments which is addressed by Section 3 on the Approval Matrix.
7) Liability limitation - Indemnities provided by Agency to client should be subject to review by legal counsel. It is important we only cover
risks that are within Agency's control and not to act as our clients' insurance policy.
8) Non-disclosure agreements - NDA received from 3rd parties must be reviewed by our appropriately skilled personnel.
9) No right to terminate client contract - Considerations would include not being able to exit a contract to mitigate the financial impact
associated with not meeting media pricing guarantees or some other liability or not being able to exit a toxic client relationship.
10) Client non-compete - Local market multi-agency decisions are approved by WPP Media local management. Multi-market decisions in
one region are approved by WPP Media regional management. Multi-region decisions are approved by WPP Media global management.
11) Centralised / cross border invoicing - After consultation with WPP Tax Dept. and if approved, guidance is that indemnities in favour of
the agency for any subsequent taxes that are charged and FX risk are required.

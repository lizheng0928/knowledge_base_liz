Compliance Briefing -- -Senior management T&E expenses
1. General
All employees who travel, including senior management, must comply with WPP
Global Travel & Expense policy as well as E mployee Expe nse policy available on
insideWPP (also see the WPP Travel pages ) .
1.1 Summary
• WPP policy is that Travel and Entertainment (T&E) expenses must
always be reviewed and approved by someone more senior than the
claimant;
• Expenses of local WPP Media CEOs must be reviewed by the regional
WPP Media CFO on at least a quarterly basis – see section 2.1 ;
• Regional and global senior management - where individual ’s primary
manager is based in a different country and there is no one more senior
in the same office, the expenses must be either:
▪ Pre-approved by their reporting manager; or
▪ Reviewed by their reporting manager on at least a
quarterly basis.

2. Senior management expenses
WPP policy is that T&E expenses must always be reviewed and approved by
someone more senior than the claimant. Therefore , additional levels of review
have been set in relation to the market , regional and global management,
including CEOs and CFOs.
Please note that these additional reviews are not simply a ’rubber stamping’
exercise. The reviewer must ensure adherence to the relevant policies, e.g. that
all expenses must be wholly for business purpose and must be ‘reasonable’. You
can refer to WPP policies mentioned above for further guidance.
2.1 Local markets
Market CFOs can approve the expenses of the market CEOs prior to payment to
avoid any delay in reimbursement. However, the expenses of all local WPP Media
CEOs must also be reviewed by the regional WPP Media CFO (or a designated
delegate) on at least a quarterly basis.

2.2 Regional and global
For regional and global management where level up approval for reimbursement
is not practical, for example individual’s primary manager is based in a different
country and there is no one more senior in the same office who would have an
overview of individual’s respons ibilities and scope of work, alternative review
procedures should be set up. Whilst, it is acceptable for such individual’s T&E
expense s to be reviewed by someone with sufficient senior ity in the same office
for processing, the expenses must a lso be either:
• Pre-approved by their primary manager ; or
• Reviewed by their primary manager on at least a quarterly basis.
Specifically, t he T&E expenses of the regional and global CEOs and CFOs must be
reviewed in line with the CEO & CFO Approval Matrix (refer to Appendix 1).
2.3 Quarterly T&E review procedures
The following procedures apply to the quarterly T&E expense review and approval:
1. Expenses must be submitted to the next level up approver (see Appendix 1:
CEO & CFO T&E Approval Matrix) no later than the 20th of the month following
the quarter end (e.g. 20th April for Q1).
2. Expenses can be submitted via individual expense reports or the expense
summary (see Appendix 2: Quarterly T&E Summary -- -submission form). Copies
of receipts are not required to be attached but should be made available in
case requested by the approver.
3. It is recommended that the approver assigns a suitable member of his team to
carry out a more detailed pre -review, including reviewing copies of receipts
as necessary , under the approver’s responsibility. This review can be done on
a sample basis.

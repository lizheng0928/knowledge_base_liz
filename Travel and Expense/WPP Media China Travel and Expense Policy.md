### 差旅及费用报销政策

										Updated in Aug 21, 2026

**General**** ****Principle**** ****for**** ****Expenses**** ****Claim**

**费用报销基本准则**


All employees are responsible for understanding and complying with the company’s policy regarding expense reimbursement. It is employees’ responsibility to provide explanations and original receipts / fapiaos or other documentation that clearly document and support the business purpose and reimbursable nature of the expense. All employees should neither gain nor lose personally by incurring reasonable business expenses and to be responsible and prudent in spending our clients' and the company's money. A reasonable approach is to spend the money as if it were our own; 

所有员工有责任理解并遵守公司关于费用报销的政策。员工有责任对费用的花销做出充分合理的解释，并且提供原始收据/发票及其他支持性文件能够说明费用性质和相关商业目的。员工不应该因为公司业务发生费用而从中获利或者损失，并且需负责的谨慎对待客户或公司承担的花销，如同对待自己承担的花销。


All expense claim form should be approved by the appropriate authorized approvers. The  approvers should review both the reasonableness as well as accuracy of each expense item supported by original invoice information, including date, time, amount, company’s name, and etc., that matches with the expense claim description.  

报销申请必须得到相关主管的签字认可。费用审批人，需要复核费用的合理性和真实性，包括费用相关原始凭证的日期、时间、金额、公司名字等等，确保原始凭证与报销费用性质描述相符。


All expense claims should apply through In House Expense Reimbursement system first to get approve and no reimbursement will be accepted without application online.

报销申请必须通过公司报销管理系统，未在系统内录入的申请将不被接受

Approved expenses claim form must be submitted to Finance Department together with official tax receipt.

已批准的报销申请表必须同正规的税务发票一起递交到财务部门。

Expenses must be claimed within three month of being incurred.

报销申请必须在费用发生后的 3 个月之内提交。

The company will not accept any expense claim, which is not proceeded according to this Travel and Expense Policy.

公司不接受任何不符合本差旅及费用报销政策的报销申请。

For detail category of reimbursement, please refer the appendix 2.

具体报销类型，请参照附件 2.	



# Entertainment, Internal Meeting

### 招待费、内部会议费


The claim form must be stated clearly that the particulars and staff involved in the function, and submit to Finance Department together with related fapiaos.报销申请表必须写清费用明细及参与活动的员工姓名，并附上金额一致的发票提交财务部审核。


Expense approver should perform due diligence review before approving the expense claim based on the consideration of real business need. 

费用批准人需结合实际业务需求，认真审核后方可批准相关费用报销。


Meeting Expense: Stop all expense and reimbursement of renting off-site meeting room, please leverage company meeting room or telecom/online meeting to hold meetings. If it is a necessary requirement to rent off-site meeting room, please get WPP Media China CEO and CFO mail approval before the meeting.

会议费用：停止一切形式的由于租赁非办公地点的会议室而产生的费用和报销。如需进行会议，请利用公司会议室开会,或以电话会议或线上会议的形式代替。如果确实有租赁非办公地点的会议室的需要，请事先获得WPP Media China CEO 和 CFO 邮件批准后方可安排相应会议.


Prior to external entertainment or internal meeting, employee should apply for Client Cluster Leader (Functional Business Head in WPP Media supporting function units) and Commercial Director (FP&A head for WPP Media supporting function units and GMT commercial for GMT)’s pre-approval via Expense Management System with proper business purpose for justification, in principle expenditure per person should not exceed RMB 300, the approver will decide whether to approve or not depending on the budget usage. For any exceptional case, applicant should apply for WPP Media China CEO and CFO’s pre-approval via system, only WPP Media China Exco members are exempt from above pre-approval process. 

业务招待费或者内部会务发生前，员工应先在Expense Management 系统中“我的费用申请”提交申请理由和相关信息给Client Cluster Leader 和 Commercial Director预先批准 (WPP Media supporting function units员工需要得到Functional Business Head和Head of FP&A/GMT commercial预先批准), 原则上人均费用不得超过RMB300/人, 审批人会视预算使用情况决定是否批准，如果预申请的人均超过300元，系统会提交给WPP Media China CFO和CEO拿预先特批, 仅仅WPP Media China Exco成员被免除以上预先申请流程。


Pre-approval application should be submitted no later than the day when such expenditure incurs.

预申请的创建日期必须在该费用实际发生日期之前。


All the external entertainment and internal meeting expenses claims need to declare the number of attendees, the name of participate, the title of participate as well as nature and purpose of the activities. Any reimbursement other than meals or drinks which claim to external entertainment or internal meeting expenses need to get pre-approval from Client Cluster Leader and WPP Media China CFO.

公司外部招待费及内部会议费均应提供充足的详细信息作为支持，包括与会人数、与会（指

定）人员姓名、职位、会议性质和目的。任何非餐饮消费项目相关的外部招待费及内部会议费，需预先得到 Client Cluster Leader 和WPP Media China CFO 的批准。


When more than one member of WPP participates in a meal or other refreshments, the most senior member of staff should settle the bill and reimburse expense, violating this rule will also trigger compliance issue.

如有超过一人以上的WPP Media员工参加的活动，由最高级别的员工付帐单并报销,违反此规定还将触犯合规条例。


Some clients cannot accept any form of entertainment and are subject to their own polices and you should make sure you are aware of those restrictions or limits.

如果客户根据其内部政策要求，不能接受任何形式的招待，请确保了解相关限制及要求。

# FLIGHT BOOKINGS

# 机票预订


At all times the lowest preferred carrier and/or applicable promotional fares should be used. Substantial savings can be made in this way and personal preferences for other airlines will not be permitted. (Prefer to use” WPP Corporate Airline Class Indication” and the list will be updated by Admin department based on WPP procument instruction.)

在任何时候机票应该首选最低舱位和可适用的促销价。个人偏好的其他航空公司不被使用。（首选是“WPP 协议航空公司机票仓位”，清单会由行政部门根据集团的指示定期更新）


If there is a requirement to cancel, exchange or downgrade a flight, it is the traveler’s responsibility to ensure that a credit or refund is obtained for any deviation from the ticket originally purchased. Downgrading to allow others (i.e. family, friends etc.) to travel at WPP expense is not permitted。

如果有要求取消,交换或下调一个舱位,在原来机票的基础上，出差人有责任确保有信用担 保或退款。使用集团的差旅预算，下调舱位为了让他人(如家庭成员、朋友等)同行是不允许的。


If an employee is accompanying a client, at the client’s expense, he or she can travel in the same class as the client travels having received prior authorization from the client company. However, when travelling alone at client expense, subject to written confirmation from the client, then business class is acceptable. (An employee should never travel in a higher class than a client). 

如果员工陪同客户出差，经客户公司的书面确认且费用由客户方承担时，员工可与客户乘 坐同等级舱位。（员工不能乘坐高于客户的舱位）


WPP Media EXCO are permitted to travel in business class.

WPP Media EXCO成员可乘搭商务舱。


First class air travel is not permitted except with the express authority of the WPP Group Finance Director.

不允许员工乘搭头等舱除非取得WPP集团财务总监明确授权允许。


The permitted classes of air travel for Non-WPP Media EXCO staffs, with exceptions as stipulated, are as follows

非 WPP Media EXCO员工适用的舱位如下：


All flights less than 6 hours: Economy Class, with restricted fares where possible.

通常情况下，小于6小时的行程，出差人必须乘坐经济舱


All flights over 6 hours: Premium Economy Class, which is subject to approval first. If there are any exceptional cases, it is subject to CEO and CFO approval.  

通常情况下，超过 6小时的行程在得到批准以后可搭乘高级经济舱。如有任何特殊情况，需要取得CEO及CFO的批准。•

出差人报销请遵从 WPP 差旅政策。不符合政策内的报销不予批准。

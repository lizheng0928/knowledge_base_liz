## 1 Policy Purpose

WPP is committed to building better futures for our people, planet, clients, and communities. This policy outlines the role employees can play in reducing our carbon footprint and costs incurred on the company’s behalf. 

Limiting our business travel is one of the biggest ways we can reduce our carbon footprint. In addition, business travel and expenses (T&E) represent one of the largest spend areas that WPP employees can directly influence. We should ensure that we adopt a consistent approach to approving and managing travel and expenses throughout WPP. 

In this policy, employees will find rules and guidelines to ensure that all costs incurred are necessary and reasonable and all employees conduct business in the most efficient, effective, and secure manner. It also prioritises the personal safety and wellbeing of employees when travelling for business. 

This policy covers business travel as well as Meetings, Conferences and Events (MCE). It is applicable to all employees of WPP and its Agencies and supersedes all previous travel policies. 

Contractors / Contingent workers and Suppliers travelling on behalf of WPP should refer to the Travel Policy that is attached to their respective contract. In case of absence, they must follow this policy. 

By complying with this policy, travellers: 

• Operate within the WPP duty of care parameters that have been defined and agreed ensuring wellbeing for our employees; 

• Are always visible within the WPP Risk Management programme ensuring traveller safety and security; and 

• Contribute to the cost efficiency of WPP by supporting and maximising WPP’s ability to secure negotiated best in class rates. 

The sets out the principles that help us to act responsibly and support all of us in making the right decisions. We expect WPP employees to act according to our Code’s principles when travelling on company business. These include WPP’s zero tolerance for human rights abuses, such as human trafficking and sexual exploitation. 

Further details of the WPP travel programme are on our dedicated Travel pages on

## 2 Travel and the Environment

WPP committed to reaching in our value chain by 2030. We are doing this because it is the right thing to do, and we are sharing our goals in this policy because we recognise that to meet our targets, we need to work together. 

One single flight between London and New York in economy class is approximately 1.19 tonnes of carbon and this equates to 4 mature trees to offset the carbon output. 

To help us achieve our net zero commitment, employees should consider travel alternatives as a first option to accomplish a business task. In addition to using Microsoft Teams, WPP has invested in video collaboration technology connecting our global offices to reduce the amount of physical travel required and this should be used as a first option. Travel should only be undertaken if it is the only viable option. 



## 3 Compliance

All employees who incur expenses when travelling on behalf of WPP should comply with this policy and manage their expenses in an efficient and timely manner. Reporting will be carried out to assess levels of compliance with the T&E policy and continued non-compliance of the policy will be grounds for the following action(s): 

Delay or withholding of reimbursement. 

Reclaiming of monies from the employee for expensed items via the corporate card. 

Notification of repeated violations to the People team. 

Disciplinary action for the employee and approving manager. 



## 4 Travel Authorisation & Planning

### 4.1 Process and Approval

Prior to planning your travel, employees should secure pre-approval from the Budget holder for any travel plans following the process set by that Budget holder. 

All travel arrangements should be made via WPP’s Global Travel Management Companies where applicable. 

Contact details of WPP's designated Travel Management Companies can be downloaded  

Where an online booking tool is available, this system should be used for all simple trips. 

All T&E costs should be charged to an individual corporate card when possible. Contact your finance team to learn the process for applying for a corporate card for your Agency. 

Employees should plan trips and obtain the necessary approvals to ensure that all travel reservations are made at least 14 days in advance of departure date whenever possible. 

Employees should also consider the time required to obtain necessary travel documents such as visas when planning business travel. 


### 4.2 Visas

WPP will cover the costs of obtaining a visa for the purposes of travelling on business. These should be obtained via our travel management company or a WPP approved supplier. 

These applications should be made well in advance to avoid unnecessary “express visa” charges. 

Employees should not travel into countries that require a business visa using a tourist visa. 


### 4.3 Passports

If an employee’s job function requires a large amount of international travel resulting in the requirement of an additional passport for visa purposes, or frequent passport replacement prior to normal expiry due to pages being full, they may be able to claim this as a legitimate expense. 

This is applicable on a case-by-case basis and should be pre-authorised by the relevant budget cost centre holder. 

WPP will not reimburse the expense of renewing a passport that has expired at the normal end of its validity. 


### 4.4 Health

Employees are responsible to be aware of and adhere to all vaccination or testing recommendations and requirements for the country they are travelling from, to or transiting. 

Employees should not attempt circumventing travel restrictions to countries. 

The employer will cover the cost of vaccinations recommended or required for the travel destination where charged. 

By travelling on business, the employee is assuring the employer that they are fit to travel. 


### 4.5 Security and Risk Management

Traveller safety and security is of paramount importance to WPP, and protocols are in place to support travellers in the event of an incident which may threaten their safety, security or health. 

In instances where travel is booked on behalf of another employee, the booker must provide the contact details of the employee who is travelling. 

This ensures the traveller can be contacted in case of an emergency. 

Travellers should ensure their personal details are maintained correctly in their travel profile and travel reservations are made via the authorised channels only. 

Travellers will need to download the International SOS app if they wish to receive information alerts, access to the 24/7 emergency helpline and be contactable in an emergency. 

Travellers will only receive incident alerts if they have booked travel via an approved WPP travel channel and have included the correct email address and phone number in their booking. 

Travellers should take appropriate security precautions to mitigate their travel risks and respond to requests from International SOS confirming their safety in an incident. 


### Travelling Together

The below should be followed when colleagues are travelling together: 

Wherever practicable, the CEO should not travel by air, road, or rail with both the COO and CFO together. 

No more than 30% of an Executive Committee must travel by air, road, or rail together. 

No more than 50% of any Executive Committee (EC) member’s first line reports can travel together by air, road, or rail. 

Senior management cannot travel with more than 50% of their direct reports by air, road, or rail. 

No more than 20% of any department should travel together by air, road, or rail. 

Road travel is more likely to encounter delay or accident than air travel. 

Any exceptions to the above should be signed off by the relevant Agency’s Global Executive Committee member in advance of travel. 


### High Risk Destinations

WPP seeks to ensure that employees travelling to high-risk countries are aware of the risks. 

Provided bookings are completed via WPP’s chosen travel booking systems, travellers to high-risk destinations shall be alerted by email. 

All WPP travellers making reservations to a high-risk destination are required to gain pre-travel approval from the designated level of authority within WPP as outlined below: 

Moderate risk destination – Line Manager Approval. 

High risk destination –WPP CMBR Approval (cmbr@wpp.com). 

Travellers can locate up to date country risk information via our security provider International SOS. Detailed information can be found  

Travellers should ensure that they take appropriate security precautions to mitigate their travel risks. 

There are some countries that WPP employee travel insurance does not cover and for which special insurance clearance is required. These excluded territories are noted on the WPP employee travel insurance policy and travellers should check for exclusions especially when travelling to new market countries. Contact the for further information.

## 5 Senior Management Travel Group

WPP companies may decide to create a Senior Management Travel Group (SMTG) to cater for senior executives in their companies. It is the responsibility of the Agency’s Global CFO to own and maintain the members of this group, following the process defined by WPP Procurement. 

SMTG members are allowed to travel business class on all routes, however conditions relating to the use of restricted fares and lowest logical fare policy still apply. For the US domestic flights, if business class is not offered in that route, first class is then permitted. 

SMTG members should use the WPP Preferred Hotels and may increase the city rate caps by a maximum of 15%. 

Agencies may decide to set more restrictive criteria with regards to eligible population and travel classes if appropriate. 



## 6 Travel Booking

### 6.1 Air Travel

All air travel must be: 

Booked via WPP’s designated Travel Management Company where applicable. 

Where an online booking tool is available, this system must be used for all simple trips. 

Training resources are available within the online booking tools. 

Contact details of the WPP's designated Travel Management Company can be downloaded  

Booked at least 14 days in advance of the departure date where possible. 

Booked using the lowest logical fare available at the time of booking in the applicable cabin class. 

The lowest logical fare is defined as: 

A restricted fare which is non-refundable and permits changes for a fee. 

Domestic flights: within +/- 2 hour of requested departure / arrival time. 

International flights: within +/- 4 hours of requested departure / arrival time. 


### Permitted Class of Travel

|  | Flight Duration | Class of Travel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior Management Travel Group  (SMTG) | All Flights | Business Class |
| All Employees | Less than 6 hours | Economy |
| All Employees | Between 6 and 10 hours | Premium Economy |
| All Employees | More than 10 hours | Business Class |


Flight duration is defined as the actual flight time excluding connecting time. 

If the policy allowed class of travel is not offered or available, the next lower class of service should be booked. 

### Employee Wellbeing

Employees travelling on long-haul (over 6 hours) or overnight in economy / coach flights should consider their wellbeing when scheduling their work plans. 

For the safety of employees, it is WPP’s strong recommendation that employees do not drive immediately after a long-haul flight. If arriving from an economy or premium economy long-haul flight, employees should use a taxi service or other form of transport instead of driving to reach their destination. 


### Exception to Policy – Class of Travel

#### Medical Exceptions

When there is a requirement to fly outside of Company policy on medical grounds the employee’s approving manager should obtain approval from their Head of People. 

Medical exceptions will be granted on a trip-by-trip basis. A medical certificate stating that the employee requesting the exception is fit to travel and that their condition dictates travel in the requested class should be sent separately to the relevant People team. 


### Business Exceptions

When there is a requirement to fly outside of Company policy on business grounds the employee should secure pre-approval from their CFO and provide the following information: 

Date of Travel 

Route 

Your permitted class of travel & the indicative cost 

Your requested class of travel and the indicative cost 

Business case for the requested class of travel 


### Non-Commercial / Charter Flights

Booking any non-commercial flights including charter requires pre-approval even if it is for a client request. WPP insurance policies have specific provisions and exclusions in such cases. If there is a requirement to book a non-commercial or charter flight, first seek approval from your Global CFO. Additionally, WPP Global CFO and WPP Risk Manager approvals are required. 

If approved, then it should be booked through the WPP designated Travel Management Company where applicable. 


### Frequent Flyer Programmes

All WPP employees are entitled to join frequent flyer programmes and accumulate mileage and status points. 

It is permissible to use airline status (or points) for upgrading flights, providing no additional costs to WPP are incurred. 

The purchase of a higher fare than required to upgrade is not permitted. 

In some countries this can be considered a taxable benefit. The employee should ensure they are accountable for any tax obligations. If the employee acquires any benefits during business travels using loyalty programs and then utilizes them for private purposes, it is the employee’s responsibility to pay tax on any non-cash benefits that may arise. 


### Compensation for Delays

Employees are entitled to claim and retain any compensation that they may receive as a result of Airline delays if this does not result in any additional cost to WPP. 

Employees may also elect to be voluntarily offloaded from an aircraft when an Airline has overbooked if this does not impact WPP financially or materially i.e., meetings, deadlines or working hours must be reorganised or missed as a result.

##### 6.5 Personal Travel

If you wish to combine business and personal travel, you should gain preapproval from your manager prior to booking. If approved, it is permissible providing: 

The primary purpose for the trip is business.

Personal travel does not interfere with the business objectives.

## 7 Managing Expenses

### 7.1 Approving Manager Responsibility

All approving managers are responsible and accountable for ensuring: 

Travel undertaken and costs incurred are necessary, all other alternatives have been considered and the travel policy has been adhered to in all aspects. 

Exceptions to the policy have been pre-approved in advance of travel by following the correct exception process. 

Pre-trip approval protocols have been followed. 

Expenses fall within their delegation of authority for approval. 

All employees familiarise themselves with this policy and associated procedures before incurring any expenses. 

There is a valid receipt to support each expense and that all expenses are coded to the correct expense type where applicable. 

Ensuring all approvals are managed on a timely basis to avoid late fees on corporate credit cards or delays in employee reimbursements. 

That the Agency Finance team is notified immediately by the People team when an employee is leaving WPP and that all expenses are submitted and approved before the employee’s last working day with WPP. 

Corporate cards are returned during the employees Exit Interview and should be destroyed. 

The approving manager should notify the People, Finance, and IT teams to ensure the employee is deactivated from all systems. 


### 7.2 Employee Responsibility

Employees are responsible for: 

Following the rules, guidelines, and procedures within this policy. 

The travel undertaken and costs incurred are necessary and all other alternatives including video collaboration technology have been considered. 

Ensuring any exceptions to the policy have been pre-approved in advance of travel by following the correct exception process. 

Exercising proper business judgement and diligence when incurring T&E related expenses. 

Charging all T&E costs to an individual corporate card where possible. The corporate card is to be used for T&E expenses only. 

Complying with the terms of the WPP corporate card which they agreed to when applying for a corporate card i.e., using the card for WPP approved purchases only. 

Improper use of the card may result in disciplinary action and WPP has the right to deduct from the employee’s salary any purchases which are outside of policy. WPP has the right to collect any amounts owed by the employee even if they are no longer employed by WPP and WPP may terminate the use of an employee’s corporate card at any time and for any reason. 

Submitting expenses in a timely manner aligned with the Agency’s process. Expenses outside of this time period will not be reimbursed unless approved by the CFO. 

‘Out of Pocket Expenses’ (E.g., cash and personal credit card expenses) and corporate credit card expenses should be submitted in the same expense report. 

Ensuring that all charges in the corporate card are paid to American Express within 25 days of the closing of the monthly statement. Charges not paid within a 60-day period will result in suspension of the employee’s corporate credit card). 

Coding all expense items to the appropriate expense type. 

Client reimbursable expenses – make sure they are coded as “billable” and are in adherence to the client’s T&E policy. 

Ensuring all charges are supported by a valid detailed receipt (following local legal/tax requirements) which shows tax details and items purchased. Credit card slips are not acceptable on their own unless they contain all details. 

Corporate cards should not be used for personal expenses. If corporate cards are used in error the costs must be repaid (paid to the Card provider directly). 


### 7.3 Using Personal Vehicle and Claiming Mileage

When employees are using their own vehicles for business it should be pre- approved by their line manager. The employee is responsible for ensuring they: 

Have a valid driver’s license. 

Have adequate personal vehicle insurance that covers them when using the vehicle for business purposes. 

The mileage rate employees can claim for using personal vehicles is published locally. 

Car mileage is claimed for travelling on business and can be claimed from your place of work or home complying to local tax requirements. 

Car mileage is calculated by deducting your regular commute from the mileage claim. 

### 7.4 Daily Meals, Subsistence & Incidental costs when Travelling

When travelling on company business, it is the policy of WPP to reimburse all costs related to food & beverage while traveling, provided that such expenses are reasonable / appropriate and do not exceed the Daily Meal caps. 

This is for the purpose of individual subsistence and is not related to entertainment or meetings, see sections 7.7 and 7.8 below. 


### Daily Meal Caps

WPP has a published list of Daily Meal Caps (inclusive of tax) by city, which can be found in Appendix A of this document. 

Mini Bar 

The use of the mini bar is permitted however any associated charges constitute part of the daily Meal Cap. 


### Group Meals

When two or more employees travelling on business dine together, the most senior member of the party must pay for the meals. The employees that were present should then be listed as part of the expense claim process. These meals are subject to the Daily Meal Cap. 


### Tips and Gratuities

Tips and gratuities are allowable expenses and should be in line with cultural norms. Excessive tipping should be supported with a full explanation on the employee’s expense report and may become a personal liability. Note: It is required that tips and gratuities are shown as separate items on an employee’s expense report. 


### Other Hotel Expenses

Hotel Health Club / SPA Charges 

The use of a hotel gym is usually free for guests. If there is a charge attached to this, it is a permissible expense. Charges for the use of a non-hotel gym, personal trainer or spa treatments are not permitted. 

Pay TV and In-room Entertainment 

These charges are not permitted. 

WIFI 

Travellers should connect to WPP Free Hotel WIFI where available. 

Hotel Internet Charges 

Permitted for business use and discretion is required for the time periods charged i.e., 1 hour vs. 24 hours. 

Laundry and Dry Cleaning 

The reasonable use of dry cleaning or laundry is permitted if travelling for four or more consecutive nights. Pressing services are permitted. 

Hotel Parking 

WPP will reimburse parking costs incurred from staying overnight or attending meetings at the property via the normal T&E expense process. 

Cash Advances from Hotels 

WPP does not permit obtaining cash advances from hotels using a corporate card. 



#### 7.5 Gifts and Entertainment

Our code of conduct states that we will not offer any items of personal inducement to secure business. This is not intended to prohibit appropriate entertainment or the making of occasional gifts of minor value unless the client has a policy that restricts it. 

Offices should set a local currency limit on the normally acceptable value of gifts and entertainment however this should not be higher than US $200 (or local equivalent) per person but will usually be lower. 

Please check if there is a specific local office Gifts and Hospitality Policy, which specifically addresses the following. If not, please use WPP policies: 

▪ 	All gifts above the local policy threshold will require pre-approval following the Agency’s controls framework. 

	▪ 	All entertainment activity should have a business purpose. 

▪ Expenses will not be reimbursed for venues deemed inappropriate for business-related matters, such as adult entertainment venues. 

▪ Planned activities with a per-head cost above the local policy threshold will require pre-approval. 

	▪ 	The most senior company employee present is required to pay. 

▪ All gifts and entertainment above the local policy threshold will be recorded on the local office Gift Register. 

▪ The following information should be included in the expense report: the full name(s), title(s), company name(s) of attendees and the business purpose. 


#### 7.6 Memberships for the Purpose of Entertaining

Employees may not submit as a business expense the joining or annual fees for a golf / sports / country or private members club, even if this membership is to be used for the purpose of business entertaining without the express written authorisation of the Agency Global CEO. 

#### 7.7 Staff Entertaining

Business meals and entertaining expenses incurred solely for the benefit of WPP employees should be rare and require a full justification within the expense report. 

- For any team / staff dinners the daily Meal Cap should be applied. These can be found in Appendix A of this document.

The total cost for these events must be paid for by the most senior WPP employee in attendance and full documentation is to be provided including receipts and the names of the attendees in the expense report along with the purpose of the entertainment. 

Companywide events are not considered staff entertaining and should follow each Agency’s approved AP Process. 


#### 7.8 Client Entertaining

We trust our employees to use their best professional judgement regarding the location, attendees, and cost for client events. 


Please make sure you are familiar with the client’s own policies on receiving entertainment and do not offer entertainment that would breach their own policies.

Baggage Handling

In-flight Wi-Fi for business purposes only 

Reasonable telephone calls 

Parking (but not parking fines) 

Toll charges 



## 9 Non-Allowable Expenses

Employees should use their judgement in assessing whether an expense is claimable and wholly necessary in the execution of their business duties. Some examples of items not permitted are shown below however this is not an exhaustive list. 

Please also refer to the General Expenses below for further guidance on expenses outside of travel. 

Airport Business Lounges 

WPP does not reimburse membership fees for utilising airline or airport business lounges. 

Additional Travel Insurance 

WPP provides insurance to all employees of any organisation in which WPP maintains more than a 50% ownership. Should additional insurance be required due to the nature or destination of the travel, employees should contact for advice. 

Luggage and Seasonal Clothing 

Luggage and seasonal clothing for business trips are considered personal items and will not be reimbursed. 

Travelling with Family / Friends 

WPP understands that employees may wish to take family / friends with them when they travel however this should not result in additional costs for WPP e.g. meal allowance should be used for an employee only, not family / friends. 


# 10 General Expenses

Only business needs costs can be expensed.

WPP provides employees with all required technology for fulfilling their job responsibilities.

### 10.2 Gifts for Employees

Gifts for employees are not normally reimbursable except in special circumstances and subject to Department Head approval. 

### 10.3 Subscriptions

WPP does not reimburse any subscription services to media or data sources. Employees can access corporate subscriptions where contracted.

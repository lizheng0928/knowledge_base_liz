POLICY
Any agency considering work with a foreign (non-US) government or non-US entity or person (known as a “Foreign
Principal”) with the goal of influencing the US market or the US government, or promoting the economic or political
interests of a foreign country or political party within the United States, must complete a FARA assessment and filing in
partnership with WPP Legal.
FARA stands for the Foreign Agents Registration Act. It’s a US federal law enforced by the US Department of Justice
(DOJ), and is meant to establish transparency and disclosure regarding US companies and citizens acting on behalf of
foreign governments or interests. FARA was originally enacted in 1938 as a legislative response to foreign propaganda
activities in the US preceding World War II.
Given that FARA is primarily focused on foreign governmental interests, most of the services WPP companies provide
for foreign clients would qualify for what is known as the “Commercial Exemption” and would not require
registration under FARA. However, even work for foreign commercial entities can require registration and it is vital that
each opportunity be properly assessed to maintain compliance with the regulation. Most of our FARA filings are for
clients that are partially or entirely state-owned, like tourism boards.
So that WPP Legal can assess FARA filing requirements, agencies must send any potential FARA engagements to their
WPP Legal partner and to commerciallegalandbusinessaffairs@wpp.com prior to entering into an agreement or
commencing work.

EXEMPTIONS
• FARA provides limited exemptions from its registration requirements. Three of the most commonly invoked
exemptions are described in greater detail below.
• Commercial Exemption: An agent who engages only in private and non-political activities in furtherance of the
bona fide trade or commerce of the foreign principal is exempt from registration under FARA, provided that such
activities do not predominantly serve a foreign interest. This exemption is not available where the activities
directly promote the public or political interests of a foreign government or political party. Most of the services
WPP companies provide for foreign clients would qualify for this “Commercial Exemption” and would not require
registration under FARA.
• LDA Exemption: An agent who is already properly registered under the Lobbying Disclosure Act of 1995 (LDA) is
exempt from registration under FARA if: (i) the agent has engaged in lobbying activities as defined by the LDA,
and (ii) the representation is not on behalf of, and will not principally benefit, a foreign government or foreign
political party.
• Religious, Scholastic, or Scientific Pursuits Exemption: An agent who engages only in activities in furtherance of
bona fide religious, scholastic, academic or scientific pursuits or of the fine arts is exempt from registration under
FARA.

EXAMPLES
• Agency is engaged to create a campaign for the Tourism Board of Japan whereby the promotional materials are
published to the US market, influencing the US population to visit Japan. You must register the work under FARA.
• Agency will create and implement an influencer marketing campaign promoting Indian gems and jewelry in the US
on behalf of your client GJEPC (Gem and Jewelry Export Promotion Council). GJEPC is an organization set up by
the Government of India to promote the Indian gem and jewelry industry and its products. You must register the
work under FARA.
• Agency will create and implement a media campaign to help facilitate more trade and commerce between your
client, the Government of Greece, and the US government. Your agency’s proposed media campaign will include
data research, investment promotion, advertisement and US government lobbying. You must register the work
under FARA.
• Agency will provide strategic counsel, media relations and communications that will extend to the US market on
behalf of your client, a food manufacturer. Your client originally incorporated in the US and the US entity has
created an affiliate in India. Since your client is a private foreign company engaging in bona fide trade or
commerce, with the commodity being its food products, the services fit within the commercial exemption and
should not require FARA registration.
• Agency will lobby Members of Congress about the benefits of krill oil and seeking to have it added to military
cafeterias on behalf of your client, Aker BioMarine, a Norwegian-based biotech company specialized in krill
harvesting and processing. While Aker BioMarine is a foreign entity, FARA registration is not required because
your agency will instead register this work under the LDA. You must register under the LDA.

FARA ASSESSMENT AND REGISTRATION PROCESS
1. Assess: Work with WPP Legal to assess any engagements with a Foreign Principal to
determine if FARA registration is required.
2. File: If the work falls under FARA, work with WPP Legal to gather the required
information quickly and file prior to the start of any work (regardless of whether a
contract has been executed or compensation has been received).
3. Bi-Annual Review: Review each FARA client engagement every six-months (or
sooner if there is a change in the relationship) with WPP Legal to supplement the
filings, as required by law.
4. File Informational Materials: Agencies must notify WPP Legal before disseminating
any Informational Materials that will be sent to two or more people on behalf of the
Foreign Principal. “Informational Materials” can include letters, press releases, fact
sheets, e-mails, and any other communications sent to two or more persons on behalf
of the Foreign Principal. These materials must include language disclosing the
agency’s relationship with the Foreign Principal and be filed with the DOJ within 48
hours of dissemination.
5. Notify of Changes: Agencies must (1) notify WPP Legal of any changes to FARA
work (e.g. change in staff, change in scope, or termination) and (2) ensure that all
client work complies with WPP policies, including a client assignment acceptance
review before any work is accepted or begins. See the WPP Client Acceptance
Policy here.

IMPORTANT FARA DEADLINES
Registration
• Required within ten (10) days of entering into an contract with a Foreign Principal. No work may begin prior to FARA
registration.
Supplemental Statement
• Due every six (6) months: within 30 days of the six (6) month anniversary of the initial FARA registration statement
date.
Informational Materials
• Must be labelled with a Conspicuous Statement disclosing the agency’s relationship to the Foreign Principal and filed
with the DOJ within 48 hours of dissemination. (e.g. This material is distributed by (name of registrant) on behalf of
(name of foreign principal). Additional information is available at the Department of Justice, Washington, DC.’)
Ongoing:
• Any amendments made to the contract with the Foreign Principal must be filed with the DOJ within 10 days after the
amendment(s) between the parties is executed. Contact WPP Legal immediately upon entering into an amended
agreement with a Foreign Principal or the termination of the agency relationship.
• FARA requires registered foreign agents to maintain all records for three (3) years following the termination of
registration for a particular Foreign Principal. Records include, but are not limited to: copies of all contracts with the
Foreign Principal, written communications to and from the Foreign Principal, and names and addresses of parties to
whom informational materials have been transmitted. Contact WPP Legal with questions about recordkeeping
requirements and procedures.

# 1．Purpose目的

In order to further improve the contractual legal awareness and contract management efficiency of each department in the company, in combination with the company’s current operation, this SOP is specially formulated aiming to regulate the company’s management process for various contracts, to promote the company’s ability of risk prevention and control.

为进一步提高公司各部门的合同法律意识和合同管理效率，结合公司目前的实际运作情况，特制定本规程用于规范公司各类合同管理的流程，以提升公司的风险防控能力。


# 2．Scope范围

All departments and their employees that managed by GroupM China shall abide by relevant provisions of this SOP in the process of negotiating, signing, implementing, filing and managing all sorts of contracts and related legal documents, which mainly cover three sectors: clients, vendors and human resources.

所有群邑中国管理范围内的部门及其员工在磋商、签署、执行、归档、管理所有类型的合同及相关法律文件的过程中均应遵守本规程的相关规定，主要涵盖客户、供应商及人事三大板块。

# 4．Principle原则


4.1	As for client contracts and vendor contracts, the parties signing contract shall be consistent with the entity that issues the invoice and the entity that receives the invoice.

就客户合同以及供应商合同而言，各签订方和发票开具或接收方应该保持一致。


4.2 	Under normal circumstances, for one single service project, only one contract shall be signed with a certain contractual party. In principle, splitting contract to avoid more senior approvals is not allowed.

一般情况下，因一个服务项目与一个合同相对方应只签署一份合同，原则上不可为了绕开更高级的审批而人为拆分合同。


4.3	Various levels of approvals are involved in the whole process of contract signing and implementation, etc. Each approver shall fulfill the corresponding responsibility for review within the scope of his/her duties and shall review not only the application form to be signed but also all of the attached supporting materials, to endeavor to protect the company’s interests and avoid risks, no matter such approval is made by signature or by email. 

合同签订及执行等整个流程在不同阶段会涉及多项不同级别的审批，无论审批是以签字或者邮件何种形式，每个审批人在自己职责范围内应尽到相应的审核责任，不仅审阅签字表格内容，也要核对所有附件资料，尽可能保护公司利益，规避风险。

As a matter of principle, a formal client contract shall be signed with the client prior to the commencement of the services. - 非续签客户合同或对前客户合同的法律条款做出实质性关键变更（包括但不限于责任限制、审计、数据安全等）的；且

- A client contract for a single project whose amount is not less than RMB 100 million or a client framework contract under which the estimated annual media spending is not less than RMB100 million.

- 客户单项合同标的或预计年度投放量不低于人民币1亿元的客户框架合同。

- 涉及要求群邑中国对WPP中国其他关联方或群邑其他国家或地区市场（即非中国大陆地区）做出承诺的；

- Any modification to GroupM’s standard audit clauses;

- For client contracts that shall be reviewed by Legal Department, based on client contracts reviewed by Legal Department, Commercial Department shall cooperate with Business Department to integrate and further analyze the review comments of relevant departments on the client contracts, summarize in writing all major amendments finally confirmed by the client to key terms (including but not limited to limitation to liability, indemnification, acceptance and payment terms, etc.) therein, and send to relevant business, financial and legal reviewers by e-mail to ensure the overall legality, strictness and logical consistency of a client contract. In case special approvals are required, Commercial Department shall make sure that all approvals have been obtained. The specific requirements shall be subject to the provisions of Client Management SOP.

- 针对应当经法务部审核的客户合同，商务部应当协同业务部在法务部审核客户合同基础上，整合及进一步分析有关部门对客户合同的审核意见，就其中核心条款（包括但不限于责任限制， 赔偿，付款验收等）客户最终确认的重大修订汇总成文，并以电子邮件形式发送至相关业务、财务及法务相关审阅人，确保一份客户合同总体上的合法性、严谨性和逻辑一致性；但凡需要特殊批准的情形，商务部需确保已获得全部批准，具体要求以客户管理规程之规定为准；

- For other client contracts that are not required to be reviewed by Legal Department, Commercial Department shall coordinate with the related Business Department to integrate and further analyze the review comments of relevant departments on the client contracts, and shall ensure the overall logical consistency of a client contract;

- 对于其他无需法务部审核的客户合同，应当由商务部协同相关业务部门整合及进一步分析有关部门对客户合同的审核意见，并确保一份客户合同总体上的逻辑一致性；

- The final decision on the terms of client contracts rests with Business Department and Commercial Department.

- 客户合同条款的最终决定权在于业务部门及商务部。

- After the review comments from various departments are integrated, it shall enter into the stage of negotiating the client contract terms with the client. Business Department shall be in charge of this stage, with support of Commercial Department and Legal Department if necessary. After the negotiation of client contract, in case another round of review of client contract is needed in line of the negotiation content, the aforementioned process of "Review of Client Contracts" and "Consolidation and Summary of Review Comments" will be repeated.

- 各部门审核意见整合后进入到与客户进行客户合同条款磋商谈判阶段，该阶段由业务部门负责，必要时，商务部和法务部提供支持。如客户合同谈判结束后，需要根据谈判内容进一步对客户合同进行审核的，则将重复上述“客户合同审核”和“审核意见整合汇总”的工作。


- Approval and Signing of Client Contracts客户合同的审批与签署

- After the parties of client contract reach a preliminary agreement through negotiation, the client contract enters the stage of approval and signing. Business Department shall, according to the category of the client contract and the requirements on the Application Form for Seal as applicable at that time, submit the client contract to each key approver for their approval and signature, and then arrange to sign and stamp the contract.

- 客户合同双方经过磋商初步达成一致意见后，进入到客户合同审批与签署阶段。业务部门根据客户合同的类别按照当时适用的盖章申请表上的要求，递交各关键审批人进行审批签字后，安排合同签署盖章。

- Prior to stamping the client contract, the final version shall be proofread by Commercial Department, in order to ensure the consistency among the version agreed upon by the parties, the version approved by the key approvers and the version to be stamped;

- 客户合同盖章之前，应当由商务部负责最终签署版本的校对，确保此前双方协商达成一致的版本、通过各关键审批人审批的版本以及盖章的版本完全一致；

- The client contract shall be stamped at the signature place and the cross-page seal shall be affixed. The first approver in the stamp and signature part under Application Form for Seal on Contract shall sign his/her name at the signature of the client contract. The requirements of specific signatories shall be referred to the relevant requirements of Client Management SOP.

- 客户合同需在落款处盖章，同时需加盖骑缝章。合同盖章申请表中的盖章签字处第一批准人在客户合同签字处手签姓名。具体签字人规定参见客户管理规程相关要求。


- Filing of Client Contracts客户合同的归档

- The original client contracts/legal documents which have been stamped by both parties shall be filed at the Commercial Department of the corresponding agency. Filing documents shall be complete and be kept for a long time.

- 完成双方盖章的客户合同/法律文件原件应在相应的代理公司的商务部归档留存。归档的文件应完整，并应长期保存。

5.3 HR Contracts Signing Process 人事合同签署流程


5.3.1 Examination Before Signing HR Contracts人事合同签订前的审查

Prior to the establishment of labor relationship/service relationship or entering into a labor contract/service agreement/internship agreement and auxiliary documents thereof with an Employee, HR Department shall review the identity information and other necessary information of the Employee, and keep relevant records of review. The specific contents which need to be reviewed include:

在与被雇佣者建立劳动关系/劳务关系或签订劳动合同/服务协议/实习协议及其配套文件之前，人事部门应先对被雇佣者身份信息及相关必要信息进行审核并留存相关审核记录，具体审核的内容诸如：

- Identity certificate, domicile certificate, academic certificate, health certificate, severance certificate, qualification certificate (depending on the position) and other necessary information of the Employee;

- 被雇佣者的身份证明、户籍证明、学历证明、健康证明、离职证明、资格证书（视不同岗位而定）等必要的信息；

- Other background investigation information (where necessary).

- 其他背景调查的情况（如需）。

Only after the Employee has passed the above review procedures, the company is able to establish the labor relationship/service relationship or sign the labor contract/service agreement/internship agreement and auxiliary documents thereof with such Employee.

在被雇佣者通过上述审核程序后，公司方可与该被雇佣者建立劳动关系/劳务关系或签订劳动合同/服务协议/实习协议及配套文件。

- 适用不同工时制度的员工应适用不同的劳动合同模板。

- 存在涉嫌违约的情况；

- Where a non-media vendor makes a complaint or provides clear and directional evidence;

- 非媒介供应商提出了投诉意见或出具了明确的有指向性的证据；

- Where a relevant client or other relevant party files a complaint or sends an official letter/lawyer’s letter;

- 相关的客户或其他相关主体提出了投诉或发出了公函/律师函；

- Where a third party claims that there is an infringement in the materials provided by GroupM;

- 第三方主张群邑提供的物料存在侵权；

- Where a third party claims that there is an infringement in the deliverables provided by non-media vendor;

- 第三方主张非媒介供应商提供的交付物存在侵权；

- Where non-media vendor’s operation status changes;

- 非媒介供应商经营状况发生变化；

- Other special circumstances under which the relevant department demanding the purchase believes that consulting Legal Department is necessary.

- 其他相关采购需求部门认为有必要咨询法务部的特殊情况。


6.3 Implementation of HR Contract人事合同的执行


6.3.1 Implementation and Supervision during Normal Performance of HR Contracts人事合同正常履行过程中的执行与监督

- Important documents and materials (including offline and online) that are required to be stored routinely during the performance of HR Contracts:

- 人事合同履约过程中日常需要备存的重要文件资料（含线下和线上）：

- Records of daily attendance, leave application and approval;

- 日常考勤、休假申请及审批的记录；

- Target-setting, and feedback from Employees;

- 工作目标的设定及被雇佣者的反馈；

- Results of performance review and feedback from Employees;

- 绩效考核结果及被雇佣者的反馈；

- Documents and materials on offer, becoming a regular member, employment, salary adjustment, grading, retaining the job but suspending the salary, resignation, severance, retirement, death, pension, resettlement, etc.,

- 人员录用、转正、聘任、调资、定级、停薪留职、辞职、离职、离退休、死亡、抚恤、安置等文件材料。

- It is recommended that the above materials should be kept by HR Department for a long term, unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations.

- 上述资料建议由人事部门长期保存，但法律法规另有规定的除外。

- Renewal and termination of labor contracts劳动合同的续签与终止

- HR Department shall keep electronic filing management of the basic information of labor contracts (including but not limited to the term, etc.) and laborers' basic information (including but not limited to working years and the number of consecutive labor contracts signed, etc.), confirm the status of expiring labor contracts on a real-time basis and handle the matters such as renewal/termination of such contracts, etc. in accordance with the corresponding advance notice period;

- 人事部门应对劳动合同的基本信息（包括但不仅限于期限等）、劳动者的基本信息（包括但不仅限于工作年限、连续订立劳动合同次数等）进行电子建档管理，实时确认临期劳动合同的情况，并按相应的提前通知期处理续签/终止等相关事宜；

- HR Department shall conduct electronic file management for the laborers approaching the age of retirement, confirm the situation of the laborers approaching retirement on a real-time basis, inform the laborers to terminate the labor contract in a timely manner and handle the retirement formalities.

- 人事部门应对即将到达退休年龄的劳动者进行电子建档管理，实时确认临退休劳动者的情况，及时通知劳动者终止劳动合同，并办理退休手续。

- Internship agreements实习协议

- HR Department shall communicate with interns in advance and confirm the details of the internship agreements.

- 人事部门应提前与实习生沟通并确认实习协议细节。

- Management of personal information个人信息的管理

- HR Department shall use, manage, store and process the personal information of Employees strictly in accordance with the requirements of letter of consent.

- 人事部门应严格按照授权书的要求使用、管理、储存、处理被雇佣者的个人信息。


6.3.2 Implementation of HR Contracts under Special Circumstances特殊情况下人事合同的执行

- Amendment or termination of HR Contracts人事合同变更或解除

- In case the HR Contract is amended during the performance, or needs to be prematurely terminated, relevant supplementary agreement/severance agreement and other legal documents shall be signed in a timely manner. For details, please refer to the procedures in Article 5.3.2 of this SOP.

- 如人事合同在履行过程中发生变更，或需要提前解除的，应当及时签署相关补充协议/离职协议等法律文件。具体可参照本规程第5.3.2条各项流程来进行。

- In case of any circumstances under which HR Department believes that consulting Legal Department is necessary, HR Department shall timely consult Legal Department.

- 如出现人事部门认为有必要咨询法务部的情况，则人事部门应当及时咨询法务部。


6.3.3 Handling Severance Formalities离职手续的办理

- HR Department shall handle formalities and process relevant documents for severance for an Employee in a timely manner;

- 人事部门应当及时与被雇佣者办理离职相关手续及文件；

- The employing department shall make arrangements for work handover, cancel the system account and require the Employee to return all materials related to GroupM in a timely manner;

- 用人部门应当及时安排办理工作交接、注销系统账号、要求被雇佣者归还所有与群邑相关的资料；

- HR Department shall confirm with the Employee the status of handover in a timely manner and require the Employee to sign the handover checklist.

- 人事部门应及时与被雇佣者确认交接情况并要求被雇佣者签署交接清单。

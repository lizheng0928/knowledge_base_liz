**Reporting a violation of WPP’s Code of ****Business Conduct and policies**** **

WPP employees who experience or hear about any behaviour which goes against WPP policies or consider there has been a violation of WPP’s Code of Business Conduct are encouraged to report their concerns promptly to their line manager, other managers or directly to their local People / HR lead. 

It is understood that sometimes it will not be possible to use these reporting channels, in which case WPP provides ahelpline which allows anyone, whether employees, freelancers or consultants to raise their concerns confidentially and, if they prefer, anonymously. Senior Management are responsible for ensuring that all employees are aware of the Right to Speak helpline. Employees also have the option of speaking to a member of WPP Business Integrity. 

All concerns raised will be investigated and no retaliatory behaviour against staff making bona fide reports will be tolerated. If the result of an investigation indicates that corrective action is called for, such action may include disciplinary measures up to and including termination of the employment of any individual found to be acting in breach of WPP policies. All investigations will be undertaken in accordance with WPP’s whistleblowing and investigations protocols. 



# Investigation

WPP’s People and Legal teams (as appropriate) will engage in an efficient, fair and fact-based investigation of any concerns raised about behaviour experienced or at odds with WPP’s policies. No agency, employee, agency department or WPP function is permitted to conduct any form of investigation. The steps to be taken during the investigation will vary depending on the nature of the allegations and the circumstances of the alleged offence. 

Employees may be interviewed as part of any investigation. In order to protect the integrity of the investigation, confidentiality will be maintained, as appropriate to the circumstances, throughout the investigative process. 

Employees must co-operate and provide truthful information in an investigation. Failure to cooperate and/or be truthful in an investigation may subject an employee to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment. Co-operation may include but is not limited to providing WPP with devices, passwords, and access codes. 

Upon completion of an investigation, WPP will take any necessary remedial action. 

Remedial action for employees may include, but is not limited to, verbal or written warnings, referral to formal counselling, suspension, probation, or termination of employment. WPP also reserves the right to require counselling, training, or monitoring as a condition of continued employment even if a breach or violation is not found. In addition, conduct that is unlawful may subject employees to civil, and in some cases, criminal liability. 

Nothing in this policy is to be construed as a guarantee of absolute confidentiality or intended to curtail employee rights under law to discuss work-related matters. Disclosure of information learned through any investigation will be limited to disclosures that are necessary for WPP to fulfil its legal obligation to investigate and to take prompt action to ensure behaviours and a culture in line with WPP’s Code of Conduct and Policies. 



# Non-Retaliation

WPP takes seriously all allegations of misconduct raised and recognises the need to protect individuals who raise concerns. WPP does not tolerate retaliatory behaviour against anyone who reports concerns in good faith. Equally WPP recognises the need to protect with due process and fairness those alleged to have engaged in misconduct. We strongly encourage our people to report any form of retaliation immediately. 



# Raising awareness of WPP’s Code of Business Conduct and How We Behave online training

The WPP Code of Business Conduct must be highlighted by every agency to their people, and we strongly encourage anyone experiencing or hearing about behaviour at odds with the Code to report this as per this policy, so it can be dealt with properly and support everyone being able to work in a culture of respect and integrity. Every local agency CEO must send periodic reminders, at least biannually, of the existence of the Code to all agency teams. 

In addition, all employees and freelancers engaged for four weeks or more in aggregate must annually complete WPP’s online How We Behave training and acknowledge in that training they have read and understood WPP’s Code of Business Conduct. Through this acknowledgment, all employees and any freelancers engaged for four weeks or more in aggregate will be confirming that they have read, understood and agreed WPP’s Code of Business Conduct and the terms of WPP’s policies. 

WPP will confirm timing of this training annually, with the period for completion. This is mandatory training for all our people including anyone working in WPP group companies for four weeks or more in aggregate.

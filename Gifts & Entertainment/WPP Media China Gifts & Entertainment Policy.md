# Gifts & Entertainment Policy, WPP Media China
礼品与招待政策（中国）

# Updated in September, 2026
更新于 2026年9月

**1. Purpose and Scope**
**1. ****目的与适用范围**
This Policy sets out the requirements and approval process for gifts, entertainment and hospitality in WPP Media China. It applies to all employees, agency brands, business units and support functions in China.

本政策规定了 WPP Media China 关于礼品、招待和商务款待要求和审批流程，适用于中国境内所有员工、代理品牌、业务单元及支持职能。

**2. Core Principles**
**2. ****基本原则**
Gifts and entertainment must be reasonable, transparent, for a legitimate business purpose, and compliant with applicable law, client requirements and company policy. They must never be intended to improperly influence a decision, secure business, create an obligation, or provide personal inducement.
礼品与招待必须合理透明、具有正当商业目的，并符合适用法律、客户要求及公司政策。不得以不当影响决策、获取业务、制造回报义务或形成个人利益诱因为目的。

Each employee is responsible for understanding this Policy, client restrictions and applicable law before giving or receiving any gift or entertainment. Employees must exercise judgment from both a legal and reputational perspective and should ask whether the item would appear reasonable, transparent and appropriate if disclosed internally or externally. Paying personally does not remove the compliance risk. When in doubt: Stop, Think, Consult, and seek guidance from your line manager, Finance, Legal or Risk & Controls before acting.
每位员工在赠送或接受任何礼品与招待前，均有责任了解本政策、客户限制以及适用法律。员工必须从法律和声誉两个角度审慎判断，并自问：如该事项被内部或外部披露，是否仍显得合理、透明、适当。个人垫付并不能消除合规风险。如有疑问，请遵循：停止、思考、咨询，并在采取行动前咨询直属经理、财务、法务或 Risk & Controls 团队。

**3****. Prohibited Gifts and Entertainment**
**3****. ****禁止的礼品与招待**
The following gifts and entertainment are prohibited and must not be given, offered or accepted under any circumstances:
以下礼品与招待属于禁止事项，在任何情况下均不得赠送、提供或接受：

G&E to / from government officials are considered unacceptable and not permitted;
向政府官员提供或接受自政府官员的礼品与招待均被视为不可接受，且不被允许；

Cash, including spending cash provided by the inviting party;
现金，包括邀请方提供的零用现金；

Gifts linked to volume of business with the provider;
与提供方业务量挂钩的礼品；

Items that are offered or received with an expectation of reciprocity (i.e. returning the favour) or with an intention of seeking improper advantage;
在期待互惠（即回报人情）或意图谋取不当利益的情况下提供或接受的礼品和招待；

Items which are in breach of any applicable laws or regulations;
违反任何适用法律或法规的礼品和招待；

Gift, entertainment or hospitality prohibited by the client contract;
客户合同禁止的礼品、招待或商务款待；

Items which are inappropriate or offensive;
不适当或具有冒犯性的礼品和招待；

Items, known to be prohibited by the other party’s company or by any of our clients;
已知被对方公司或任何我方客户禁止的礼品和招待；

Lavish gifts, entertainment or hospitality (e.g. car, luxury watch, holiday, etc.);
奢华的礼品、招待或商务款待（例如汽车、奢侈手表、度假等）；

Spouses are invited and paid for by the external party;
配偶受邀且费用由外部方承担的情形；

Foreign travel without the inviting party present; and
邀请方不在场的境外旅行；以及

G&E at the time / leading up to an important business decision involving the inviting party (e.g. pitch, renegotiation of contract, etc.).
在涉及邀请方的重要商业决策期间或临近该期间发生的礼品与招待（例如比稿、合同重新谈判等）。

**4****. Approval and Registration Rules**
**4****. ****审批与登记规则**
The following approval and registration rules apply to all gifts and entertainment in China:
以下审批与登记规则适用于中国地区所有礼品与招待事项：

**Threshold ****/ ****金额****阈值**
The de minimis / local threshold for gifts and entertainment is RMB 500 per person or per item. Any gift above RMB 500, and any entertainment (including business meals) above RMB 500 per person, must be recorded in the online G&E Register:  before being given, offered or accepted.
礼品与招待的最低申报/本地审批阈值为每人或每项人民币500元。任何超过人民币500元的礼品，以及任何超过人均人民币500元的招待（包括商务餐），在赠送、提供或接受前，均必须登记至在线 G&E Register:  。

**Line ****Manager**** approval and email attachment ****/ ****直属****经理审批及邮件附件**
Line Manager approval is required for gifts and entertainment above a nominal value. It is mandatory to attach the manager’s email approval in the StarCompliance system where required.
对于超过象征性价值的礼品与招待，必须取得直属经理审批；并应在需要时于 StarCompliance 系统中强制附上经理邮件审批记录。

**Required register information / ****登记必备信息**
The register entry must include at minimum: giver and recipient names, titles and company names; date; description; actual or estimated value; confirmation that the timing is not linked to a pitch or key decision; confirmation that no Government Official is involved; the name/title of the approver; and the line manager’s email approval attached in the StarCompliance system where required.
登记信息至少应包括：赠与方和接受方姓名、职务及公司名称；日期；项目描述；实际或估算价值；确认时间点不涉及比稿或关键决策；确认未涉及政府官员；审批人姓名/职务；以及在需要时于 StarCompliance 系统中附上的直线经理邮件审批记录。

**System notificatio****n, reimbursement attachment and settlement responsibility ****/ ****系统****通知、报销附件及结算****责任**
After the application is approved, the system will automatically send a notification. Approval is considered granted only after you receive the system-generated approval notification. The approval email must be printed and attached to the expense reimbursement voucher. If more than one WPP Media employee attends the same meal or event, the most senior employee present should settle and claim the expense.
申请获批后，系统会自动发送通知。只有在收到系统自动生成的批准通知后，方可视为获批。批准邮件必须打印出来并附在费用报销凭证上。如同一餐饮或活动有多名 WPP Media 员工参加，应由在场级别最高的员工统一结算并报销。

**Recordkeeping ****/ ****记录****保存**
Supporting documents and receipts / fapiaos must be retained in accordance with Finance and compliance requirements.
相关支持文件及收据/发票必须按照财务和合规要求妥善保存。

**5****. ****Other special circumstances**
**5****. ****其他特殊情形**

**Self-approval prohibited ****/ ****禁止****自我审批**
Self-approval is strictly prohibited.
严禁自我审批。

**Sporting, cultural events and recreational activities**** ****/ ****体育****赛事、文化活动和休闲娱乐**

Personnel invited to attend sporting (e.g. World Cup), cultural / social (e.g. concert, festival) and recreational activities (e.g. ski trips) must be of a suitably senior level such that the invitation is objectively considered appropriate. Note that all such activities / events will be subject to extra scrutiny.

受邀出席体育赛事（例如世界杯）、文化/社交活动（例如音乐会、节庆活动）及休闲娱乐活动（例如滑雪旅行）的人员，其职级须达到足够高的级别，以确保该邀请在客观上被视为恰当合理。所有此类活动／事件均将接受额外审查。

For all sporting, cultural or recreational activities, Agency / WPP Media must pay for any travel and accommodation. This should either be invoiced to WPP Media by the Supplier at a commercial rate or booked by the employee via the company travel center. Where for whatever reason this is not possible, WPP Media Global CFO pre-approval must be obtained.

就所有体育、文化或休闲娱乐活动而言，代理公司／WPP Media 须承担全部差旅及住宿费用。相关费用应由供应商按正常对外收费标准向 WPP Media 开具发票，或由员工通过公司差旅中心进行预订。若因任何原因无法采用上述方式，则须事先取得 WPP Media 全球首席财务官的批准。

**Cannes**** ****/**** ****戛****纳电影节**

Individuals attending Cannes are approved centrally at WPP Media Global level. The lists of approved individuals will be available to the relevant regional and global G&E approvers. Individuals outside the central list may not be allowed to attend.

参加戛纳活动的人员需由WPP媒体全球总部集中审批。获批人员名单将提供给相关区域及全球的G&E审批人。未列入中央名单的人员无法参加活动。

Only entrance tickets or accommodation of reasonable value may be accepted from vendors (subject to approvals). OpCos must cover transportation for WPP Media and Agency employees (i.e. not allowed to be paid by vendors).

仅可接受供应商提供的入场券或合理价值的住宿（需经批准）。WPP Media以及代理公司员工的公交通费用必须由公司自行承担。（即不得由供应商支付）。

**Items Received Without Prior Approval**** ****/ ****无法****事前审批的受赠项目****
**If a gift or entertainment is received from an external party and prior approval was not possible, it must be declared and submitted for approval within 7 days of receipt. Any item that is not subsequently approved must be returned in accordance with company instructions or paid for personally where appropriate. Employees should not keep excessive or non-compliant items for personal benefit.
如员工收到外部方提供的礼品或招待而无法事前取得审批，必须在收到后7天内完成登记并提交审批。任何事后未获批准的项目，必须按公司要求退回，或在适当情况下由个人自行承担费用。员工不得将过度或不合规项目留作个人受益。

**6****. Breaches, Monitoring and Support**
**6****. ****违规、监控与支持**
Breaches of this Policy may lead to rejection of reimbursement, withdrawal of approval, disciplinary action and other remedial measures. The company may review trends, exceptions and register completeness on a periodic basis. Questions should be raised to your line manager, Finance, Legal, or Risk & Controls team before any commitment is made.
违反本政策可能导致报销被拒、批准被撤销、纪律处分及其他整改措施。公司可定期对申报趋势、例外事项及登记完整性进行审查。任何疑问均应在作出承诺前向直属经理、财务、法务或 Risk & Controls 团队咨询。

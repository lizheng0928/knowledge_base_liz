
**INTRODUCTION **


# Sustainability / CO² Emissions


WPP set approved science-based emissions reduction targets to reduce scope 1 and 2 emissions by 84% by 2025. WPP need to embed and action sustainable thinking into the way we work, how we offer employee benefits and the goods and services that we procure. 

Where possible and practical our target is to have 100% of our new company car contract  to be low-emission vehicles by 2025, (e.g., 100% Electric Vehicles or Plugin Hybrid Electric Vehicles - PHEV). This target will account for the available infrastructure and EV maturity in each country. WPP will be ahead of incoming Government regulations across a number of markets aimed at phasing out petrol and diesel vehicles.  

Where an Agency has employees with company cars, the following data must be provided in October and January each year to WPP’s sustainability team (Sustainabilty@wpp.com):

| --- | --- | --- |

The data shared must include the relevant contract documentation and evidence in PDF that reconciles to the data shared.

## Section 1: Eligibility criteria


The WPP global company car policy applies to all eligible full-time and part-time employees (as specified by the inclusion of a company car benefit within their employment contract), excluding employees of contractors and temporary agencies. 


### 1.1 Requirements for participating employees

A company car is assigned based on job need or job role and eligibility is governed by the Agency/ Country benefit policy and is subject to the terms of the relevant employment contract. A company car will only be provided if it is contractually agreed upon. This policy has been designed to provide a range of cars suitable to meet both job requirements 


### 1.2 Driving licence

Eligible employees must prove that they have a valid driver’s license in the country(ies) in which they wish to drive and that they are fit to drive (i.e., do not have an underlying medical issue that would prevent them from driving safely or legally). The employee will immediately inform their Agency People team as well as their local Fleet Manager if the license is withdrawn, suspended or becomes invalid. 


If an employee breaches the above rules the employee will be held personally responsible for any damage to the vehicle and/or third parties. 


### 1.3 New starters

New starters eligible for a company car may only order a company car after successful completion of their probationary period. Until they are allowed to order a new car, they may be given (where possible) an unallocated vehicle from a limited list appropriate for their grade or, if unavailable, a hire car. Where the employee has been given an unallocated vehicle as mentioned above, the employee may at the discretion of the Agency be asked to retain this vehicle for the remainder of the current vehicle lease term. 

Unless by pre-approved exception new starters are only permitted to have a Cash Allowance or a Battery Electric Vehicle.  

## Section 2: Company car


### 2.1 Car brands

When selecting a company car, employees are strongly recommended to choose a brand of car from the Approved Manufacturing Brand list (Appendix 1). Although this selection is not mandatory, it is advised to ensure consistency and alignment with our company’s Procurement Policy. 

### 2.2 	Vehicle Power Type

For market competitiveness reasons, if a cash allowance is not appropriate, leasing a Battery Electric Vehicle should always be the first option when providing an actual vehicle is necessary. If neither a cash allowance nor a BEV is viable, an exception must be authorised to permit a different Vehicle Power Source type as set out in Table 1 (Vehicle Power Source Table). 

If an exception is being sought the local Fleet Manager (or equivalent) or relevant HR contact will reach out to the WPP Sustainability Team (sustainabilty@wpp.com), who will assess your country’s Electric Vehicle (EV) maturity. After consultation with the Sustainability team, if it is verified that the maturity level of EV infrastructure in country is mature then an exception to a BEV should not be sought. Alternatively, if your countries maturity level is low, an exception for a different Vehicle Power Source will need to be provided by the WPP Group Chief Counsel and the WPP Global Chief People Officer. 

No exceptions should be sought for leasing a car in countries deemed to have mature EV infrastructure. A list of these countries is provided in Appendix 2 and will be updated as new countries reach maturity.  

Please be advised that if the Vehicle Power Source is anything other than a BEV, the Agency could be subject to an offset charge. For the avoidance of doubt an offset charge is a fee imposed to compensate for the negative environmental impact caused by certain activities, in this case, using a vehicle that is not a Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV). This charge is typically used to fund projects that reduce carbon emissions or otherwise mitigate environmental damage, effectively "offsetting" the impact of the vehicle’s emissions.  

When selecting a vehicle, employees should consider factors such as emissions, mileage and taxable value.

## Section 3: Supply and return of company cars


All company cars will be contracted for a minimum of 36 months and a maximum of 60 months. WPP may vary this arrangement on individual mileage parameters for any individual car at point of order and may amend this Policy at any time to suit business requirements. 

### 3.1 Ordering procedure


Company cars can only be leased through a full-service lease contract, and only from the WPP Approved Suppliers (Appendix 1). Below is the process for ordering a new car. It must be followed for each individual lease requirement (with the exception of Germany): 


Employee is eligible for a Company Car as specified within their contract within the allocated budget 


Agency Fleet Manager (or equivalent) check whether there is an unallocated car available within the Agency Group. 


Only where there is no available car, either the eligible Employee or Agency Fleet Manager (or equivalent) will email each WPP Approved Supplier (Appendix 1) to request a quote for a Company Car. Additionally, they must always email Ayvens for a quote if it operates in country. 


Agency Fleet Manager (or equivalent), Agency Chief People Officer and Agency Chief Finance Officer (or other designated individual as specified within the Agency Car Fleet Policy stipulates) will approve one of the quotes 


Order is placed 


Either eligible Employee or Agency Fleet Manager email the approved Fuel Card provider as per Appendix 1 for a fuel card 


Where the company car is for an employee located in Germany, email the following address to assist with the lease of a Company Car:  fuhrpark@wpp- germany.de 


### 3.2 Delivery

At the time of delivery, the employee should check the car for damages and ensure that the specification is correct. 


In the unlikely event of any issues being identified at point of delivery, the car should not be accepted and will remain at the dealer’s premises and custody. The Fleet Manager should be informed immediately and will be responsible to deal with any car defects. 


### 3.3 End of contract period


At the end of the contract period, the employee should plan with their local Fleet Manager or local People team for the existing car to be collected on the same day, or before, as the delivery of any new car. Failure to do so may result in taxable benefit being incurred by the employee for both the existing car and new vehicle in the overlap period. 

### 3.4 Early Terminations


If the car needs to be returned to the leasing company before the contracted expiry date (early termination), the employee must first seek approval from their local Fleet Manager (or equivalent) and Agency Chief Finance Officer. Once approval is sought request an early termination of their Individual Lease Agreement from the Approved Leasing Supplier.  

Employees should only terminate a car lease early if they are leaving their employment contract. Termination charges may apply for any early termination of a car lease and be charged to the employee.    

### 3.5 Unassigned Cars

Where possible an Agency will try and internally reallocate a car before an early termination to avoid incurring any additional unavoidable cost.  


## Section 4: Care of company cars

Employees should ensure that the company car is properly maintained in a roadworthy condition and serviced in accordance with the manufacturer’s recommendations included in the vehicle’s user manual. 

Standard maintenance and repair costs are most often included in the contract with the leasing company, but these costs do not include the costs of maintenance or repair resulting from driver misuse or negligence (such as use of wrong fuel, failure to maintain sufficient oil in the car, kerbing of tyres and wheel trims). Employees will be responsible for costs associated with driver abuse and negligence. 

### 4.1 Routine maintenance and servicing


Employees must undertake regular checks recommended by the manufacturer (such as oil, water, fluid levels, tyres pressures and condition), and must arrange routine servicing and maintenance with the leasing company, including: 


Windscreen and window breakage (incl. glass sunroofs) 

Tyres 


### 4.2 Breakdown and accident management services

Employees should report any breakdown to the leasing company that provided the vehicle, as per the contact method in the contract. Where the authorised breakdown agent provides loan cars at no cost, the Employee should use these cars. Only in exceptional circumstances will WPP provide an employee with a hire car. Hires will be subject to authorisation by the relevant Agency line management. 

### 4.3 Expected condition of returned cars


Employees must take care of their Company cars. Any damage must be repaired promptly and professionally at the time of an incident. Returned cars should be in a condition consistent with age, mileage, and use (i.e., ‘fair wear and tear’). Car interiors should also be clean and thoroughly vacuumed. 

Employees should return all 

handbooks and service records, 

accessories 

tyres (if winter tyres) 

keys 

security devices 

## Section 5: Employment changes


### 5.1 Leavers


When leaving a company, employees will need to arrange for collection of the company car. Any outstanding costs and administration fees attributable to employee misuse must be paid in full to the relevant company prior to the termination date or they will be withheld from any final salary payment to the employee. If the final net pay is insufficient to cover outstanding amounts, WPP will deduct the appropriate amount from any other sum due to the employee and reserves the right to take action to recover this sum. 


## Section 6: Motoring offences / Fines


### 6.1 Compliance with law


Employees are required to comply with all relevant laws while driving on business or using a company car. 

### 6.2 Parking fines


It is the responsibility of the employee to pay parking fine tickets immediately. It is the employee’s sole responsibility to resolve any disputes with the issuing authority. 

### 6.3 Camera / Speeding fines

In the case a driver commits an offence that is caught ‘on camera’ a notification will be received from the issuing authority to the Fleet Manager. The Fleet Manager will refer the notification immediately back to the issuing authority providing them with the driver’s name and home address. It is the employee sole responsibility to resolve any disputes with the issuing authority. 


## Section 7: Fuel


### 7.1 Fuel cards


Fuel cards should only be ordered if permitted by the local car policy or employee contract, and must be sourced from the WPP approved Supplier listed in Appendix 1. If the approved Fuel Card provider does not operate within country, a fuel card can be ordered from the Car leasing company Employees should contact their Fleet Manager to arrange for the opening of the accounts. 

Fuel cards are valid within the country the car is leased and need to be used to purchase fuel, lubricants, and standard car washes. The mileage needs to be provided and logged with each transaction. 


The specified mileage or collected data on the time, place, and date as well as the fuel type and quantity of the individual refuels are made available for billing purposes to both the participating fuel card issuer and the company in the form of collective billing and evaluated, inter alia, for the control of the above regulations. 


Premium fuels (ex. Ultimate, V-Power or Excellium) may not be purchased. 

Other vehicle related costs, such as parking, tolls etc. cannot be billed via the card but through expenses only. Other costs will not be refunded unless incurred as a business-related expense, and they should be expensed through the established employee expense reimbursement process. 


## Section 8: Security

All employees are responsible for the security of their vehicles and contents. The company accepts no responsibility for the loss or damage to contents left in the company vehicles. Personal belongings should be removed from the vehicle or stowed out of sight, preferably locked in the boot. 

Personal belongings are the responsibility of the individual. Company property such as computer equipment should be removed from the vehicle and stored securely. 

WPP recommends the following: 


Ensuring all vehicle keys are kept away from the front door or open windows 

(thieves have been known to ‘hook’ the keys without entering the building). 

Locking all valuables out of sight. 

Always removing keys and locking the vehicle, even if only for a short time. - 	Selecting secure parking options.

### 9.2 Collision / Incident or Theft related damage and charges

In the event of a collision / incident the employee should: 

Check to see if you or any passengers with you were injured in the incident 

Safely get out of the way of traffic 

Call the police if the incident involves serious damage / injury or death 

Obtain information from all other drivers and witnesses involved in the incident 

Write down the incident information 

Take photographs of the car 

Create a sketch / diagram of the incident 

Schedule an appointment with your doctor 

Your local Fleet Manager should always be made aware of any collision / incident. Any damage collision / incident should be reported within 48 hours to the insurance company. 

In the event of a vehicle being deemed a total loss following a collision / incident a temporary car will be made available to the employee. 

In the event of the car being stolen a temporary car will be made available until the existing car has been confirmed stolen / not recovered (normally six weeks)

## Section 10: Foreign Travel

### 10.1 Insurance, Private and Business travel

If going abroad with a company car for either private or business use, the employee should contact their local Fleet Manager. Please be aware insurance certificates are sometimes not valid abroad. 

For private travel, the employee will be responsible to cover any additional costs required to ensure the car is insured and for adhering to the legislation in that foreign jurisdiction (warning triangles or spare bulbs) at their own expense. For business travel, the Company will be responsible for those additional costs. 


## Section 11: Health and Safety


WPP is committed to ensuring the health, safety, and welfare of its employees and as far as reasonably practicable, to providing and maintaining safe working conditions. For those opting for a company car, the cars provided are all manufactured to high safety standards. 


### 11.1 Driving safely

Follow all laws and regulations when driving at all times. 

Do not drink and drive. 

Do not drive when tired (see 11.5 below). 

If taking prescribed medication, ensure that it does not cause tiredness, affect perception, or decrease reaction times. 

Keep valuables out of sight. 

Ensure all occupants of the car wear a seatbelt. 

In adverse weather, modify driving to suit the conditions. - 	Keep doors locked at all times. 


### 11.2 Air bags and head rests

Employees driving vehicles fitted with air bags must ensure they adhere to the manufacturer’s guidelines in respect of the use of baby seats within the car. 


For your safety in the event of an accident, it is important that head restraints are set to the correct height and touching the back of your head when driving. 

### 11.3 Mobile telephones and handheld devices


You should never use a mobile phone handset whilst driving. If you must take a call, please pull over in a safe place and park. If pulling over is not possible please use Bluetooth functionalities if a call is necessary but limit the use of mobile telephones when driving. Always comply with local laws and regulations,  


### 11.4 Smoking policy


WPP operates a no smoking policy (this include e-cigarettes and vaping) in all company vehicles. This applies to all occupants. 


### 11.5 Preventing driver tiredness

Employees should avoid driving for more than four (4) hours every day. Suitable rest should be had before setting off and a break is recommended at least every two (2) hours but sooner if possible, where conditions require. 

### 11.6 Breaking down

The primary concern is to protect the driver and passengers, and then to make sure the car is in a safe position with hazard lights on to warn other road users. A breakdown recovery number should always be kept in the car.

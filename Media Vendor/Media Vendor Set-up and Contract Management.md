# Purpose目的：


The policy aimed to regulate the media vendor management process of WPP Media China, ensuring its compliance to company policy and adapting to practice.

本文档用于规范公司媒介供应商业务流程，以确保其符合公司政策并具有实际可操作性。本规程的规定应被当作业务执行的最低要求。与本规程相左的实际操作需经商务部门正式提出，通过更新本规程报管理层审批后执行。

# Definition定义：

Media vendor：Vendors provide media placement services. Media vendors consist of media owners, Digital Intermediary, Exclusive Reseller or Sales House, Geographical Aggregators, OOH Aggregators, Non-Exclusive Intermediary/Broker.

媒介供应商：指提供媒介投放服务的供应商。媒介供应商分媒体主、数字中介，独家经销商或销售机构，地理聚合商，OOH 聚合商，非独家中介/经纪人。


**Media owner:** Media is a medium for disseminating information, media owner is the certified legal entity own the medium platform.

**媒体主：**媒体是传播信息的介质，拥有媒介传播渠道平台的法人实体是媒体主。


**Digital Intermediary** ：Entity that is a participant in the digital supply chain (with exclusive or non-exclusive rights to certain inventory) including: Ad Network, DSP, Managed Service Provider, vertical aggregators, and KOL/Influencer aggregators.

**Global Scope:** Excluding intermediaries who are authorized by Large Digital Platforms* to sell their inventory (as an Exclusive Reseller or as a Non-Exclusive Broker). This category includes vendors such as Teads.

*******Large Digital Platforms** means any of: Google, Meta, TikTok, Bytedance, Baidu, Little Red Book, Bilibili, Tencent, Alibaba, Sina Weibo, Xiaohongshu, iQIYI, Snapchat, Pinterest, LinkedIn, X, and equivalent entities with a significant market share of digital spend in a specific market.

**Approved China Local Exception:** In the China market, any digital broker/intermediary that has obtained exclusive or non-exclusive authorization from any digital media platform, regardless of the platform’s size, shall not fall within the Digital Intermediary category and should be classified as an Exclusive Reseller or Non-Exclusive Intermediary/Broker, provided that it does not possess or utilize any proprietary technology, proprietary data, or independent processing capabilities.


**数字中介：**是指参与数字供应链的实体（对某些存量拥有独家或非独家权利），包括：广告网络、DSP、托管服务提供商、垂直聚合商、KOL/影响者聚合商。

**全球适用范围：**由“大型数字平台”授权出售存量的中介（以独家经销商或非独家经纪人的身份）除外。Teads等属于此类。

“**大型数字平台****”****是指**：谷歌、Meta、TikTok、字节跳动、百度、小红书、哔哩哔哩、腾讯、阿里巴巴、新浪微博、爱奇艺、Snapchat、Pinterest、领英、X，以及在特定市场上拥有大量数字支出市场份额的同等实体。

**已获批准的中国区例外适用范围：**在中国市场，凡是从任何数字媒体平台（不论该媒体平台的规模大小）获得独家或非独家授权的数字代理/中介，只要其不具备或不利用任何专有技术、专有数据或独立处理能力（即不具备构成数字中介的实质特征），则不属于本类，而应被归类为独家经销商或非独家中介/经纪人。


**Exclusive Reseller or Sales House****：**Appointed by a Media Owner to be the sole party, in the market, which is authorized to sell the inventory made available by the Media Owner. 

**Global Scope:** Includes exclusive resellers for Large Digital Platforms but excludes all other digital media resellers/sales houses, which will be captured by the definition of Digital Intermediary.

**Approved Ch****i****na Local Exception:** In the China market, this includes exclusive resellers authorized by any digital media platform, regardless of platform size, provided that they act only as an authorized sales channel and do not possess or utilize any proprietary technology, proprietary data, or independent processing capabilities.


 If an Intermediary acts exclusively for one Media Owner and non-exclusively for other Media Owners and we purchase across all represented Media Owners, then the Intermediary should be treated as a Non-Exclusive Broker in below. If we purchase only exclusively represented Media Owner inventory, then it may be treated as an Exclusive Reseller.


**独家经销商或销售机构**：指被媒体主委任为市场上唯一有权出售媒体主所提供存量的一方。

**全球适用范围：**包括“大型数字平台”的独家经销商，但“数字中介”定义所涵盖的所有其他数字媒体经销商/销售机构除外。

**已获批准的中国区例外适用范围：**在中国市场，该类别包括获得**任何**数字媒体平台（不论该平台规模大小）独家授权的经销商，前提是其仅作为销售渠道行事，且不拥有或利用任何专有技术、专有数据或独立处理能力。


如果某中介作为某媒体主的独家代理行事，对于其他媒体主属于非独家代理，在我们从所有被代理的媒体主手中购买存量的情况下，该中介应被视作下文所述的非独家经纪人。但如果我们只从被独家代理的媒体主手中购买存量，则该中介可以被视作独家经销商。


**Geographical Aggregators**: Authorised by local TV or local radio Media Owners in a specified geographical area, (e.g. a Tier 2 city or local region). Authorisation may be exclusive for some Media Owners and non-exclusive for others. 

地理聚合商:得到指定地理区域（如二线城市或当地）的地方电视台或地方广播媒体主授权。对于某些媒体主而言属于独家授权，对于其他媒体主而言属于非独家授权。


**OOH Aggregators**：Operating as a licensee of OOH multiple media spaces or authorised as a reseller of multiple individual licensees of OOH space. May represent a specific geography or type of OOH, e.g. transport OOH  

OOH聚合商：作为OOH多媒体空间的持牌商运营，或被授权为OOH空间多个个体持牌商的经销商。可能代理特定的地理位置或OOH类型，例如交通OOH


**Non-Exclusive Intermediary/Broker**：Excluding any intermediary that falls within one of the above types, and authorized by one or more Media Owners as a reseller of inventory as a legal principal.

**Global Scope:** Includes non-exclusive resellers for Large Digital Platforms.

**Approved China Local Exception:** In the China market, this includes non-exclusive authorized brokers/resellers for any digital media platform, regardless of platform size, provided that they do not possess or utilize any proprietary technology, proprietary data, or independent processing capabilities.


**非独家中介****/****经纪人**：指不属于上述任何类别，且经一个或多个媒体主授权，以合法主体身份经销媒体库存的中介。

**全球适用范围：**包括“大型数字平台”的非独家经销商。

**已获批准的中国区例外适用范围：**在中国市场，该类别包括获得**任何**数字媒体平台（不论该平台规模大小）非独家授权的代理/经销商，前提是其不拥有或利用任何专有技术、专有数据或独立处理能力。


Principle原则：


4.1 The period of media placement service starts upon campaign starts. Therefore, vendor setup process needs to be completed before campaign starts. Vendor contracts should be signed timely.

媒介供应商的媒介投放服务期间自排期上线时开始，因此供应商在排期上线前，应完成供应商入库流程。供应商合同及时签订。


4.2 Buying from media owner and principal vendor are considered as direct buy.

向媒体主或经授权的媒体主控股母、子公司的购买都视为直接购买。


4.3 The definition of authorized brokers should be extensive and may include regional or industry brokers. The default is always to buy direct from Media Owners, without Intermediaries. In order to deviate from the default position there must be a 'client-first' business reason. It is not acceptable to select an Intermediary solely because it benefits to WPP Media China. When broker is not avoidable, the reason why use the broker needs to be elaborated for each broker used in route to media. 

代理授权必须是宽泛的，可以是区域代理或者行业代理等。在使用供应商服务过程中，不通过中介而直接从媒体主处购买是默认规则，必须是出于“客户至上”的商业理由。不得仅仅因为对WPP Media中国有利而选择中介。如遇必要使用代理商的情况，需要对于购买链路中涉及的代理商逐一说明使用原因。


4.4 Brokers are limited within two layers in principle. Exclusive brokers with control or under common control are regarded at the same layer.

 代理商的层级原则上不超过两级。在代理的层级内，存在控股或者同一控制关联方关系且独家资源授权关系视为同一级代理。


4.5 Different approvals are involved in the whole vendor setup process. Each approver should be responsible for performing a diligent review before approving, no matter such approval is made in physical sign-off or by email. When necessary, one should check to the original documents, to learn substance of the business, 

供应商入库流程在不同阶段会涉及多项不同级别的审批，无论审批是以签字或者邮件何种形式，每个审批人在自己职责范围内应尽到审阅责任，必要时核对资质等文档，了解实际业务情况，避免形式审批。


# Vendor set up Process narrative供应商入库流程描述


The vendor setup process includes pitch, approval and regular review.

供应商入库流程涵盖供应商比稿、审批和定期审阅。


5.1 Direct media Owner、Exclusive Reseller or Sales House、Client appointed vendor

直接媒体、独家经销商或销售机构、客户指定供应商无需比稿。


5.2 The pre-assessment should be done before a pitch and reviewed on each participant for the pitch.	

在比稿前应对每个参加比稿的供应商进行事前评估和审查。


5.3 Broker Pitch Process  代理商比稿流程

First, a negotiator invites, by e-mail, vendors with relevant media resources to participate the pitch, such vendors are mainly recommended candidates from the Group’s current vendors and by media owner or clients.

首先谈判发出邮件邀请有相关媒体资源的供应商参与比稿，邀请供应商为集团现有供应商和媒体或者客户推荐供应商。


- If the annual expenditures on a single media placement is expected to be less than RMB10 million or involving a back-to-back broker, no Pitch Committee will be established; negotiators, the Chief Negotiator and commercial will complete the pitch scorecard and submit it to the General Manager of the Negotiation Department and the Chief Investment Officer for approval.

- 单媒体年投放量预计不到人民币1000万或风险背书代理商，不设比稿委员会，由谈判、谈判长和商务填写供应商比稿打分表(附件SOP MV10-1)，提交谈判总经理和首席投资官审批。


- For Pitch Committee should be established for pitches if the annual expenditures placement between [10 MM ~ 50 MM CNY]. The negotiation team is required to document the rationale of candidates selection and perform the preliminary assessment for final broker candidates, by using the template in appendix 10-13 [Preliminary assessment template] . The negotiation team should send notices three business days before the pitch, copying Chief Investment Officer, Chief Financial Officer, CIGO and Chief Executive Officer. The Committee members shall note down the rationale of their scoring (example in appendix 10-14) The pitch results need to be approved by Senior management (CIO, CFO, CIGO).

单次比稿在[1000万人民币~ 5000万人民币]之间的比稿，需要组建比稿委员会进行比稿评分。谈判团队需要记录选择候选人的理由，并使用附录10-13[初步评估模板]中的模板对最终候选人进行初步评估。 谈判团队在比稿日前3个工作日发出通知，且同时报备首席投资官、首席财务官、首席投资治理官和首席执行管。委员会成员应记录其评分的理由（示例见附录10-14）。比稿结果需要得到高级管理层批复。


- The broker pitching over 50 MM CNY, the framework for executing broker pitching from Jan 2025, as pilot program. Please refer to “WPP Media China-Media Management Guideline for Significant Intermediary Pitching_Bilingual_V1.1”

- 从2025年1月起对超过5000万人民币的比稿推行的试点计划的操作步骤。具体步骤请参考“WPP Media中国-Media Management-5000万人民币以上重要供应商比稿指南”(附件SOP MV10-2)进行操作。

5.9 供应商代码建立


- After the online approval process is completed, the system will automatically notify the Data Center, and relevant workers will create codes for each vendor accordingly. After the new vendor/media outlet has its code in the Minder system, a reviewer with the WPP Media China Commercial department will send relevant information by e-mail to the SSC team responsible for conducting the vendor setup process in the financial system; meanwhile the Symphony support team will be notified of providing codes for digital media vendors in the Symphony system; then the reviewer will enter the Symphony code in the Minder system。

- 线上审批流程完成后，系统会自动通知数据中心，相关人员据此建立供应商代码。新增供应商/媒体在Minder系统中建立代码后，WPP Media中国商务部审核人将相关资料邮件发送至SSC团队安排财务系统入库，同时通知Symphony支持团队为数字媒体供应商建立Symphony系统代码，然后WPP Media商务部审核人将对应的Symphony代码录入Minder系统。


- Vendor /Media code encoding rule：

- 媒介供应商编码规则如下：


- The encoding rule of Minder for vendor code is the initial letter for vendor name .

- Minder系统中媒体和供应商编码规则为媒体或供应商名称首字母组合，位数不限。


- The encoding rule of Adept for vendor code is M(initial)+ the initial letter for vendor name .

- SSC在Adept中供应商编码规则为M(开头)+ 供应商名称首字母组合，M表示Media Vendor, 位数不限。


5.10 供应商定期审阅


- Each vendor has a validity period in the system. Once the validity period expires, the vendor will be invalid and no longer be available for use. The validity period of the client-appointed vendor is established based on the broker period or specific needs. For most of the preferred vendors, the validity period will be from January 1 to December 31 of each calendar year.

- 供应商在系统中设有有效期，一旦有效期结束，该供应商就失效无法使用。客户指定供应商根据代理期间或者特定需求设定有效期，而大部分的大盘供应商有效期为自然年的1月1日到12月31日。


- After October 1 of each year, the WPP Media China Media Buying Department will carry out an annual evaluation of the preferred vendors, and the Data Center derives a list of existing non-client appointed vendors from the system. From the list, the Negotiation Department and the Commercial department will prepare a list of source vendors whose service period will be prolonged based on the intent to continue the process of collaboration. WPP Media China Data Center obtain approved vendor list by the Chief Investment Officer, the validity period of such vendors will be extended to March 31 of the following year and the extension should be completed prior to November 15 of each year.

- 每年10月1日开始，WPP Media中国媒介采购部门开始进行非客户指定供应商年度评估，由数据中心从系统内导出现有供应商名单，谈判和商务根据合作意愿筛选需要继续合作的供应商，数据中心获得WPP Media 中国首席投资官批准的延期供应商名单后,统一将供应商的有效期延长至次年3月31日，延期的动作在每年11月15日前完成。


- Starting from April 1 of each year, the Commercial department tracks the vendors whose service period has been prolonged to March 31 during which no setup information has been supplemented, and media placement has been executed, such vendors should provide the requisite setup information; if during the same period no media placement has been done, the validity period of vendors should be adjusted to the end of the previous year, to ensure that ads are placed by eligible vendors.

- 4月1日开始，商务跟踪已被批量延期至3月31日但未补充供应商入库资料的供应商，延期期间已发生投放的需补充入库资料，没有投放的供应商有效期期间调整回为上年末，以确保投放均由有相关资质的供应商执行。


- In November of each year, the Commercial department will submit to SSC a list of vendors whose service period will be renewed. SSC will carry out a sourcing from options established 24 months earlier in Adept and shut down vendors that had no media placement in the past 24 consecutive months with zero unpaid amount and are not on the list provided by the Commercial department.

- 每年11月，商务团队将需要继续使用的供应商清单提供给SSC，SSC针对Adept中24个月之前建立的供应商进行筛选，针对连续24个月没有投放且未决款项为零的供应商，且通过比对也不在商务团队提供的继续使用的清单中的，进行关闭处理。

6.1 供应商合同模板

与供应商签订媒介采购合同应使用WPP Media 中国供应商媒介采购合同模板，当单波排期执行合同标的大于等于人民币1000万元的或框架合同的预计年度投放量大于等于1亿元人民币时，涉及以下情况时商务部需要安排法务审阅，

- Amendments have been made to legal provisions of the contract signed with the renewed vendors based on the previous version;

# 7. 大盘户外制造供应商甄选Outdoor Preferred Production Vendor sourcing


The WPP Media China Outdoor Media Buying Department initially selects no less than 5 vendors based on the Group’s record of previous collaboration and industry recommendations, and invites them by e-mail to join in the pitch session. Before the pitch session, negotiators should specify the time of pitch and members of the Pitch Committee which may include the Media Buying Department Outdoor’s business negotiation team, inventory media sales team, business team, data center team, pitch team as well as the representative of the broker’s Commercial Director and above. A notice will be sent out three business days before the pitch session, and be simultaneously reported to the Chief Investment Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Executive Officer for filing. Meanwhile, the pitch manager should collect and summarize pitches of all contestants for scoring purposes. Three parts are contained in the pitch scorecard:

WPP Media 中国户外媒介采购部门根据集团过往合作以及业内推荐，初步筛选出不少于5个供应商，发出邮件邀请这些供应商参与比稿。比稿前谈判安排比稿时间、确定比稿委员会成员，可以包括媒介采购部门户外组谈判组、存量媒体销售组、商务组、数据中心组、比稿组，代理公司商务总监以上级别也可派人参加，并在比稿日前3个工作日发出通知，且同时报备首席投资官、首席财务官和首席执行官。同时谈判比稿负责人获得各个供应商比稿资料，进行汇总，以供打分。比稿打分内容涵盖三大部分，如下：


Part one: company qualifications, such as business scope and capital size. The business team will check the information of contestants through online searches and other means in addition to the documents submitted by vendors.

第一部分：公司资质，如经营范围、资本规模等。除供应商主动提供之外，还由商务团队通过网上检索等进行核实内容；


Part two: price policy, including discounts, tax rate, and payment term, etc.;

第二部分：价格政策，含价格折扣、税点、付款周期等；


Part three: company capabilities, such as business scope, production capacity, and response speed;

第三部分：公司能力，如业务范围、产能、响应速度等；


The pitch manager will summarize the scores of items based on the established weights, calculate the total score, select one contestant with the highest score, and finalize conclusion. With respect to the winning contestants, negotiators with the Outdoor Media Buying Department will carry out a site survey into their production capacity, security, and environmental protection measures. The winning contestants will go through the vendor setup process by supplying materials described in items and have a contracted service period of one year. The term of the vendor with annual service volume below RMB20 million should be renewed automatically. Vendors with an annual service volume more than the specified amount will be only used in the following year after another pitch.

比稿负责人将各项打分按既定权重进行加总，得出总分并选出评分最高供应商，形成最终的比稿评分结果文档。针对胜出的供应商，户外媒介采购谈判安排实地勘察供应商的生产能力、密保和环保措施，通过者按照媒介供应商流程提供资料入库，供应商服务期为一年，年服务体量小于人民币2000万可续用，超过体量的供应商下一年继续比稿后使用。


The client-appointed outdoor production vendors should comply with requirements of the vendor purchase process applicable for appointed outdoor media outlets. The vendor appointed by media outlets should also be included in the media purchase schedule.

客户指定户外制造供应商，遵照客户指定媒介采购供应商流程。如遇媒体指定供应商，该供应商涵盖在媒介采购排期中。

Energy saving and emission reduction, low-carbon working
⚫ Please turn off the lights and shut down projectors and TVs after meeting.
⚫ Promote paperless office practices, double-sided printing is recommended to save paper.
⚫ Set the air conditioner panel to ventilation mode when the temperature is moderate.
⚫ Set the air conditioning between 24°C and 26°C in summer season; no higher than 18°C to
21°C in winter season.
⚫ Adjust your computer screen brightness to a comfortable level to save energy and protect
your eyes; turn off in time when leaving.
⚫ Turn off faucets promptly to avoid wasting.
⚫ Save paper (including toilet paper and desk tissues) to protect the environment.

**Translation of ****WPP Media**** China Supplier on-Boarding SOP **

**Purpose and scope**

- This document is used to standardize the Company's supplier onboarding management process to ensure that it complies with the Company's policies and is practical. This Supplier Onboarding Management Procedure is applicable to all departments and employees within the management scope of WPP Media China.


- Unless otherwise specified, the requirements of this Procedure are applicable to all suppliers, and some paragraphs are applicable to certain categories of suppliers only when it is clearly specified.


**Basic principles**

- The supplier with a valid supplier code shall be a supplier approved by the Company for use. A supplier whose supplier code has not been established or whose supplier code has expired shall be an unapproved supplier. Only suppliers approved by the Company can enter into relevant service contracts and provide services. No employee can order services from an unapproved supplier, including entering into a contract, placing an order, or requiring such supplier to commence services in any form.


- The contracting party, the service provider, the invoicing party, and the beneficiary of the supplier shall be consistent and shall not be different.


- The whole process of supplier onboarding involves several approvals at different levels. Regardless of whether the approval is in the form of system, signature, or email, each approver shall fulfill the review responsibility within his/her scope of responsibility, not only reviewing the content of the application form but also checking the attached data, so as to protect the interests of the client and the Company and avoid risks during the use of supplier services.


**Supplier onboarding**

- All media suppliers are required to complete the supplier onboarding steps and can be used only after entry into effectiveness upon being updated.


- For non-media suppliers, when the annual service amount is estimated to be RMB 30,000 yuan or more (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), they may be used after completing the supplier onboarding process and establishing a supplier code. Non-media payment applications less than RMB 30,000 yuan may directly go through the one-off payment process. For details, please refer to the Non-Media Supplier Ordering Management Procedure.

Supplier onboarding process

- When there is a WPP-approved supplier for a potential service, preference will be given to using the WPP-approved supplier. The list of WPP-approved suppliers may be found by inquiry via the WPP Procurement Hub. Reference may be made to Appendix II for the scope of services currently covered by WPP-approved suppliers.


- When there is no available WPP-approved supplier, it is required to inquire and check whether there is any suitable local onboarded supplier from WPP Media's local supplier database to reduce unnecessary new supplier onboarding applications.


- When applying for a new supplier, the following basic requirements are required to be considered. If the media supplier has additional requirements, they shall be listed in the Media Supplier Management Procedure.


- Bidding assessment results or supplier designation letter from client‘s team leader.

- For bidding assessment results, it shall include original quotation communication documents and bidding scorecard, see 3.3 for details. For WPP-approved suppliers, it is not required to be provided, but a screenshot of the WPP-approved supplier query results with the system date is required to be archived.

- For the client-designated supplier, it is not required to provide the supplier designation letter or email of the person responsible for client contact, see the relevant guidelines in 3.4 below for details;

- Copy of business license affixed with the official seal of the supplier, where the registration place of the supplier may not be a tax haven.

- Bank account information affixed with the official seal of the supplier, where the account name is required to be consistent with the supplier name.

- Supporting documents searched from the Internet for reputational  checks, including adverse media checks indicating no tax fraud, tax evasion or bribery or corruption committed by the supplier. Nexis Diligence may be used for inquiring the results. Nexis Diligence account may be found and applied for through the WPP My Apps Dashboard, and the account may usually be activated within 24 hours. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- Query results in the National Enterprise Credit Publicity System, Dun & Bradstreet Credit Report, or other credit investigation systems. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- National Enterprise Credit Publicity System: http://www.gsxt.gov.cn/index.html

- Related Party Relationship Disclosure Commitment (form for suppliers) signed and affixed with the official seal of the supplier, in which case it shall be ensured that all parties that WPP Media intends to sign the contract with are included. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- Related Party Relationship Disclosure Commitment (form for supplier demand applicants and approvers), in which case signatures are required at least by the supplier applicant and his/her client team leader or department head. For media suppliers, signatures of all involved negotiations and the CIO are required.

- In the case of client-designated suppliers, all WPP Media employees and their line managers copied in the client-designated email are required to be included, and the client team leader is required to be included.

- If the reviewer finds that the supplier or applicant or approver has a declaration for association relationship during the supplier onboarding process, please suspend the onboarding process of such supplier, notify the Risk & Control department of WPP Media China in time to follow up on the subsequent declaration and approval.

- If vendor cannot provide the RPD, please obtain global approval as section 3.4 instructed below before proceeding with the supplier onboarding process .

- WPP Code of Business Conduct – Supplier Version affixed with the official seal of the supplier.  If the supplier cannot sign the WPP Code of Business Conduct, the applicant is required to fill in the WPP CBC vendor exemption application_E, provide specific business reasons for using the supplier, attach the supplier's code of business conduct (if any), and after approval by the department head and obtain global approval as section 3.4 instructed below, before proceeding with the supplier onboarding process. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- WPP Nexis Diligence may be used for inquiring sanctions. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- If the supplier contract involves the provision of media, data, Tech, or HR related services, please provide the evaluation result from Safer Data Navigator. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- In the case of an advisory supplier, the corresponding data and approval shall be provided according to the guidelines in 3.4 below. This item is not required to be provided for WPP-approved suppliers.

- In the case of a non-WPP Group creative agency, the corresponding data and approval shall be provided in accordance with the guidelines in 3.4 below.


- When applying for onboarding of non-media suppliers, the corresponding supplier service type shall be selected according to the proposed purchase business. For details, please refer to Appendix II.


Bidding and designation

- Detailed guidelines on bidding of media suppliers are set out in the Media Supplier Management Procedure.


- The following part is for non-media suppliers:


- The applicant is required to conduct a tripartite bidding assessment and summarize and compare the bidding results as one of the necessary data for the supplier onboarding application. The main determining factors of the tripartite bidding assessment are the service content and related price. Other factors that may be considered include but are not limited to the coordination of service schedules, quality assessment of previous services, smooth cooperation and communication, and other value-added services. When the contract proposed to be signed is a framework contract and does not cover the determination of the service amount, the price factors in the bidding assessment often refer to the unit price.


- The responding bidder's company name, contact name, position, telephone, and e-mail address are required to be included in the summary sheet of bidding results. The bidder's bidding communication email or quotation document, recommendation information, or previous successful case introduction shall also be attached to the summary table of bidding results.


- If the existing non-media supplier applies for adding service types across categories or the media supplier adds agency media types, it is also required to provide a new tripartite bidding assessment.


- In the case of a framework contract or fixed amount contract exceeds RMB 200,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), it is also required to provide the evaluation results of the tripartite bidding assessment.


- For the bidding assessment of a framework contract or the estimated annual purchase amount exceeding RMB 200,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses) another colleague (not the approver) with the same or higher level as the applicant is required to independently evaluate the bidding assessment and score. Appendix III provides the price comparison scoring format for reference..


Review and approval

- For supplier onboarding, it is required to check the corresponding options, upload the required data in the corresponding system, and submit it to the approver for approval according to the system settings.


- For media suppliers, it is to be checked by the commercial team and approved by the negotiation at the corresponding level, CIO, and CCO. Media agencies and client-designated suppliers with an annual advertising amount of more than RMB 2 million yuan shall be approved by the CFO. Some agencies shall be subject to approval by the regional and global CFOs, as detailed in the Media Supplier Management Procedure.


- For non-media suppliers, it is to be approved by SSC, the team leader, and WPP Media Procurement Director.


- Both the applicant and the approver are required to note that the services of the supplier can be used only after completing the supplier onboarding approval process.


- In the event of the following special circumstances, please handle them separately according to the following specific cases:


- When lacking of WPP CBC or RPD signed by supplier, please submit to Marina Maloca Marina.Maloca@wpp.com (cc Tara Rajah (WPP) Tara.Rajah@wpp.com) to obtain approval before proceeding.  In an urgent situation or need translation help, please submit to WPP Media China's Legal department, which will arrange for WPP Global legal approval before proceeding.


- When other data required for onboarding is incomplete, additional approval by the CFO is required.


- Generally, advisor payment are not allowed. The applicant is required to determine whether a supplier becomes an advisor according to the Advisor Payment Policy, and the approvers required to evaluate whether the determination is reasonable when approving the supplier onboarding, and need the approval from Commercial Director. If an advisory supplier is involved, additional due diligence may be required under the arrangement of the WPP internal audit and approved by the CEO, CFO, and WPP CEO (or WPP FD) in writing.


- When ordering creative design services from a non-WPP agency, the applicant is required to determine whether a supplier is a non-WPP agency according to the Paying Non-WPP Agencies Policy, and the approver is required to evaluate whether the determination is reasonable when approving the supplier onboarding, and need the approval from Commercial Director. If a non-WPP agency is involved, the CCO is required to conduct additional due diligence and contact the WPP Media Risk & Control department for contract review and approval. In addition, the Paying Non-WPP Agency Approval Form is required to be filled out to obtain WPP Media China's written approval, as well as regional and global written approval in accordance with the relevant policy.


- Generally, we do not accept client-designated suppliers. If there are special circumstances where the client needs to designate the supplier, the applicant is required to provide a supplier designation letter or email in accordance with the relevant terms of the client contract or from a person at a level not lower than the team leader responsible for the corresponding business. For the wording, reference may be made to <Non-media client appointment of supplier general> or <Recommended format of client appointment of media vendor> When applying for the onboarding of a supplier designated by a client, the department responsible for supplier onboarding shall also perform the supplier review steps. Once any problem with the qualifications or service capabilities thereof is found, the department shall promptly feed it back to the client and obtain the supplier risk notification letter from the client, see <Supplier Risk Notification Letter> for reference.


In the case of a client-designated supplier, please add the client-designated supplier terms to the (service) agreement of the relevant client and the (purchase) agreement of its designated supplier, as detailed in Appendix IV. If the client's master contract, project contract, spotplan, or designated email does not contain the risk aversion terms of the designated supplier, the CFO approval is required for a single project/schedule of more than RMB 2 million yuan.


- Other scenarios where a tripartite bidding assessment cannot be provided:

- Only Available Source since as Appointed Service Standards or Brand Limits;

- Fewer than three suppliers are able to participate in the bidding and provide valid quotations.


- WPP’s standard payment terms are 60 days from the date of invoice. If the supplier’s payment terms are less than 60 days in the contract application, special approval is required.


- A more detailed approval process for the onboarding of media suppliers is set out in the Media Supplier Management Procedure.


- After confirming that the use and related information of the supplier are correct, the approver shall approve the onboarding application in the system and submit it to SSC for the arrangement of onboarding. For non-media suppliers, it generally takes 3 working days from the completion of the financial review to onboarding by SSC. SSC shall backfill the number to Chorus on the same day Adept creates a new supplier code, in which case the backfill date recorded in Chorus shall be the supplier establishment date.


Supplier coding rules

SSC coding for media suppliers starts with M, and the coding rules for non-media suppliers are as follows: O + C/H/P /B + Location (S for Shanghai, G for Guangzhou, B for Beijing) + (N) + supplier abbreviation, where,

C: refers to Client Related Vendor

H: refers to In-House Vendor (non-billable)

P: refers to Production Related Vendor

B: refers to Pitch/bidding deposit party


**Supplier data change**

- If the supplier needs to update relevant information after onboarding, it is required to provide different data according to different situations, as follows:

- In case of a change to the name of the supplier, it is required to provide a copy of the updated business license and the directory name change letter issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation.

- In case of a change to the supplier's address, bank account number, contact person, and other information, it is required to provide a description of the changed information affixed with the official seal of the supplier. The supplier contact person and his/her contact information is an important way to verify the authenticity of the supplier information without error. Therefore, the applicant is required to update the supplier contact and his/her contact information in time.

- If the existing non-media supplier applies for adding service types across categories or the media supplier adds agency media types, it is required to provide a new tripartite bidding assessment.

- Upon system approval, the SSC shall arrange the modification of the supplier-related data and take effect.


- The change of non-media supplier information shall take effect after being reviewed and approved by SSC, and the team leader. If it involves the addition of service types across categories, and changes to supplier payment terms or descriptions of payment terms, the review by the Procurement Director shall be added. The review steps for media suppliers shall be equivalent to onboarding.


**Periodic review**

- The suppliers are required to be reviewed annually. The SSC master data team will screen out the list of non-media suppliers with no existing contracts in the last 15 months and send it to the finance department. The finance department will send the list to the corresponding team for review. For non-media suppliers will no longer be used as confirmed, they will be deactivated in the system. This review shall be conducted in November each year. For media suppliers, it is reviewed and re-onboarded annually.


- Suppliers established more than 6 years ago need to update their due diligence, and WPP-approved suppliers need to update their query in  WPP Procurement Hub instead. For other expired suppliers, they shall be uniformly closed in the annual review, without affecting the performance of existing contracts. If such supplier is required to be used subsequently, a new contract is required to be created, the onboarding application process is required to be updated.


**Precautions**

- The Company will update this system and relevant process on the internal website of WPP Media China. It is the responsibility of each employee to visit the website frequently to understand and abide by the latest system and procedure.

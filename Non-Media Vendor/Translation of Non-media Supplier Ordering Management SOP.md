**Translation of Non-media Supplier Ordering Management SOP**

**Purpose and scope**

- This document is used to standardize the Company's non-media purchase supplier order management business process to ensure that it complies with the Company's policies and is practical. This Supplier Management Procedure is applicable to all departments and employees within the management scope of WPP Media China.


**Basic principles**

- Only suppliers approved by the Company can enter into relevant service contracts and provide services. No employee can order services from an unapproved supplier, including entering into a contract, placing an order, or requiring such supplier to commence services in any form. To order services from approved suppliers, it is required to submit a purchase request application in advance and obtain the approval therefor.


- The contracting party, the service provider, the invoicing party, and the beneficiary shall be consistent and shall not be different.


- Since only one service contract shall be signed for one service project and one supplier, the contract may not be split to avoid higher-level approval.


- The negotiation, approval, and signing of a supplier contract involve several approvals at different levels. Regardless of whether the approval is in the form of system, signature, or email, each approver shall fulfill the review responsibility within his/her scope of responsibility, not only reviewing the content of the application form but also checking the attached data, so as to protect the interests of the client and the Company and avoid risks during the use of supplier services.


**Description of order management process**

- In this Procedure and non-media supplier management system Chorus, the amounts involved in the system are the total amounts inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses. Therefore, for all the amounts mentioned below and the amounts required to be listed in the system, the teams are required to pay attention to covering the service price and all taxes, surcharges, and other necessary expenses.


Project

- The application and approval of contracts are managed by Chorus through the method of project management. The applicant is required to set up a project as needed, set the project budget and other information, and obtain the approval of the team leader and the commercial before starting to enter into a contract under the project. The team may determine the division of projects by different dimensions such as clients and work tasks. Since budget management may be set for the project, contracts are managed at the project level, while receipt and payment are managed at the contract level, so reasonable division of projects is a key point for smooth use of Chorus.


- If a project adopts budget control, the cumulative purchase amount of the project shall not exceed the project budget. When an applicant submits a purchase request or contract application, if the sum of the cumulative purchase amount of the project exceeds the project budget, the applicant is required to complete the project budget update first. The cumulative purchase amount is the sum of the following:

- Amount of all submitted purchase request (fixed amount contracts and purchase orders not submitted yet)

- Amount of submitted fixed amount contracts, purchase orders, and settlement statements

- Amount of submitted one-off payment demand


Purchase request

- The purchase request is a step before the applicant applies for a fixed amount contract or a purchase order under a framework contract after determining the purchase request and specific quantity, that is, the amount can be basically determined. When filling in the purchase request application form, the total amount inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenditures shall be filled in the amount part, and the pre-approval by the team leader shall be obtained. The approval date of purchase request shall not be later than the start date of contract service. The purchase request amount shall not be substantially different from the amount of the subsequent contract or order.


Framework contracts, fixed amount contracts, and purchase orders

- A contract is required to be entered into for each service prior to the start of the service. Some business divisions have worked with the legal department to develop their own business-specific standard contract texts for use. For those who do not have such a standard contract yet, it is strongly recommended to use the text of the WPP Media standard contract (Appendix I) to negotiate and sign the supplier contract to speed up the contract approval process. The standard contract text is divided into two parts. The "Individual Service Agreement" part is modified according to the specific business and covers the supplier's service content, delivery period, acceptance conditions, payment period, etc. The "General Terms and Conditions" part specifies the general rights and obligations of the contract and also covers intellectual property rights, data security, dispute resolution, etc. If a non-standard contract text of WPP Media is used or the "General Terms and Conditions" in the standard contract text is modified, and the total contract amount exceeds RMB 300,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), the commercial shall determine whether to introduce the legal team to provide professional advice.


- In the case of a client-designated supplier, please add the client-designated supplier terms to the (service) agreement of the relevant client and the (purchase) agreement of its designated supplier, as detailed in Appendix III. If the client's master contract, project contract, spotplan, or designated email does not contain the risk aversion terms of the designated supplier, the CFO approval is required for a single project or schedule with a total amount of more than RMB 2 million yuan.


- For a framework contract or fixed-amount contract with a total contract amount of more than RMB 200,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), the evaluation result of tripartite bidding assessment is required to be provided separately. For specific guidelines on price comparison, please refer to WPP Media Supplier Onboarding Management Procedure 3.3. If the total contract amount cannot be listed in the framework contract, the calculation method of the total amount shall be clarified, and the quotation sheet stamped with the valid official seal of the supplier shall be attached. The quotation needs to be formally sent via email. Arrangements for subsequent project purchases may be made only after the framework contract is sent back to Chorus upon being signed and sealed by both Parties.


- Only if WPP Media has signed the framework contract, and the framework contract has designated the unit price corresponding to the continuous provision of services within a certain period, the rental fee, management fee, utilities, cleaning fee, warehousing charge, photocopying fee, and translation items (see Appendix IV for details), the signed and sealed purchase order may not be used, and the actual settlement may be applied according to the actual amount incurred (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), and the charge calculation sheet and invoice sealed by the supplier shall be directly used as the application basis. In other cases, a purchase order is required to be signed by the project (Appendix II WPP Media Non-Media Service Purchase Framework Contract - Purchase Order). The purchase order shall be deemed as a contract, and the approval and sealing process shall be consistent with the requirements for review and approval of fixed-amount contracts. The purchase order is required to be signed and sealed by both Parties to take effect and be sent back to Chorus. The purchase application for approval is required to be made before arranging the purchase order.


- After the purchase request application is approved, the applicant may arrange the application for the contract and purchase order. The applicant and reviewer of the contract and purchase order are required to review the completeness and reasonableness of the contract and its Appendixes according to the actual business situation and the requirements of this Procedure. The contract and purchase order shall come into force once being signed and sealed.


- The contract and purchase order are required to be reviewed and approved by the team leader, the commercial team, the finance team, the CCO, and the financial director. The contract signatory shall be the team leader or above. If the total contract amount is more than RMB 100,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), it shall be additionally subject to the review and approval of the agency's CEO or functional department head.


- The review and approval by the Procurement and CFO shall be added in the following cases.

- Framework contract

- The total contract amount is RMB 300,000 yuan or more (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses)

- Contracts with a service period of more than one year

- Other scenarios where a tripartite bidding assessment cannot be provided:

- Only Available Source since as Appointed Service Standards or Brand Limits;

- Fewer than three suppliers are able to participate in the bidding and provide valid quotations.


- Additional approval requirements for special businesses are as follows:

- The purchase of fixed assets (including office decoration) uniformly arranged by WPP Media administration shall be subject to the prior budget approval of WPP Media CFO, CEO, and Asia-Pacific CFO, and the budget above USD 250K shall be approved by WPP.

- All IT department supplier contracts are required to be signed by a Technology Business Partner or above. The IT equipment fee is required to be approved by the Procurement and WPP Media CIO.


- WPP’s standard payment terms are 60 days from the date of invoice. If the supplier’s payment terms are less than 60 days in the contract application, special approval is required.


Settlement statement

- When using a framework contract to order services on an actual settlement basis, the settlement statement may be applied after the supplier provides the services and issues a bill, sales order, or payment notice to determine the amount of the services. In the case of irregular services, it is also required to submit an application for business development to the team leader in advance, to order services from the supplier after obtaining consent, and to upload approval and communication emails when applying for a settlement statement.


- The settlement statement is required to be reviewed and approved by the team leader, commercial team, and CCO. When the amount exceeds RMB 100,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), the review and approval of the agency's CEO or functional department head shall be added; If the amount exceeds RMB 300,000 yuan (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses), the review and approval of the CFO shall be added.


Contract seal

- After the contract is approved, Chorus will assign a unique contract number and watermark the contract. Then, the applicant may download and print the watermarked contract from Chorus, submit it to the finance department, and fill out the seal registration form, in which case the finance department will search in the system for the contract with the corresponding contract number, check the approval status again, and arrange for sealing.


- It is required that the contract signatory sign by hand on the place of signature and sealing of the signed original contract, and all contracts are required to be stamped with a cross-page seal. Upon completion of sealing, the seal registration form shall be archived by the finance department.

- The service can be started and subsequent arrangements can be made only after the contract and purchase order signed and sealed by both Parties are uploaded to Chorus as required.


Contract change

- The applicant may initiate the contract change process in Chorus. In case of changes to the contract amount, the applicant is required to present the latest total contract amount inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses. The requirements for approval and sealing process of the contract change process shall be consistent with the requirements for review and approval of non-framework contracts.


**Receiving process description**

- After receiving the goods or services, the applicant is required to retain the supporting documents for the provision by the supplier of the goods or services and attach the evidence that the goods or services have been provided to Chorus, in which case the payment application may proceed only after obtaining approval from the team leader.


- Upon completing the provision of the supplier's service, regardless of whether the payment is due or not, the corresponding responsible person is required to timely inspect and accept the service quantity and quality, upload the acceptance data, and complete the acceptance steps in the system. For advance payment, the applicant is required to supplement the uploading of the receipt certificate in time after the goods or services are provided.


**Payment application process**

- When applying for payment, for the payment after the due date, the cumulative payment amount shall not exceed the total contract amount. The payment may be arranged upon review without error after finance team, and financial director compare the contract and collection information archived in the system. If the Payment Due Date is earlier than the “Payment Term Due Date”, additional approval from the Commercial team and CCO is required.


- When a client service supplier applies for payment, the applicant is required to confirm the Cash-In from the client before applying for payment and provide the PCI number and the email from SSC notifying the Cash-In. The finance department shall re-check the Cash-In from the client according to the PCI number. When the amount of a single payment exceeds the filled PCI collection amount, it shall be a Payment Without Client Cash-In, which is additionally subject to approval by the Head of FOC.


- When the cumulative payment amount of a single contract exceeds the cumulative amount of receipt for goods, the system will determine it as an advance payment and remind the approver in red letters.

- When the amount of a single payment exceeds RMB 1 million yuan, the financial director shall manually submit it to the Head of FOC for approval, except for expenses of the following nature:

- a.	Monthly fixed fees (including expenses related to rent, wages, social security and related expenses, insurance premiums, taxes, and expenses related to the account settlement of affiliates)

- b.	FCM travel expenses, budgeted decoration purchase expenses, computer-related consumables expenses

- c.	Supplier expenses to be borne by the client


- Electronic invoices (or invoices in electronic form) are required to be submitted for payment application, or to be supplemented after payment. The applicant is required to complete the invoices in a timely manner, and the SSC may close the payment application only after the invoices are supplemented completely.


- After the payment is completed, the payment application form will be archived by the SSC together with the payment voucher.


**One-off payment process**

- Non-media payment applications with a total amount (the total amount is inclusive of all applicable taxes, surcharges, and necessary expenses) less than RMB 30,000 yuan may directly go through the one-off payment process. The one-off payment process mainly deals with occasional and one-off payment demands with small amounts, such as the payment of bidding deposits, award fees, or other special expenses. The beneficiary in a similar business is often not consistent with the nature of the service product that the supplier sells. The applicant is required to fully and accurately describe the purpose of the demand to obtain timely approval.


- The supplier adopted for one-off payment cannot be an advisor, non-WPP Group creative agency, client-designated supplier, or supplier involved in the provision of data, technical, or HR related services.


- The one-off payment demand shall be subject to approval by the team leader, commercial team, finance team, CCO, and financial director. When the payment demand of the bidding deposit or award fee exceeds the amount limit, the system will submit it to the Head of FOC for approval. When the payment demand of other nature exceeds the amount limit, consideration shall be first taken whether to apply for onboarding of the supplier. If consideration is still taken for applying for a one-off payment, the system will submit the application to the CFO for approval.


- The finance department and the Risk & Control department shall regularly organize the review of the one-off payment business. For payers with repeated large amounts, it is recommended to go through the supplier onboarding process. After that, such supplier will not be able to continue to obtain the approval for one-off payments.


- For bank transfer payment demand, a one-off payment supplier code is required to be established for a one-off payment, and the applicant is still required to provide the following materials:

- a. Copy of business license affixed with official seal;

- b. Bank account information affixed with official seal;

- c. Supporting documents for the supplier's acknowledgment of the WPP Code of Business Conduct, not required for the WPP-approved suppliers;

- d. Materials attached with other information describing payment demands, such as sales order, and payment notice.


- It should be noted that the one-off payment supplier code will be accepted only for one-off payments, after which the supplier code will become invalid. If enabled again, it is required to be re-created and approved.


The coding rules for one-off payment supplier codes established by SSC for one-off payment demands are the same as those for general suppliers. When the application is re-enabled after a one-off payment supplier demand is closed, the normal supplier onboarding application steps may be followed as option to become a general supplier upon approval. Approved onboarding suppliers may also be selected for one-off payment applications, and no new one-off payment supplier code will be assigned.


**Precautions**

- The Company will update this system and relevant process on the internal website of WPP Media China. It is the responsibility of each employee to visit the website frequently to understand and abide by the latest system and procedure.

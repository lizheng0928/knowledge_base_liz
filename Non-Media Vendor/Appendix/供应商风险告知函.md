**情况****一****：**** ****指定供应商资质缺陷**

**供应****商风险****告知函**


尊敬的【客户名称】媒介采买总负责人：

Dear CMO or equivalent:

我司的供应商管理团队已经对指定代理商【供应商名称】展开了常规审核流程，并且注意到，与其他适合的供应商相比，该供应商缺乏以下相关资质证明：

Our vendor management team has gone through a routine process of checking and noticed that the appointed vendor, compared to other qualified vendors, is lacking of below certification(s):

1.       XXXXXX 

2.       XXXXXX


以上相关资质证明的缺乏可能会导致在项目执行及履约保证上可能存在较高的潜在风险，因此，我们强烈建议贵司选择其他适合的供应商如【供应商名称】

Lacking of above certification(s) will lead to a high potential risk in campaign delivery and performance guarantee. Therefore we highly recommend you to use other qualified vendors suggested as 【vendor name】,


请以回复本邮件确认的方式明确贵司的选择：

A.       选择【vendor name】

B.        贵司仍然坚持通过【vendor name】投放，且贵司已知晓相关风险。

Please revert to us with your choice below by replying this email that,

a. Choose 【vendor name】; or

b. You still would like to buy through 【vendor name】 and have acknowledged the risk accordingly.


 若贵司选择B方案的，这意味着贵司确认并同意，因【vendor name】资质缺乏所导致的所有主张、责任、损失、费用等将由贵司自行承担，同时贵司应保证使群邑免于遭受因由此产生的全部损害，并赔偿群邑因此遭受的全部损失。

If you choose selection b., it means that you agree and acknowledge that all claims, liabilities, losses, expenses may be caused by the 【vendor name】’s unqualification will be your sole discretion and responsibility, in addition you shall hold GroupM harmless and indemnify GroupM with respect thereto.

 此致

# WPP CBC vendor exemption application

**Type:** Outlook Email Template (.oft)
**Message Class:** IPM.Note
**Last Modified By:** ethelind.zhang@groupm.com

## Email Body

This form is used to apply for exemption approval of vendor s sign-off on WPP CBC. Please complete all questions and send with attachments to approver as SOP indicated.

### Form Fields:

- **Chinese name of the vendor**
- **English name of the vendor**
- **Type of the vendor**
  - Media broker
  - Media owner
  - Non-media vendor
- **Planned service to be provided by vendor**
- **Business rational of choosing the vendor**
- **Who bears the cost?**
  - Client
  - GroupM
- **Vendor s reason of not signing WPP CBC**
- **Does vendor have its own Code of Business Code?**
  - Yes (Please attach to the email)
  - No
- **Please attach other supporting and remark**
- **Department head/Team leader/Agency CEO/Functional department Head s approval**
  - If others, please specify:
- **Please prepare at least 3 business days to go through WPP global approval process.**

**Conflicts of Interest Policy**** **


**What is a conflict of interest?**** **

WPP considers a conflict of interest to have arisen if an employee allows their actual, perceived or potential personal, financial or non-financial interests to affect their objectivity when working for WPP. This can lead to biased decisions, overlooked obligations and potential legal and financial consequences for both WPP and employees. 

WPP employees must avoid situations in which there is, or there may be perceived to be, a conflict between (a) the best interests of WPP or the agency for which they work and (b) their own interests. 



**What is a related-party transaction?**** **

A related-party transaction is a deal or arrangement being negotiated or agreed between WPP or a WPP agency and a connected individual or business. A connected individual or business can include any individuals, their close family members or businesses (a) with control, joint control or significant influence over WPP or the WPP agency or (b) in a ‘key management personnel’ role at WPP or in a WPP agency, this means anyone with the authority and responsibility for planning, directing and controlling business activities. 

**A related-party transaction has the potential to create a conflict of interest where the two related or connected parties are in a position to influence the decision-making including the benefit to be provided to each of the parties and the terms of that. **For example, a contract between a major shareholder of a company and the company itself, agreeing that the shareholder’s company will renovate the company’s head offices, would be a related-party transaction. 



**What is an outside interest?**** **

WPP considers any non-WPP related appointment, engagement or investment to be an outside interest. 

Any outside appointments or engagements, whether or not there is or could be a conflict of interest, will need to be disclosed and approved in line with paragraph 3.17 of WPP’s People Policies. Any conflict of interest that could arise as a result of an outside appointment, engagement or investment will also need to be disclosed and approved in line with this Conflicts of Interest Policy. 



**Mandatory disclosure and approval process - all conflicts must be disclosed and, for any workstream of transaction with a conflict to go ahead, approved**** **

Companies often seek to secure business deals with parties with whom they are familiar or have a common interest. Although these types of transactions are legal, they potentially could create a conflict of interest, or lead to another situation that is illegal, and so need to be reviewed and managed appropriately. 

WPP therefore requires that: 

all conflicts of interest including any related-party transactions are disclosed (through line managers if more practicable) to Senior Management – the obligation to disclose is on the WPP employee with the conflict of interest and others who have knowledge of any conflict and do not see it being managed or mitigated in accordance with this policy are encouraged to speak up; and 

for any workstream or transaction with a conflict of interest to go ahead, approval must be given by the relevant Senior Management. 

- Approval should not be given without appropriate management and mitigation measures in place where any conflict exists,

- Consideration should be given to whether it is commercially sensible for the workstream or transaction to continue, whether the employee with the conflict can remain involved (for example, they may need to step aside from negotiating the terms of any deal or responsibility for monies passing to or from a related party)

- Both the conflict and any steps being taken to manage it should be documented in writing on the employee’s HR records

If Senior Management has queries over how to manage and mitigate a conflict or whether to give approval, please contact

All Senior Management at WPP agencies and all WPP HQ employees must disclose any conflicts of interest (including any transactions or workstreams with related parties) on an annual basis through the annual certification process and as required or necessary in the course of the year. 



**Additional examples:**** **

A conflict of interest may arise, and disclosure is therefore required, when a WPP employee: 

Allows their personal values, beliefs or political views to influence their performance or decision-making at WPP 

Hires or manages someone they know personally or hires someone in exchange for a personal or business favour 

Accepts or performs a public official role, or has a family member or a close personal contact who is a public official and could take decisions impacting WPP 

Has, or close family members have, a personal interest or investment in a competitor or another business that is or could be a client, vendor, supplier or acquisition target of WPP 

Encourages clients to do business with a company in which they or their family have a personal or financial interest 

Owns, or their family owns, property leased by WPP 



**Definitions:**** **

***Close family members ****of a person are those family members who may be expected to influence, or be influenced by, that person in their dealings with a company and include: (a) children and spouse or domestic partner; (b) children of the spouse or domestic partner; and (c) dependants (of either the person or their spouse or domestic partner). *

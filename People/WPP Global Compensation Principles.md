


**WPP Global Compensation Principles **


**SECTION 1 - INTRODUCTION & GENERAL PRINCIPLES **


# Scope

This policy outlines the global compensation principles for WPP plc and its subsidiaries.   


This policy is a summary framework and does not replace applicable plan rules, compensation policies, collective bargaining arrangements or local legal requirements, and does not create any contractual entitlement to pay, pay progression, a pay increase, bonus, allowance or benefit.  


The implementation of compensation principles will be guided by local market practices and regulations in each country where WPP operates. 


# Principles

WPP employs a Total Reward approach to compensation, designed to foster a highperformance, aligned culture and motivate employees. Our comprehensive reward and recognition programs are strategically crafted to attract, engage, and retain top talent, thereby ensuring exceptional service to our clients. Decisions relating to pay and other elements of total reward are made based on fair, transparent, and consistent pay practices that reward employee contributions and support the achievement of business objectives. 


# SECTION 2 - PAY STRUCTURE


Total Reward is delivered through three key elements: Fixed Pay, Variable Pay, and Benefits.  Eligibility for specific pay elements depends on role, seniority and local market practices.   



# Fixed pay

Fixed pay is primarily comprised of base salary and allowances. 

Base salary – Base salary is the guaranteed, regular pay an employee earns, excluding any other variable or non-cash elements. 

Allowances – May include cash in lieu of pension/retirement fund, and other cash allowances, in accordance with local market or legislative practice. 


# Variable pay

Where employees are eligible for variable compensation, awards are determined under the applicable plan and the company governance framework. Relevant objective, gender-neutral criteria may include: 

 	 Individual performance and contribution, where relevant under the applicable plan. 

Risk, compliance and control considerations. 

Role, level and eligibility under the applicable plan. 

Applicable legal, regulatory or local requirements. 


All variable pay is discretionary, and dependent on business performance, individual performance, and overall affordability.  


While these Global Compensation Principles provide the overarching framework for 

WPP's total reward strategy, specific, detailed incentive plans (such as the ShortTerm Incentive Plan or Long-Term Incentive Plans) are governed by their respective plan rules and policies. In the event of any conflict or ambiguity between these Global Compensation Principles and the specific rules of any such incentive plan, the specific rules of the relevant incentive plan shall take precedence for matters relating to that plan. 


# Benefits

At WPP we offer a range of additional benefits that are designed to support the physical, mental and financial wellbeing of our employees.  These benefits can range from health to retirement, combined with various family and career related support. WPP aims to offer benefits which are competitive in support of our people and talent. The level of benefits provided will vary in each country where we operate, and in line with local market practice and legal requirements.  


**SECTION 3 - ****HOW WE SET PAY AND HOW ****PAY**** PROGRESSES** 


# Principles

We strive to deliver a transparent approach to our pay principles and pay progression. Pay progression may occur through salary review, promotion, role change, change in role scope or responsibility, movement within an applicable salary range or pay scale. Our approach to pay and how pay progresses is made by reference to the following objective, gender-neutral criteria, which may include: 

**Market Competitiveness:** Reviewing salaries to ensure they remain competitive within the relevant market. 

**Career Architecture & Level:** Utilising a consistent job evaluation system, ensuring internal equity and reflecting the relative value of different roles to the organisation. 

**Skills and Development:** Reviewing the role performed, the relevant skills, qualifications, experience and professional development. 

**Performance:** Assessing sustained individual performance and contribution. 

**Fairness:** Using objective criteria, free from discrimination, and applied consistently. 

**Budget & Affordability:** Understanding the context of overall affordability and the salary review budget available in each business, unit or country. 

**Work location:** Applying where relevant to the local pay framework or local legal requirements. Working within applicable local law, regulation, collective bargaining agreements, works council agreements or similar local arrangements.

WPP is committed to equal pay for equal work. Pay decisions are objective and fair to all our employees, irrespective of gender, race, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. We regularly review our pay practices to ensure compliance with local pay legislation and monitor their effectiveness to ensure fairness and equality. 


# Salary Review Cycles

Salary reviews are scheduled annually as part of the WPP review cycle, although timing may vary depending on business needs or other operational considerations. A review does not automatically result in a salary increase. Merit increases and inplace promotions are generally considered through the regular review cycle. If an individual is promoted to a vacant role during the year through the “promote to vacancy” process, their salary will be reviewed in line with the nature and responsibilities of the new role. Other off-cycle reviews are limited and will usually only take place in exceptional circumstances. 


# WPP Salary Ranges

As the roles in our business are broad, there can be a great variation in skills, knowledge and ability amongst employees performing those roles. We take several factors into account when setting our salary ranges, such as industry standards, market forces and other economic factors. WPP salary ranges, where they exist, apply to all agencies within a country. The ranges are regularly reviewed.

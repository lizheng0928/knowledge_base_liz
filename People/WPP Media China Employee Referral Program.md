Objectives
The policy describes the standard of WPP Media China Employee Referral Program (ERP) to better
encourage and reward the employees for referring new talent to our company.
目的本政策概述了WPP Media中国员工内推项目（Employee Referral Program，简称 ERP）的标准流
程，以更好地鼓励、奖励为公司推荐人才的员工。
WPP Media China Talent Referral Award Program
The following rewards will be granted to employees whose referral led to a successful full-time hire and
a sequent employment with WPP Media China of no less than 6 months.
WPP Media中国人才推荐奖励计划以下奖金将发放给内推成功的全职员工，其推荐的全职员工必须在
WPP Media中国连续工作不少于 6 个月。
Level/职级 Reward (Taxable)/奖金 (税前)
Director and Director level above ¥ 12,000.00
Manager/Associate Director ¥ 8,000.00
Senior Executive/Supervisor ¥ 5,000.00
Employee Eligibility
All referral cases must be submitted through below channel:
 WeChat: WPP Media HR WeChat account “WPP Media Careers” – Employee Refer
All full-time employees of agencies/functions under WPP Media China are eligible for the ERP reward,
except:
 WPP Media China ExCo members
 HR team members
 People managers on the reporting line chain of the vacancy (Example: to hire one Sr.
Executive, the ‘line managers’ on the role’s reporting line chain, including line manager
(Manager or AD), line manager’s line manager (Dir. or BD), and line manager’s line
manager’s line manager (GM or MD), won’t be eligible)

 WPP Media HR招聘微信公众号“WPP Media Careers “ - ”员工内推“
除了以下员工，WPP Media中国所有正式员工均有资格参与内推项目：
 WPP Media中国 ExCo 成员
 WPP Media中国 HR 团队

Referral Validity
The reward program is not applicable in any of the scenarios below that the referred candidate:
 Has been screened/interviewed (including phone interview) by WPP Media in the past 6
months for any open or potential requisition.
 Is an employee under WPP Group.
 Is an ex-WPP Media employee who left within 12 months.
 Is converted from an intern or temporary/freelance/contractor position.
In case of dispute, the final judgment of an eligible referral is to be made by the HR team.
推荐有效性奖励计划不适用于以下情况：
 在过去 6 个月内曾以任何公开或潜在形式的申请WPP Media工作机会且通过WPP Media的
简历筛选/面试
（包括电话面试）
 被推荐人为 WPP 集团的员工
 12 个月内离职的前WPP Media员工
 推荐人为实习生、临时工/自由职业/第三方岗位的员工
如有争议，由人力资源团队最保留最终解释权。
Reward Payout
The payment will be made after the referred employee completes 6 months of service at WPP Media
China, and the payment will be taxable and through payroll.
奖金发放奖金将在被推荐员工在WPP Media中国服务满 6 个月后，通过工资的形式发
放且该奖金需纳税。

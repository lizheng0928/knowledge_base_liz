# WPP MEDIA 中国 - 媒体管理员工轮岗政策

**介绍：**

为进一步加强 MEDIA MANAGEMENT 内部的投资治理和控制框架，将针对参与供应商谈判和互动流程的关键岗位推出 MEDIA MANAGEMENT 员工轮岗计划，生效日期为 **2025****年****1****月****1****日**。

**适用员工：**

适用员工的标准和名单将由 CIO、CPO、CIGO 和 CFO 每年审阅一次（见“轮岗的一般流程”第2项）。当前标准如下。

符合下列全部标准的员工需要进行岗位轮换，并在新岗位任职一段明确期限。标准包括：

- Negotiation 团队内 Business Director 及以上级别员工，以及根据酌情判断选定的部分 Negotiation Director。

- 在同一岗位*任职超过3年（即36个月）。

- *同一岗位是指负责同一供应商/中介组合的谈判岗位。

**轮岗类型：**

适用员工将在明确期限内调至其他岗位。轮岗可采取不同形式。

默认原则是适用员工应完成标准轮岗。轻轮岗仅在因合理的实际原因导致标准轮岗暂时无法实施时，作为替代安排适用，并须经过必要的审阅和批准。

**标准轮岗**（优先适用）：

- 在同一岗位（原岗位）服务满3年后，员工必须调至另一岗位（目标岗位），轮岗期限至少为1年。

- 轮岗期结束后，员工可根据业务需要返回原岗位、留任目标岗位，或调至另一新岗位（见下文“轮岗计划的准备和审阅”部分）。

**轻轮岗**（基于理由说明和批准的替代安排）：

- 当标准轮岗因实际原因暂时无法实施时，例如存在重大业务连续性顾虑，或尚未确定/培训长期继任者，适用员工可获批准进行为期2至4周的轻轮岗。轻轮岗必须在本应开始标准轮岗的日历年度结束前完成。

- 轻轮岗审批申请必须包含充分说明的理由，并取得员工直属经理的书面支持。

- 在任意5年期间内，轻轮岗最多只能申请2年；完成两次轻轮岗后，必须在12个月内启动标准轮岗。

- 例如，轻轮岗安排可通过以下方式实现：

- 两名团队负责人互换岗位2至4周。临时团队负责人将由一名资深员工或副手支持，以确保业务连续性。

- 一名 Negotiation 团队负责人可被任命为 CIO 或另一名 Negotiation 负责人副手，期限为2至4周；期间可任命一名资深员工或副手临时履行原负责人职责。

**轮岗计划的准备和审阅：**

- 对于每位适用员工，应在轮岗到期日前充分提前准备轮岗计划（建议提前12个月），并在提交审批前与所有相关方达成一致。

- 轮岗计划应包括轮岗类型（标准或轻轮岗）、目标岗位、时间安排、继任者、交接培训方案、流程更新以及轮岗期后的安排等内容（见附录1轮岗计划模板）。

- 轮岗计划必须由适用员工及原岗位和目标岗位的直属经理共同参与，并须经 CIO、People team 和 CIGO 批准。

- 适用员工的直属经理负责设计轮岗计划，并应推动各相关方讨论，包括征询适用员工意见。

- 在年度/半年度绩效评估时，或根据员工临时提出的请求，员工或直属经理可启动对轮岗计划细节的审阅或调整。

**轮岗的一般流程：**

- 直属经理和员工必须在年度/半年度绩效评估期间，提前讨论并评估未来轮岗计划和选项。

- 每年年底前，CIO、CPO 和 CIGO 将共同审阅并确定已属于适用范围或将在未来12个月内进入适用范围的员工名单（即“适用员工名单”）。审阅会议也可在可能情况下初步确定轮岗形式、时间安排和目标岗位建议。

- 每年1月，People team 向相关员工和直属经理发送通知，提醒其准备轮岗计划。

- 适用员工的直属经理与所有相关方（即适用员工、潜在目标岗位直属经理、CIO、People team 等）开展讨论，以确定轮岗计划，包括继任者识别、时间安排（开始-结束）、目标岗位、交接计划、新岗位绩效目标以及轮岗期后的安排。

- 讨论后，员工完成轮岗计划，并取得相关方签批（即适用员工、原岗位和目标岗位直属经理以及 People team）。People team 归档记录并准备行政流程（见附录1轮岗计划样本）。

- 员工和直属经理启动交接。

- People team 处理内部调动行政流程。

- 轮岗开始。

- 定期开展检查沟通，审阅绩效并在需要时更新轮岗计划。

- 轮岗结束。根据轮岗计划执行轮岗后的安排（例如返回原岗位、留任目标岗位或转至另一岗位）。

**监控：**

- 在半年度/年度末，HR MEDIA MANAGEMENT People Partner 将向 CIO、CPO、CIGO、R&C 提供一份适用员工名单，列明已在同一岗位完成36个月服务但尚未实施轮岗的员工。该名单必须包含员工/直属经理的说明，以及轻轮岗或标准轮岗的建议，或延迟轮岗例外审批申请。

- CIO 应负责确保所有适用员工的轮岗计划里程碑始终按计划推进。

**例外延迟审批：**

- 在例外情况下，适用员工或其直属经理可基于理由说明，以书面形式提出延迟轮岗申请。

- 标准轮岗最多可申请延迟12个月，轻轮岗最多可申请延迟3个月。

- 申请必须提交高级管理层（CIO、CPO、CIGO），并须获得一致批准。

**业务连续性保障：**

- 适用员工负责提出其继任者和继任计划，并由直属经理审阅和批准。

- 适用员工负责及时规划和实施培训及交接，并更新其流程和其他必要工作文件。建议员工至少在标准轮岗开始前3个月准备交接。

- 直属经理负责通过适用员工实施的适当交接和培训流程，确保业务连续性。

# WPP MEDIA CHINA - MEDIA MANAGEMENT EMPLOYEES ROTATION POLICY


**Introduction:  **


In order to further strengthen the Investment governance and control framework within MEDIA MANAGEMENT, a MEDIA MANAGEMENT Employee Rotation program will be rolled out to critical roles involved in the vendor negotiation and interaction process, with an effective date of **1****st**** Jan 2025**.


**In-****S****cope ****Employees****: **


The criteria and the list of in-scope employees will be reviewed annually by CIO, CPO, CIGO, and CFO. (See section General Workflow of Rotation - #2). The current criteria are described below.

Employees meeting all the criteria set out below are required to rotate role and spend a defined period of time in such new role. The criteria are: 

- Business Director and above within Negotiation teams, plus certain Negotiation Directors selected at discretion.

- Has been in the same role* for more than 3 years (i.e. 36 months).

- *Same role refers to a negotiation role covering the same vendor/intermediary portfolio.


**Types of ****Rotation:**


The in-scope employees will move to another role for a defined period. 


There are different forms of rotation.  


The default role is that in-scope employees should complete a standard rotation. Light rotation is an alternative arrangement only where standard rotation cannot be implemented temporarily due to justified practical reasons, and is subject to the required review and approval.


**Standard rotation **(preferred case): 

- After 3 years’ service in the same role (original role), the employee must move to another role (target role) for a rotation period of at least 1 year.

- At the end of the rotation period, the employee may return to the original role, stay in the target role, or move to another new role, subject to the needs of the business (see the section “Preparation and Review of Rotation Plan” below).


**Light rotation **(alternative upon justification & approval):  

- When standard rotation cannot be implemented temporarily for practical reasons, e.g. significant business continuity concerns or a long-term successor not yet identified/trained, the in-scope employee may be approved to take a light rotation for a period of 2-4 weeks. Light rotations must be completed by the end of the calendar year in which a standard rotation should have commenced.

- Requests for approval of a light rotation must include a fully reasoned justification with the written support of the employee’s line manager.

- Light rotation may only be requested for 2 years in any 5-year period, and once 2 Light rotations have been completed, a standard rotation must commence within 12 months.

- By way of example, light rotation arrangements may be achieved as follows:

- Two team heads swap roles for a period of 2-4 weeks. The temporary team head will be supported by a senior employee or deputy to ensure business continuity.

- A Negotiation team head may be appointed to act as deputy to the CIO or another Negotiation head for 2-4 weeks, during which a senior employee or deputy may be appointed to perform the original head role temporarily.


**Preparation ****and Review ****of Rotation Plan:  **


- For each in-scope employee, a rotation plan shall be prepared sufficiently in advance of the due rotation date (ideally 12 months in advance) and agreed with all stakeholders before being submitted for approval.

- The rotation plan shall include details such as: type of rotation (standard or light), target role, timeframe, successor, handover training proposal, procedure updates, and arrangements after the rotation period. (See template rotation plan in Appendix 1.)

- The rotation plan must involve the in-scope employee and the line managers of both the original and target roles, and must be approved by the CIO, the People team, and CIGO.

- The line manager of any in-scope employee is accountable for designing the rotation plan and shall facilitate discussions across stakeholders, including consulting the in-scope employee.

- At the annual/semi-annual performance review, or upon an ad-hoc employee request, the employee or line manager may initiate a review or adjustment of the rotation plan details.


**General ****Workflow ****of ****R****otation:  **


- The line manager and employee must discuss and assess the future rotation plan and options in advance during the annual/semi-annual performance review.

- By the end of each year, CIO, CPO, and CIGO will jointly review and determine the list of employees who are already in scope or will become in scope within the next 12 months (i.e. the “in-scope employees list”). The review meeting may also establish a preliminary proposal for the rotation format, timeframe, and target role where possible.

- Each January, the People team sends a notification to the relevant employees and line managers to remind them to prepare a rotation plan.

- The line manager of the in-scope employee sets up discussions with all stakeholders (i.e. the in-scope employee, potential target role’s line manager, CIO, the People team, etc.) to define the rotation plan, including successor identification, timeframe (start-end), target role, handover planning, performance objectives for the new role, and arrangements after the rotation period.

- After the discussion, the employee completes the rotation plan and obtains sign-off from the relevant stakeholders (i.e. the in-scope employee, line managers of the original and target roles, and the People team). The People team files the record and prepares for the administrative process. (See rotation plan sample in Appendix 1.)

- The employee and line manager kick off the handover.

- The People team processes the internal transfer admin process.

- Rotation starts.

- Regular check-ins are held to review performance and update the rotation plan if needed.

- Rotation finishes. The post-rotation arrangement is executed according to the rotation plan (e.g. return to the original role, stay in the target role, or move to another role).


**Monitoring****: **

- At half-year/year-end, HR MEDIA MANAGEMENT People Partner will provide CIO, CPO, CIGO, R&C with a list of in-scope employees who have not yet implemented a rotation following completion of 36 months’ service in the same role. The list must include an explanation from the employees/line managers and a proposal for either a light or standard rotation, or a request for exceptional approval for postponement.

- The CIO shall be responsible for ensuring that milestones are on track under the rotation plan for all in-scope employees at all times.


**Approval ****for**** Exceptional ****Postponement****:  **

- In exceptional cases, in-scope employees or the line manager can make a request in writing to postpone a rotation, based on justification.

- Postponements may be requested for up to 12 months for standard rotation and up to 3 months for light rotation.

- Requests must be submitted to Senior management (CIO, CPO, CIGO) and require unanimous approval.


**Business Continuity Assurance: **

- The in-scope employee is responsible for proposing their successor and succession plan, which shall be reviewed and approved by line manager.

- The in-scope employee is responsible for planning and implementing the training and handover in a timely manner and updating their procedures and other necessary working documents. It is recommended that employees prepare the handover at least 3 months before standard rotation is due to commence.

- The line manager is accountable for ensuring business continuity through the proper handover and training process implemented by the in-scope employee.
